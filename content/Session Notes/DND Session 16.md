# DND Session 16 — The World Untrue

---

## TL;DR

The party walks back from Canary Park into a town full of sleeping bodies and a fat finch that refuses to leave Mira's head. They get kidnapped by gas-masked Amasia loyalists, wake up in a very nice limousine wagon, and learn the organization behind the sleeping plague. The ride ends at a train bound for the capital. Inquizo throws a mysterious blue clock off a bridge into the ocean and the sky turns blood-red, the city ahead starts melting, hell creatures board the train, and a stained glass dragon attacks them with shards — which the party recognizes in real-time as matching the prophecy line "when hammer breaks on dragon scale." The train becomes a sky train and crashes into a bathhouse where Gbot'n is eating a wet croissant. The party shops at a floating capital market, buys an alarming number of fire ant jars, and arrives at a platform on the city's edge where a white-haired little girl is being protected from a monster-boy by a false knight. Session ends on the cliffhanger.

## Full Summary

### Scene 1 — The Town That Fell Asleep (00:00–26:00)

Walking back from Canary Park toward the winged woman's safehouse, the party finds everyone in Eustace lying on the ground — sleeping sickness has reached them. One fat, rotund finch sits on a pole gazing at Mira. Wisdom saves called: George and Halcyra fail and become drowsy (half movement); Inquizo rolls a natural 20 and is unaffected; Mira rolls a 5 and fails hard.

The fat finch — Cuckoo — flies directly onto Mira's head and nests there. Constance confirms the sickness: *"It got here too. It's been sweeping the place recently."* Mira attempts to remove Cuckoo via acrobatics and rolls a 1 — she knocks herself unconscious. The bird hat stays. The party decides to move toward the safehouse.

### Scene 2 — The Elephant Creatures (26:00–48:00)

Small, fluffy elephant-like creatures with long anteater tongues walk through the air above the sleeping people, inserting their tongues into the ears of the unconscious. They are incorporeal — hands pass through them. One is actively draining George; he sleepily swings his hammer but it passes through harmlessly. Halcyra, in her drowsy state, perceives the creatures as warmth and sunlight and can trace dusty trails from them back to a single origin point — a pillar.

Behind the pillar: an **albino man**. Pale skin, crimson eyes, platinum blonde hair, voluptuous beard with a curly mustache, black button-up shirt, green vest with a **dreamcatcher insignia**, silver watch, chains hanging from blue denim pockets, loafers. Beer belly. Impressive pectorals. He claims to be a healer (lying). Claims to be a bounty hunter waiting for his mark (also lying — DM confirms). George, half-asleep, swings his hammer at the man's wrist, connects, and smashes his wrist and hand into the ground. The man hyperventilates.

**Cascading sleep:** Mira is already unconscious (bird hat acrobatics nat 1). George falls fully asleep. Halcyra falls fully asleep. Inquizo flies upward to escape the sleep gas. George manages one last act of brilliance — he plays his drum using **Enthralling Brawl**, causing 21 of the 24 cloaked figures watching from the perimeter to stop and enjoy the show. Then falls asleep. Inquizo is the only one standing.

### Scene 3 — Kidnapping (48:00–1:07:00)

Five of the cloaked figures (all wearing gas masks) emerge from behind the group. A caravan of wagons approaches. The entire sleeping party is loaded up and carried — George requires four people due to size. Constance is also taken. Inquizo goes invisible and follows from the air.

**Halcyra wakes while being carried.** A tall, buff woman is transporting her. Halcyra uses **Enthralling Strike** on the woman — vines tangle up the captor and she collapses paralyzed. The woman says: *"I'm trying to get you out of here."*

**Halcyra spots Mira being carried** and shoots her captor (4–6 damage) — the captor drops Mira. Mira wakes, assesses the situation, and immediately pretends to be asleep (successful Deception roll). Halcyra is loaded into the wagon anyway. Mira is loaded in. George is still asleep and carried by four people.

### Scene 4 — The Limousine Wagon (1:07:00–1:50:00)

Inside: green cushions with dreamcatcher insignia throughout. A woman with short hair, tank top, cargo pants, combat boots, and a **barbed-wire spear** is inside cutting fruit and making drinks. Constance and Liz are inside, terrified.

Meanwhile outside: **Inquizo steals a gas mask from the back of the caravan** and accidentally also steals a hamster.

**Mira casts Hold Person** on the woman about to cuff her — the woman fails the save (rolls 2) and goes paralyzed. **Halcyra cuffs the paralyzed woman**, takes 3 extra pairs of handcuffs (no key found — coin flip was tails). **George is woken up by saxophone music**, sits up, and immediately begins eating the sausages and eggs that are apparently present.

The woman — later revealed to be **Lila Monger** — eventually admits she works for the organization after bullshitting for a while. Her philosophy on deception, delivered with complete sincerity: *"If you trust someone who's honest, they have every opportunity to betray you. But if you listen to a liar, you already know that they might."*

**Constance reveals:** Her person was **Eskander**, who is the son of **Gore Harlow** — the organization's ringleader. Eskander betrayed his family for Constance and is now "gone." *"They hurt him. He fought them to save me. Now he's gone. He was their family. He betrayed them for me."*

**Lila reveals:** The organization is Amasia loyalists — classified as "terrorist factions" by the Erloonian nation. They escaped from Eroport/Aerofort after the region was isolated. The **COG** (Commonwealth Oversight Guild) is the militia/mercenary structure that governs Lozenland's branch of local law. The caravan is headed to a train that connects to the capital.

Inside the train car: five statues with **Pmurt's face** plastered over the original god-faces. **Halcyra removes the Pmurt-face disguises from all five statues.** There are 5 statues, not 4 — the fifth has a different design: a kindly old man with a big beard, holding an open book with a **play/pause button insignia** (Cooper/George's player confirms this was designed for George's backstory). Tapestry maps of Errolon show: Reven, Eustice, Gwongondola, Lozenland, rest of Sypium, Deneb (island archipelago), and the capital. An X-marked map shows organization targets: fresh X on Eustace, dry X's on Gwondola, Lozenland, Sypium, Vanini.

### Scene 5 — The Bridge, The Clock, The World Untrue (1:50:00–2:07:00)

The train crosses a bridge over the ocean heading toward the capital — **Alastair** — a massive bronze column city rising an impossible distance into the sky. The city is on a grid system, not floating; just very tall.

**Inquizo has a blue clock** — one of the evil clocks the party has been encountering. He throws it off the bridge into the ocean.

The clock hits stone and shatters. A **crack appears in reality**. The sky turns blood-red and bare bone. The sun goes black. The city ahead begins to **melt** — buildings sagging and flowing like wax. The clock tower at the center of Alastair glows blue with dark shadow tendrils pulling city pieces toward it. Hell hound-like creatures appear on the railway.

**A stained glass winged dragon** attacks the train car with shards of glass. Inquizo takes 9 damage. A silvery flame appears in the glass reflection, visible to Inquizo and George. A **vyvern-esque skeletal bat-winged creature** manifests spewing dark fog.

*"When hammer breaks on dragon scale / The circle falters, the right shall fail"* — this is happening right now. The prophecy stanza is describing this exact moment. The table recognizes it in real time.

**George summons Valu**, who turns the train into a sky train. The train eventually crashes into a **bathhouse** — large, ornate, gold leaf chandeliers, three feet of water.

### Scene 6 — The Bathhouse (2:07:00–2:19:00)

**Gbot'n is here.** He is NOT in prison (the pre-session "halfway to the sky prison" was incorrect). He is wearing a **pinstripe bathrobe**, standing in the shallow end of the bathhouse with a **wet croissant** actively disintegrating in his hand. He walks **on water**. A **pug in wizard robes and a wizard hat** is also in the bathhouse.

Bodies visible outside — some bloody, some just sleeping. Constance and Lila both survived the crash. The party is given two doors: the **market door** or the **slaughter door**. They choose the market door.

### Scene 7 — The Market in Alastair (2:19:00–2:51:00)

The market in the capital city of **Alastair** — a spinning welcome sign confirms the name. Various vendor stalls. Broad shopping session.

**Currency confirmed:** approximately 10 arrow = 1 gold piece.

**Mira buys:**
- Alchemist's tool belt — comes with 2 alchemist fire, 2 acid, 1 antitoxin; replenishes on long rest; costs 100 arrow

**Halcyra buys:**
- Embroidery — chance to replenish divine power as an action

**Inquizo buys:**
- 10 fire ant jars (in the "right size jar")
- 1 rainbow fire ant jar ("it looks like a rainbow, you got me there")
- A weird mirror — traded a glass shard still embedded in his own body (*"it's still in me because I don't remember it"*)

**George/Grungrak buys:**
- Wrist crossbow (100 arrow) — purchased partly using shared 100-piece coins
- 34 crossbow bolts
- An old goblin woman wanted a kiss for the discount; outcome uncertain

**Liz buys:**
- 2 rifles

### Scene 8 — The Prophetess (2:51:00–3:12:00)

Lila leads the party through the market and up stairs to a platform on the city's edge — open air on one side, a fence, cliff face to the ocean. On the platform:

- A **small white-haired girl in white long clothes** — Halcyra's player whispers *"prophetess"* upon seeing her. This is the girl Starglass's brother Haki went north to protect. She was referenced by THETA ("protect her"). She is connected to Starglass. A significant figure.
- A **burnt-skin boy** with growing claws and increasingly monstrous nails, wearing shorts and a ragged t-shirt. He is mid-transformation into something wrong.
- A **False Knight** with a large silver war hammer inlaid with trees and crystals — defending the prophetess.

The monster-boy says: *"You can't take her from me."* He lunges.

**SESSION ENDS HERE.** The prophetess runs. The monster-boy accelerates toward her. The False Knight raises its hammer.

## Lore & Worldbuilding Discoveries

1. **Sleeping sickness = gas** — the fat finch and elephant creatures are symptoms; the albino man (no gas mask himself) appears to be the source of the sleep agent via canisters. He has a dreamcatcher insignia on his vest.

2. **Amasia loyalists** — an organization of people loyal to the former Amasia regime; classified as terrorists by the Erloonian nation. Leader = **Gore Harlow** (ran a brothel for garrison affinity). His son = **Eskander** (betrayed family for Constance; now gone). Operative = **Lila Monger**.

3. **COG = Commonwealth Oversight Guild** — militia/mercenary structure that upholds local Commonwealth law. Lozenland is governed by its COG branch. Adventurers can function under COG authority.

4. **Capital = Alastair** — confirmed from a spinning welcome sign inside the city. A massive bronze column city rising far above the ocean — not floating, just extremely tall, on a grid system.

5. **Errolon map** — the Erloonian continent contains: Reven, Eustice (Eustace), Gwongondola, Lozenland, part of Sypium, Deneb (island archipelago), and Alastair (the capital). The organization has marked targets: fresh X on Eustace, dry X's on Gwondola, Lozenland, Sypium, and Vanini.

6. **FIVE statues** — not four. The fifth has a different god: a kindly old man with a big beard, holding an open book, with a play/pause button magical insignia. Cooper (George's player) was describing a god from his own world — this is not connected to George's in-game backstory.

7. **Blue clocks cause chaos** — throwing a blue clock (shattering it) opened a crack in reality, turned the sky blood-red, caused city structures to melt, and summoned hell creatures. The large clock tower in Alastair glows blue with shadow tendrils pulling city pieces toward it. Breaking clocks makes it worse. **Do not break clocks.**

8. **Prophecy confirmed live** — the stained glass winged dragon attacking the train matches *"When hammer breaks on dragon scale, the circle falters, the right shall fail."* The blood-red sky with the sun going black matches *"The sky's untrue."* OOC: DM confirmed these are intentional prophecy fulfillments.

9. **Numgis** — went north to **free the Nightmare God**. Made a deal with a child. Showed up in Alastair and created the clock tower problem. The organization is working with Numgis temporarily but is not true believer.

10. **The Prophetess** — a small white-haired girl in white clothing. Previously referenced by THETA ("protect her") and connected to Starglass (Haki went north to protect her). She is being protected by a False Knight and is being contested by the monster-boy.

11. **The monster-boy** — burnt skin, growing claws, monstrous nails, mid-transformation. Says *"You can't take her from me."* Mira has at least 3 missing brothers. Starglass had a brother. The possibility that this is Starglass's brother Haki — now transformed — is a real one.

12. **Gbot'n's actual location** — NOT in prison. He is in Alastair's bathhouse. The pre-session framing ("halfway to the sky prison") was inaccurate.
