### Who He Is

Grungrak doesn't remember who he is. His real name is Grungrak, but at some point he forgot, and he's been going by George ever since — the name he deflected with the moment Mira walked into a tavern and said *"Grungrak, is that you?"* He said no. He's been saying no ever since.

He was once a miner from a mountain town. That's almost all that's confirmed. He doesn't know what a penis is (genuinely, not as a bit). He carries the shards of a broken hammer he can't fully explain. He has gaps in basic knowledge about the world that suggest the amnesia goes deep. He met a version of himself in the River Lethe and held hands with him — a Natural 20 self-acceptance roll. He has been secretly learning the Ballad of Mira for approximately eight sessions and got caught in the act. He moved a heavy statue up two flights of stairs muttering *"I hate stairs"* and that's also who he is.

He is the heart of the group. Chaotic and warm and deeply confused about the world, but genuinely good — the kind of person who makes a stranger cry with songs in a tavern while a queen is being killed upstairs, who runs after Inquizo for no stated reason when Inquizo runs, who gives Reficul a speech about second chances using Reficul's own words. He has pomegranate seeds from Persephone's underworld inside him and doesn't know what that means yet. The prophecy names him directly. His amnesia may not be accidental.

### How He Fights

George is a Bard who hits surprisingly hard and supports the party with healing, crowd control, and emotional manipulation in roughly equal measure.

**Damage & Offense**
- **Thunder Wave** — his biggest combat tool; killed approximately 20 palace guards in a single cast in Session 11; also used to shatter a window during a bar sing-along
- **Earth Tremor** — used against a metal horse on a boat deck in Session 6 (ruling: boat decking counts as ground); used in the Pmurt fight
- **Cloud of Daggers** — 11 slashing damage to the cave creature in Session 10
- **Vicious Mockery** — cantrip; psychic damage + disadvantage
- **Tasha's Hideous Laughter** — used on the white-haired NPC in Session 3 (triggered by a fart)
- **Anglite rock (Session 7)** — threw a rock into the River Lethe during the Urscylla fight; strength save 19; the rock caused an electrical steam explosion that fired both him and George out of the water

**Healing & Support**
- **Healing Word** — bonus action heal; used on Arcus, Mira, and Starglass (healing Starglass made her slightly more metallic)
- **Mass Healing Word** — kept the party standing during the Pmurt fight
- **Spare the Dying** — cantrip; used on commoners knocked to 0 HP in the slums
- **Inspiring Leader / Motivational Speech** — gives the party temp HP before an encounter
- **Bardic Inspiration** — bonus die for another creature's roll
- **Calm Emotions** — 20-ft sphere DC 15 Charisma save; made a blood angel go docile in Session 5; partially de-escalated a hostile slum crowd in Session 8
- **Song of Healing** — healed Halcyra and Inquizo for 9 HP each in Session 10

**Utility**
- **Sleep** — knocked out a berserk, house-possessed dragonborn in Session 9 (it was under 30 HP; it worked)
- **Plant Growth** — dense growth to slow enemy movement; used against Galveston and hellhounds in Session 14; vines grew inside the mine clock in Session 15 and made the clock face fall off
- **Heroism** — touch spell; gives target immunity to frightened + temp HP each turn; used on Halcyra in Session 10
- **Nat 20 ship reboot (Session 14)** — the only party member to fully hear and retain the ship's reboot message: *"Reboot completed. Memory erased, wind collection restored, heart beating, destination status unknown, origin remembered. When the child breathes the vessel remembers the womb. Return the heart, return the breath, return the wandering daughter. Course corrected for home."*
- **Boulder push (Session 7)** — Nat 20, result 30; rolled Sisyphus's boulder over a cliff for the first time in history; it landed on and pinned Urscylla

### The People Around Him

> George, Halcyra, and Reficul were a trio before the party formed. They met Inquizo and Mira at the tavern — that's how the group came together.

**[[Halcyra]]**
She caught him secretly learning the Ballad of Mira on the airship. He shoved the paper in his pocket, went bright red, and ran. She hasn't told anyone. He knows she knows. The awkward silence between them about it is its own kind of thread.

**[[Reficul]]**
George gave him one of the most earnest speeches of the campaign — using Reficul's own philosophy of second chances back at him. He called out Reficul's name in a hell vision. He said *"Don't be scared of the stick"* when they parted. He called Reficul "gorgeous" once. Reficul called him "gorgeous" back. There is something genuine here that neither of them has fully named.

**[[Mira]]**
They knew each other in a previous campaign — a whole history George can't access. He has been secretly learning the Ballad of Mira for approximately eight sessions. In Session 8, Mira reached out first and apologized for pushing him too hard to remember. He opened up: *"He was kind. I see why he was your friend."* She was inside his shadow dragon. When she severed the cord, the real Grungrak emerged and embraced her.

**[[Inquizo]]**
Inquizo stole the core of his broken hammer and still has it. When George panicked and fled the throne room, Inquizo ran out after him for no stated reason. He sat on a throne and waited. No words needed.

### Prophecy Connections

- *"A shattered mind turns towards the gate / One must reverse the dwarven fate"* — his amnesia is part of the prophecy; he must actively reverse whatever dwarven fate was set for him
- *"A mind broken by design / Holds the rites of the divine / Memories tangled, hidden from time"* — the amnesia may not be accidental; someone may have broken his mind deliberately; he holds divine knowledge without knowing it
- *"When hammer breaks on the dragon's scale / When the written fate in shards is cast"* — his missing war hammer and the shards he carries are both named in the prophecy as trigger conditions for a larger event
