## Description

Starglass is a steampunk-adjacent airship. You'd call it a ship except it breathes. The nameplate says "Starglass" — and that's also the name of the being in the glass case below deck who is literally providing the respiration for the vessel through a network of pipes. The navigation AI is a hologram that addresses whoever's at the helm as "King Pmurt." The deck has a server room full of what looks like steampunk computers that the party cannot figure out. Below deck: a grandfather clock where only the second hand moves, a cargo hold, crew sleeping quarters, captain's quarters, and two glass cases. One of those cases has been opened. The other — foggy, faint blue glow, tapestry over it showing sun/rays/wings/sheep — has not.

This ship is not just a vehicle. It's a character. It has a name, a history, a mother, a brother, and secrets that are actively being discovered mid-flight.

---

## Significance

Pmurt received this ship from the Doctor in exchange for his crown. The Doctor had modified it — Starglass was already inside it when the trade happened. The party commandeered it after the island city revolution in Session 13, and [[Character Profiles/Inquizo|Inquizo]] directed it north by wearing Pmurt's crown and impersonating him to the navigation AI.

What the party has learned so far: the rune alphabet seen throughout the campaign (on buildings in X-salent, on the Velodorn wagons, in the doctor's dossier) is Malafera's language — Starglass's mother's language. The ship connects directly to the campaign's central mysteries. The grandfather clock, the server room, the second glass case, and the voice in the wind that George and Mira both heard aboard are all unresolved.
