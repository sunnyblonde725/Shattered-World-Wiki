# Inquizo's Hat Collection

*A living document. Updated as the collection grows.*

---

## Hat 1 — Bard Hat
**Acquired:** Before Session 1 — part of his starting equipment / personal belongings
**How he got it:** Unknown origin; he arrived wearing it
**Current status:** On his head (bottom of the stack)

---

## Hat 2 — Tiny Mystery Hat
**Acquired:** Discovered before or during the early sessions
**How he got it:** Found nested underneath the bard hat when he first examined his stack — it was already there; origin unknown
**Current status:** On his head (second layer, between the bard hat and anything above it)
**Notes:** Nobody knows how or when this got there. It may have always been there.

---

## Hat 3 — Pmurt's Crown
**Acquired:** Session 13 — found in the cargo hold / rooms of King Pmurt's commandeered airship
**How he got it:** Found it labeled "My special crown" aboard the ship; immediately put it on
**Current status:** On his head (top of the current stack)
**Notes:** Tall red velvet. About as tall as Inquizo himself. The ship's hologram navigation AI is programmed to recognize it — wearing the crown while bumping the push-to-start button caused the AI to greet him as "King Pmurt." He used this to mimic Pmurt's voice and direct the ship to fly north toward Sypium.

---

## Hat 4 — Tree-Symbol Hat #1
**Acquired:** Session 13 — found in the ship's cargo hold
**How he got it:** Found it during the airship exploration; kept it
**Current status:** In his possession
**Notes:** Tall hat with golden tree symbols on each side, branches reaching together. He declined to read the label.

---

## Hat 5 — Tree-Symbol Hat #2
**Acquired:** Session 13 — found in the ship's cargo hold alongside Hat 4
**How he got it:** Found as a pair with Hat 4
**Current status:** In his possession
**Notes:** Identical in description to Hat 4 — golden tree symbols, branches reaching together. He kept both.

---

## The DO NOT TOUCH Hat
**Status:** NOT YET ACQUIRED
**Location:** The pirate crew's back gear room aboard the submarine (encountered Session 2)
**Notes:** This hat exists in the pirate submarine's back gear room, labeled DO NOT TOUCH. Inquizo found it in Session 2. The session notes explicitly flag — twice across multiple sessions — that he never actually took it, and that the DM keeps flagging it. The DO NOT TOUCH hat is a known narrative time bomb. Something will eventually happen with it.
