# Locations

Every place the party has been — and a few they haven't reached yet.

---

## Where It All Happens

| Location | What It Is |
|----------|-----------|
| [[X-salent]] | A sprawling guild town that served as the party's Chapter 2 base, full of mounting mysteries. |
| [[The-Island-City\|The Island City]] | A post-revolution metropolis — now leaderless after Pmurt fled and Reficul punched Naamon to death. |
| [[Voxus]] | A bird kingdom where shadow therapy sessions revealed the party's depths and Valu briefly manifested. |
| [[Sypium]] | A convergence point to the north where the God of Dreams' unsealing mechanism awaits — and everyone is heading. |
| [[Pmurts-Airship-Starglass\|Pmurt's Airship — Starglass]] | A steampunk vessel where Starglass breathes for the ship and unresolved mysteries tick beneath every hatch. |
| [[King-Pmurts-Kingdom\|King Pmurt's Kingdom]] | An isolated, hermetically sealed kingdom that believed itself the entire world — until the party raided it in Session 1. |

## The Underworld Arc

| Location | What It Is |
|----------|-----------|
| [[The-Field-and-The-Pit\|The Field and The Pit]] | A field surrounding a pit of glowing white strings — it resisted the living, but welcomed the dead-adjacent. |
| [[The-Underground-Hell\|The Underground]] | A blood-soaked underworld level where the Blood Angel rose and the campaign's mythology went cosmically unhinged. |
| [[The-Shrine-of-Persephone\|The Shrine of Persephone]] | A pomegranate forest sanctuary where Persephone negotiated for the sickle and Grungrak ate seeds with consequences. |
| [[The-Underground-Temple\|The Underground Temple]] | A marble sanctuary holding Cybele's statue and the sickle — 247 sheep and a magical heartbeat that stopped. |
| [[The-Gate-of-Ivory\|The Gate of Ivory]] | A cliffside portal where the party exited the underworld after crushing River Styx pearls and became level 6. |
