## Appearance & Personality

Cowboy hat. Darker skin. An open booth — no glass panel between him and whatever comes through the door. He has been at this desk for four years and has achieved an almost Zen-like state of not caring about anything. His affect is completely flat. You can hear caffeination potion bottles clinking when he walks.

He assigned the party rooms in the 800s and 900s range. He retrieved [[Character Profiles/Reficul|Reficul]]'s records from the vault — a water-damaged page with the bottom half missing, including the birthdate. He did not react to this in any meaningful way.

His one moment of personality came at the end of the interaction: *"The pleasure was all yours."*

## Relationship to the Party

He processed their registration. He found the records. He assigned the rooms. He did not form a relationship with them. This is probably fine.
