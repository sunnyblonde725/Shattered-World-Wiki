## Appearance & Personality

Spectacles. Clipboard. Very obviously handwritten forms. The kind of person who runs on bureaucratic anxiety — organized specifically because if they weren't, everything would fall apart. They recognized [[Character Profiles/Mira|Mira]] Tiyake and Grungrak as former guild members who disappeared seven years ago and were immediately, visibly relieved that new adventurers had arrived.

After registering the party, they spotted another group of adventurers in the distance and bolted to greet them with the same urgent energy.

---

## Relationship to the Party

Brief and functional. They were the party's first contact with X-salent. They got everyone pointed toward Justin at the Guild Office and ran off to the next thing. The Notorious Nincompoops were mentioned as a reference point.
