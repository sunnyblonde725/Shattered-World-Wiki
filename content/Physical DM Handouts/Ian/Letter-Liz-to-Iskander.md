# Letter — Liz to Iskander

*Undated year. Written from Paradise Manor.*

---

April 16th

I did it! I joined the COG! I'm in Free Company 24B now. They said I'm the youngest one there… so I guess I win at something, right? You'd probably make that dumb face if you heard that. I wish you *were* here to make it.

I kinda sold my soul to the devil for the pay, but… it's a lot. Maybe enough that I can live in the Capital someday. That means I'll be closer to you. I'm getting closer. I really am.

Oh! And Evelyn's cough is getting better. I thought you'd want to know.

And! I changed my name before they wrote it down. Eustice is gone now. Just like that. You said we could start over, remember? I did my part.
…You're not even here.

Have you heard from the Doctor? He stopped telling me where you are. I don't know anything anymore. It feels like I'm just… stuck.

They call the manor "Paradise," but it doesn't feel like it when it's this quiet. I don't think it's just me.

I want to see you again.
…Or maybe punch you again.

I'm starting to worry you didn't get any of my letters. You wouldn't ignore me, right? You're not that stupid.
I'll
…I didn't mean that. Sorry.

The others need you. More than you think.
I know you're trying. I know you are.

I'm proud of you.
I think your mom would be proud too.

This is our address:

**[ Paradise Manor, Sypium, Solar Place ]**
Latitude: -3.87216, Longitude: 174.36311

Please come back soon, Iskander. Or tell me where you are, then I can deliver these letters myself.

We're a team, remember?
We're supposed to do everything together.

*Liz Larkin*

---

## Notes

- Liz's full name: **Elizabeth Sasha Constance Eustice** — she took the Larkin surname when she changed her name
- "Eustice is gone now" — **The Eustace community in Bermuda Foundation is named after her birth name**
- **Liz joined the COG, Free Company 24B** — the organization Alia mentioned wanting to visit for cartographers
- "Evelyn's cough is getting better" — Evelyn is one of the orphans at Paradise Manor
- The Doctor was once communicating Iskander's whereabouts to Liz — then stopped
- These letters were never delivered; found in Iskander's locked study
