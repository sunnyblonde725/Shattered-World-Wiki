## Appearance & Personality

Also called "Derv" or "Dervnumgis." In Session 1, he walked the party down a long hallway and opened the conversation by announcing that everything in existence is, fundamentally, related to sex — then escalated into a full argument with [[Character Profiles/Reficul|Reficul]]. He is articulate, ideological, and utterly convinced of his own prophetic purpose.

He favors spectacle: in the island city he communicated official policy via a waterfall-and-light projector system (described in-session as "Star Wars-ing it"). He was identified by party-adjacent sources as a man with wire glasses — the man seen at Ravalli's nest two days before the party arrived at Voxus.

---

## Backstory

Numgis started as Pmurt's vizier — the second-most-powerful man in an isolated kingdom that thought it was the only kingdom. At some point he began receiving dreams that he interpreted as divine instruction. The God of Dreams (Yomi) is imprisoned — sealed by her own nightmare/shadow — and Numgis was told, in those dreams, to free her.

His method: build a network. Disciples of dreams are meant to be power-nodes spread across the post-Shattering fractured world, with Pmurt as Node #1. He is working directly with the nightmare entity that imprisoned Yomi, which means he intends to unseal her on terms that benefit him, not her.

He has also referenced wanting to free a second unnamed "him" — a separate imprisoned entity whose identity is unknown. His connection to [[NPCs/The-Doctor|The Doctor]] is confirmed; what they are building together in the north is not.

---

## Relationship to the Party

Numgis cast a Nightmare Spell on the party in Session 1 (succeeded against [[Character Profiles/Mira|Mira]], [[Character Profiles/Halcyra|Halcyra]], and Grungrak; resisted by [[Character Profiles/Reficul|Reficul]] and [[Character Profiles/Inquizo|Inquizo]]). He offered passage on his personal floating ship; the party refused and jumped off the railing instead.

He has not been directly encountered since, but his fingerprints are everywhere: in Pmurt's palace, at Voxus, on the road to Sypium. The party needs to reach whatever unsealing mechanism is in the Sypium area before he does.
