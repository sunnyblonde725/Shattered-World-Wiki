**Player:** Cooper (Sunny's husband)

### Who He Is
Grungrak is the heart of the group — chaotic, warm, and deeply confused about both the world and himself. His real name is Grungrak, but at some point he forgot who he was and acquired the name George. How much of that original identity he recovers — and when — is something that will unfold through the sessions.

### What We Know About Him
- **Grungrak is his real name** — George is the name he picked up in a tavern in Session 1. Mira walked over and said "Grungrak, is that you?" and he deflected on the spot: "No. My name is George." He has gone by George ever since.
- He has **amnesia** — doesn't know his own identity, doesn't know what a penis is (genuinely, not as a bit), has gaps in basic knowledge about the world
- He was once **a miner who lived in a mountain town** — one of the few backstory fragments confirmed
- He is **friends with Mira** from the previous campaign — George doesn't recognize her, but their history is real
- He carries **shards of his broken hammer** — the core of the hammer was stolen by Inquizo
- He **bought a new war hammer** in Session 11 — the smithing hammer has been replaced; the original hammer's core was stolen by Inquizo and that thread remains unresolved
- He **healed Reficul** in Session 2 out of guilt about something involving an eye

### Prophecy Connections
- *"A shattered mind turns towards the gate / One must reverse the dwarven fate"* — his amnesia is part of the prophecy; he must actively reverse whatever dwarven fate was set for him
- *"A mind broken by design / Holds the rites of the divine / Memories tangled, hidden from time"* — possibly Grungrak; the amnesia may not be accidental — someone may have broken his mind deliberately; he holds divine knowledge without knowing it
- *"When hammer breaks on the dragon's scale / When the written fate in shards is cast"* — his missing war hammer and the shards he carries are both named in the prophecy as trigger conditions for a larger event

### Relationships

> George, Halcyra, and Reficul were a trio before the party formed. They met [[Inquizo]] and [[Mira]] at the tavern — that's how the group came together.

**[[Halcyra]]**
She caught him secretly learning the Ballad of Mira on the airship. He shoved the paper in his pocket, went bright red, and ran. She hasn't told anyone. He knows she knows. The awkward silence between them about it is its own kind of thread.

**[[Reficul]]**
After the Victorian manor chaos, George gave Reficul one of the most earnest speeches of the campaign — using Reficul's own philosophy of second chances back at him. George called out Reficul's name in a hell vision. He said *"Don't be scared of the stick"* when they parted. He called Reficul "gorgeous" once. Reficul called him "gorgeous" back. There is something genuine here that neither of them has fully named.

**[[Mira]]**
They knew each other in a previous campaign — a whole history George can't access. He has been secretly learning the Ballad of Mira for approximately 8 sessions. In Session 8, Mira reached out first and apologized for pushing him too hard to remember. George opened up about Grungrak — talking about him as if he were someone else entirely: *"He was kind. I see why he was your friend."* She is inside his shadow dragon in a way that isn't fully understood yet. When she severed the cord, the real Grungrak emerged and embraced her.

**[[Inquizo]]**
Inquizo stole the core of George's broken hammer and still has it. When George panicked and ran from the throne room, Inquizo ran out too — just to keep him company. He sat on a throne and waited. No words needed.
