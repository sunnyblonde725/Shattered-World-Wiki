## Description

X-salent is a wide, flat guild town — it spreads out rather than up. The main building is three stories tall and shaped like a horseshoe, with outward-facing doors like a motel, each one belonging to a different guild or department. The heraldry is two crossed feathers in an X. Behind the town when you arrive: a saltwater river that smells like the sea — and it stops 100 feet too early, which no one has explained yet. Ahead: the guild complex, numbered blocks of rooms, a tavern, and a whole city's worth of ongoing problems. Somewhere in the distance, mountains that shouldn't exist.

It's not glamorous. It's exactly the kind of place where the party would register themselves under a name like "The Incredible Mistakes" and immediately get handed three quests they haven't finished yet.

---

## Significance

X-salent is [[Character Profiles/Mira|Mira]] and [[Character Profiles/Grungrak|Grungrak]]'s former guild town — they have history here. As a Chapter 2 base, it's where the party re-established themselves after returning from the underworld, got rooms, and started picking up threads. Those threads turned out to be: a drying river, a writing creature in the woods, a possessed Victorian manor in the wealthy district, a suspicious caravan from a city that appeared out of nowhere, and a dead man in a bear den. So, normal guild town stuff.

The world has changed. The new mountains and volcanoes visible from the city walls are a reminder that the Shattering didn't stop — it just became the new normal.
