## Appearance & Personality

Made of metal. Has an axe attached to its bandana collar. The collar reads "..." — three dots, meaning "source," identifying its lineage as the grandson of Poseidon's horse. It pressed elevator buttons with the flat face of its axe, which is a very particular skill set.

First encountered as the anchor on [[Character Profiles/Reficul|Reficul]]'s boat. When disturbed, it attacked. Once subdued (Burning Hands + Earth Tremor), it sat down on the hole it had helped create and fell asleep. It does not need air to breathe. It is not a normal horse.

---

## Backstory

The grandson of Poseidon's horse. Poseidon is implied to be present in this campaign world — this relationship may not go unnoticed by the god of the seas. It was serving as the literal anchor on a boat in the underworld before the party showed up and woke it up.

After the fight, it traveled through the underworld elevator with the party, pressing buttons with its axe, and exited through the Gate of Ivory into the living world. [[Character Profiles/Halcyra|Halcyra]] rode it through and never dismounted. The horse appears to accept this arrangement.

---

## Relationship to the Party

Halcyra's mount. It attacked the party first, then became theirs. [[NPCs/Penopolis|Penopolis]] asked if it needed any snacks, which it presumably did not. It grants advantage on Athletics checks to whoever rides it.
