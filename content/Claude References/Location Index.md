## Location Index

#### [[The Tavern]] *(unnamed — Session 1 meeting point)*
- **First visited:** Session 1
- **Description:** A tavern in an unnamed town; the party's first meeting place
- **Significance:** Where all five party members first came together. George got his name here. Inquizo sold all the pillows.
- **Status:** Left
- **Session history:**
  - Session 1 — Party convergence point. George introduced himself as "George" to Mira. Inquizo stole a rock, sold all the pillows.

#### [[The Carnival]]
- **First visited:** Session 1
- **Description:** A traveling or established carnival with a thoroughfare, games, and a large big top tent
- **Significance:** Where the party was kidnapped by hooded figures with red fog. Also where Reficul's Nat 20 performance triggered a spontaneous dwarf circus. The "Chosen One" child moment happened here.
- **Status:** Left — under unclear circumstances (knocked out and transported)
- **Session history:**
  - Session 1 — Cotton candy chaos, strongman game, dwarf circus, kidnapping via red fog and hooded figures.

#### [[King Pmurt's Kingdom]] *(unnamed — island/coastal cliff location)*
- **First visited:** Session 1
- **Description:** An isolated kingdom on what appears to be a coastal island or cliff-top location, accessible via stone stairs from a beach. Contains a castle with gold-plated decorations and a town square with four god-statues.
- **Significance:** The party's first proper location after being shipwrecked. A kingdom with no knowledge of the outside world — only humans exist, only this kingdom exists, King Pmurt rules it all. One of the four statues (the fishtail one) was stolen by the party on the pirate crew's behalf.
- **Status:** Left *(after heist at end of Session 1)*
- **Notable sub-locations:** King Pmurt's throne room; Numgis's hallway and balcony; town square (four statues); the castle walls
- **Session history:**
  - Session 1 — Party infiltrated, met King Pmurt and Numgis, jumped off the balcony, stole the fishtail statue, escaped to beach.

#### [[The Beach]]
- **First visited:** Session 1 *(shipwrecked arrival)* / Session 2 *(pirate ultimatum)*
- **Description:** Sandy shore at the base of the mountain/cliffs. Two sections: a first beach (where party woke up shipwrecked) and a second beach around the corner (where the pirate crew was anchored).
- **Significance:** The party woke up here after the carnival kidnapping. Also where the pirate captain issued her "submarine or die" ultimatum. The two beaches are connected around a corner — the party didn't know this in Session 1.
- **Status:** Left
- **Session history:**
  - Session 1 — Party woke up shipwrecked. Climbed stairs to kingdom. Jumped from balcony onto second beach. Met pirate crew. Stole statue. Received second job.
  - Session 2 — Opening scene. Pirate captain confrontation. Party boards submarine.

#### [[The Submarine]]
- **First visited:** Session 2
- **Description:** A metal aircraft/submarine hybrid with a full button panel, magical interior, and a hidden back room containing dive suits, pirate costumes, and miscellaneous gear
- **Significance:** The pirate captain's transport. Pre-programmed to dock at the underwater city. Contains the DO NOT TOUCH pirate hat (now claimed by [[Inquizo]]).
- **Status:** Docked at underwater city — likely left behind when party departed after Session 3
- **Session history:**
  - Session 2 — Chaos aboard. Reficul locked outside in shark form. Grungrak nearly drowned. Button 61 unlocked the back room. Submarine auto-docked.
  - Session 3 — Grungrak woke up here after the Session 2 events, then left to find a bar.

#### [[The Underwater City]]
- **First visited:** Session 2
- **Description:** A city built into the ocean floor. Pearly white and gold paths, tidal pools where citizens relax, windows looking out into the surrounding ocean, and a tall castle with a spiral staircase. Air-filled interior. Clams on the walls function as surveillance cameras ("Security Glabra").
- **Significance:** A women-only society formerly at war with winged beings ("bird folk"). Ariel's death changed the city's power structure — the silencing enchantment is gone, the mermen's contract status is unresolved, and the city is leaderless.
- **Status:** Left (after Session 3 long rest)
- **Session history:**
  - Session 2 — Party docks, navigates the city. Reficul imprisoned. Mira and Inquizo meet the historian. Party ascends to the throne room.
  - Session 3 — Ariel defeated. Hamish freed and named. Grungrak at the bar with Fish George. Long rest taken. Party departs.

#### [[The Holding Room]]
- **First visited:** Session 2
- **Description:** A room inside the underwater city with a window overlooking the city's water channels
- **Significance:** Where Reficul was imprisoned after being captured in shark form. Site of his philosophical conversation with Sisyphus.
- **Status:** Left
- **Session history:**
  - Session 2 — Reficul's base of operations for the session. Long telepathic conversation with Sisyphus through the window.

#### [[The Throne Room]]
- **First visited:** Session 2
- **Description:** Top floor of the city castle, third floor up a spiral staircase. Massive arched door, two seahorse guards flanking the entrance.
- **Significance:** Where the party fought and defeated Queen Ariel. Session 2's cliffhanger was resolved here.
- **Status:** Left
- **Session history:**
  - Session 2 — Mira delivered the diplomatic report. Queen declared combat.
  - Session 3 — Full combat. Ariel charmed half the party. Reficul died. Ariel killed. Silence lifted.

#### [[The Field / The Pit]]
- **First visited:** Session 3
- **Description:** An open field/forest area featuring a circular pit section filled with glowing white strings. The pit has a mud-like quality that resists movement — specifically for certain people. Creatures from the northern forest are nearby. The underground temple is accessible beneath.
- **Significance:** Where the party encountered Arcus and Lorya. Also where Mira's death-state was first revealed.
- **Status:** Left (party descended underground in Session 4)
- **Session history:**
  - Session 3 — Arcus encounter. Mira revelation. Good Boy of the North prayer. Lorya and hyenas emerged and retreated.
  - Session 4 — Party passed through the barrier (Mira freely; Halcyra and Inquizo via Lorya's cloak). Descended via secret tunnel.

#### [[The Abandoned Town]]
- **First visited:** Session 4
- **Description:** A cluster of rotting wooden buildings covered in fungus, adjacent to the empty crop field. Long-abandoned. Nursery with an empty crib inside. Murals on the exterior: winged figure descending; nails digging into the ground and raising it up. Interior murals: giant glowing horns, black-winged angelic being.
- **Significance:** Source of the jade fragment ("Birth," Infernal 9th accent), the topaz gem (carving of a man, Celestial inscription), and the black orb — Caelus's prison. The children who should have lived here were taken when the seed cycle broke.
- **Status:** Left
- **Session history:**
  - Session 4 — Explored. Key items recovered. Backstory for the agricultural agreement established.

#### [[The Underground Temple]]
- **First visited:** Session 4 (temple), Session 3 (descended into it briefly)
- **Description:** Marble staircase (dead hybrid willow/grain seeds on steps), 247 sheep, stone altar with the Cybele/Avani statue (petrified wood clothing like lava — red and blue mosaic), a newer stone cairn containing the sickle. A magical heartbeat pulsed through the space (stopped when Arcus activated the sickle's clockwork).
- **Significance:** Resting place of Cybele/Avani's statue and the sickle "Castrator of the Sky." Arcus's handwritten prayer is on the altar base. Entry point to Hell below via Lorya's secret tunnel.
- **Status:** Party passed through; Arcus remained here with the sheep while party descended
- **Session history:**
  - Session 4 — Statue identified as Cybele/Avani. Sickle found and wielded by Grungrak. Lorya's tunnel used to descend.

#### [[The Underground / Hell]]
- **First visited:** Session 4
- **Description:** An otherworldly space accessed by falling through Lorya's tunnel. Ankle-deep in dark, viscous blood. Nothing like anywhere the party has experienced. The sickle confirmed "down = Hell." The DM confirmed: "You passed through more than just a hole getting here."
- **Significance:** Current combat zone. The blood angel rose here. Ways out include: passing through rifts/reality tears, or death.
- **Status:** Current location (as of Session 4 end — Phase Two combat begins here next session)
- **Session history:**
  - Session 4 — Party descended. Blood angel encounter. Angel defeated. Arcus's eyes went dark. Mira's memories stolen. Phase Two of combat teased.

#### [[The Shrine of Persephone]] *(Underworld — Black Sand Beach)*
- **First visited:** Session 6
- **Description:** Black sand beach. White tree forest with black leaves and pomegranates — dryad/nymph habitat, now cleared by Grungrak. A building at the forest's edge serves as Persephone's shrine. Inside: a swirling soul pool (not water, but souls), a chest of Reficul's clothes, and several very distressed dryads with protest signs.
- **Significance:** Where the party's boat crashed. Where the sickle was handed over. Where Eric was recovered. The pomegranate forest is mythologically significant — the seeds Grungrak ate here have unresolved consequences.
- **Status:** Left (party exited via elevator)
- **Session history:**
  - Session 6 — Boat crash. Reficul's first death. Grungrak clears the forest with sickle. Persephone negotiation. Sickle given over. Eric retrieved from soul pool. Party exits to upper city.

#### [[The Upper City of the Underworld]] *(Underworld — Civic Level)*
- **First visited:** Session 6
- **Description:** Accessible via elevator from the Fields of Asphodel level. Skyscrapers. Trees wrapped around buildings. Roads of both asphalt and flowing water — boat traffic runs along the water roads like Venice. This is where the Gate of Ivory is located.
- **Significance:** The exit level of the underworld. The Psychopomp (Charon) docks here. The Gate of Ivory is the party's route out.
- **Status:** Left — party passed through the Gate of Ivory in Session 7
- **Session history:**
  - Session 6 — Party arrives via elevator. Receives new map from Persephone. Boards new boat with Charon. Scylla surfaces — initiative called. Session ends here.
  - Session 7 — Scylla fought; Grungrak pushes boulder, pins Scylla. Party crushes pearls and passes through the Gate of Ivory.

#### [[The Gate of Ivory — Beach]] *(Underworld exit point)*
- **First visited:** Session 7
- **Description:** Sandy beach at the base of a cliff face. Cliffs on both sides. The boat crashed here. Sisyphus's cliff is accessible from the beach. The Gate of Ivory itself is a portal in the cliffside. The Saltwater Falls ("the pit") is approximately 100 feet away.
- **Significance:** The exit from the underworld. Where Reficul reappeared. Where the boulder was pushed. Where the River Styx pearls were crushed.
- **Status:** Left — party passed through the Gate of Ivory
- **Session history:**
  - Session 7 — Scylla beach confrontation. Reficul's brief reappearance. Boulder thrown. Pearls crushed. Party levels up. Party exits through the gate.

#### [[X-salent]] *(Guild Town — Chapter 2 base)*
- **First visited:** Session 7
- **Description:** A large, flat guild town — wider than it is tall. Horseshoe-shaped main building (three stories, outward-facing doors like a motel). Heraldry: two feathers crossed into an X. Wooden buildings in the surrounding area. Behind the party when they arrive: a saltwater river that smells like the sea. Ahead: the guild complex. Guild rooms (former housing) radiate outward in numbered blocks. A tavern is part of the complex. Guild Room 5 = Cartographers. Rooms 800s and 900s = residential.
- **Significance:** Mira and Grungrak's former guild town. Now the party's Chapter 2 base. Multiple quests available. The world has changed here — mountains and volcanoes visible that weren't there six months ago.
- **Status:** Current party location
- **Session history:**
  - Session 7 — Party arrives through the Gate of Ivory portal. Registers as "The Incredible Mistakes." Gets rooms. Meets Justin, Clipboard NPC, Glendale, Penopolis. Three quests available. Chapter 2 begins.
  - Session 8 — Morning. Party reunites after split night. Party investigates writing mystery in wealthy district. Reficul causes chaos in slums (4 homeless people briefly killed, all revived). Grungrak/George and Mira reconcile. Inquizo snuck off unknown. Mira heading to Alchemy Guild. Grungrak heading to blacksmith. Session ended with Reficul still in Oni form.
  - Session 9 — Mira spends the day at the Alchemy Guild (substance analysis with Altair and Leander) then the city library (finds "Ruins from Ancient Ages" pages 35–40, copies 18 plant entries; assisted by Ny'e). Refisul and others explore the writing-covered Victorian manor in the wealthy district; encounter adventuring party inside; dragonborn is possessed by the house, goes berserk; Grungrack's Sleep spell saves the party; house screams as they flee. The spaghetti incident occurs during/after. Grungrack gives Refisul a speech. Halqira prays to Apollo at temple district; YOMI letters glow. Prophecy Day postponed again. It is now 8 PM in-game. Party heading to Refisul's apartment for long rest. Plan for next session: forest in the morning.
  - Session 10 — Morning at Refisul's apartment; card game reveals (Halcyra is 18, Refisul's hands moved on their own when writing as a child, George's grief over original Grungrak). Velodorn caravan goods are gooped at the gate (Transmutation magic). Party goes to forest; follows trail of candle wax and broken branches to a cave. Encounters and kills a goop creature (NOT celestial/fiend/undead; a construct built to consume). Missing merchant confirmed dead and consumed. Two guilds now investigating caravan at gate. Enchanters found runes on wagon bottoms (same alphabet as ruins). Mira has the dead man's journal.
  - **Sub-locations confirmed (Sessions 8–10):** Slum district along outer city wall; wealthy Victorian manor district near Guild Hall (houses covered in writing are actively malevolent); river separates two sections of the city; alchemy guild and city library confirmed; Apollo's temple in the temple district; Refisul's apartment (party long rest location); forest trail and cave (bear den, ~35 ft wide) confirmed; caravan camping site in woods (~1 hour from gate, party plans to investigate).

#### [[The Cave (Old Bear Den)]] *(Forest outside X-salent)*
- **First visited:** Session 10
- **Description:** An old bear den approximately 35 feet wide, located in the forest outside X-salent. Reached via a trail of hardened candle wax, broken branches, and burned-out torches. Inside when the party arrived: the dead body of the missing merchant, 7 burned-out torches, 1 burning torch, and an open journal on the ground.
- **Significance:** Site of the goop creature encounter. The creature materialized here from the walls, consumed the corpse, and was killed by the party. The goop from the dead creature absorbed into the ground. Mira recovered the journal.
- **Status:** Cleared — creature dead; goop absorbed; journal and evidence retrieved
- **Session history:**
  - Session 10 — Party followed trail here; killed the goop creature; recovered journal and evidence.

#### [[The Island City]] *(Mert's Kingdom — offshore)*
- **First referenced:** Before Session 11; party arrived Session 11
- **Description:** An island city ruled by King Mert, connected to the mainland by a portal system. The city has at least two portcullises (bodies hanging from both), a prison building, a palace with a throne room, and a market district where the party purchased supplies before the mission.
- **Significance:** The primary location for Session 11. Mert rules here with the backing of Nungus (the Nightmare prophet) and uses visible brutality to suppress rebellion. The resistance is real but small. The party infiltrated the palace, fought Mert, and Mert escaped. The party is still in the throne room at session's end.
- **Status:** Party currently present (end of Session 11)
- **Notable sub-locations:**
  - **The Portal** — connects to a mainland town; operates at limited hours; run by the gender-fluid portal operator
  - **The Market** — where the party bought supplies (George bought his war hammer here)
  - **The Prison Building** — holds mostly women, birds, and mermaids; observed by Inquisio during recon; a hub of the dissenter population
  - **The Palace** — Mert's residence; multiple guard layers; kitchen (chef encounter); throne room (Mert fight)
  - **Mert's Bedroom** — observed by Inquisio during recon; empty bed; Mert was not sleeping
  - **The Throne Room** — site of the King Mert fight; has a chandelier with a lever; space for a large court; now emptied of ~20 guards by George's Thunder Wave
  - **The Town Square / Platform** — a platform built overnight by Naamon's forces; site of public executions; the Thorin statue was beneath it; site of Reficul's speech and double killing (Session 13)
- **Status (updated Session 13):** Post-revolution — Naamon dead; former prisoners in control; executions ongoing but disrupted by Reficul; Reficul staying as stabilizing presence with Gunge incoming; no stable leadership
- **Session history:**
  - Session 11 — Party traveled here via portal; infiltrated the palace; fought King Mert; Mert escaped; party at session's end is standing in the throne room at level 6.
  - Session 13 — Party returned via airship; found city in revolution under Naamon; executions in progress; Reficul gave speech and killed two people; Thorin statue removed and taken north; Reficul stayed; party departed.
