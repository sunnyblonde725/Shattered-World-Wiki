# Things Inquizo Has Stolen

*An incomplete record. Probably.*

---

## All the Pillows in the Inn
**Stolen from:** The shared room at the tavern (specifically from the room Mira was staying in)
**Session:** Session 1
**How:** Went upstairs while invisible and took all of them
**Outcome:** Kept — this was his very first act as a party member. No explanation was ever given.

---

## An Irresponsible Amount of Cotton Candy
**Stolen from:** A vendor at the carnival
**Session:** Session 1
**How:** Wandered the carnival stalls while invisible; took a "truly irresponsible amount"
**Outcome:** Consumed — he wandered the carnival as a bundle of floating cotton candy, visible to everyone because the cotton candy was not invisible

---

## The Core of Grungrak's Broken War Hammer
**Stolen from:** Grungrak
**Session:** Session 3 (or thereabouts — confirmed as in Inquizo's possession by Session 3)
**How:** The original Grungrak's hammer was destroyed when he struck a dragon with it; the core was left behind. Inquizo took it.
**Outcome:** Still has it — the hammer shards are with Grungrak/George and the core is in Inquizo's possession. What happens when they are reunited is an unresolved thread.

---

## Lorya's Serpent-Circle Jewel
**Stolen from:** Lorya (daughter of Cybele/Avani), discovered on the ground near the mirror wall in the nail cave after she died
**Session:** Session 5
**How:** Found it on the ground while moving through the nail cave; picked it up and kept it
**Outcome:** Still has it — its purpose is unknown; it may have been meant for him given the serpent imagery that keeps appearing specifically around Inquizo

---

## Pirate Crew Items from the Back Room (attempted)
**Stolen from:** The pirate captain's back gear room in the submarine
**Session:** Session 2
**How:** Found the back room after pressing button 61; immediately started poking through the contents "playing dumb"
**Outcome:** Unclear what he actually took vs. examined. The character notes say he was "poking through the contents." Nothing specific is confirmed as stolen from this room in the session notes — except that he did not take the DO NOT TOUCH hat (flagged multiple times as something he never actually took).

---

## The Guard's Notebook (attempted / borderline)
**Stolen from:** A city guard in X-salent investigating the writing incidents
**Session:** Session 8
**How:** It was Grungrak who nabbed the guard's notebook, not Inquizo — noted here for clarity. Grungrak is the one who copied the poem from it.
**Outcome:** Not Inquizo — Grungrak

---

## A Gas Mask (+ Accidental Hamster)
**Stolen from:** The albino man's caravan / kidnappers
**Session:** Session 16
**How:** Inquizo was the only party member awake (nat 20 Wisdom save). He went invisible, located the caravan, and stole the gas mask from the kidnappers while they were occupied managing the unconscious party members. The hamster was attached to or inside the gas mask. He did not know about the hamster. He now has a hamster.
**Outcome:** Kept both. The hamster was not intentional. The hamster is now his.
