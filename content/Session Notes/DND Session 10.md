# Session 10 — The Thing That Eats

*Linked to: [[Shattered World]] | [[Halcyra]] | [[Grungrak]] | [[Reficul]] | [[Inquizo]] | [[Mira]]*

---

## Pre-Session Recap

Session 9 ended with the party at Reficul's apartment, planning to head into the forest in the morning to investigate the creature from the poem. Mira had completed her alchemy and library research — confirmed the mystery substance is not from SKRP, reacts with Agopyl (maple stem membrane) to turn gaseous, and is fixed by salt. The YOMI letters glowed during Halcyra's prayer at the temple (either divine response or heat reaction). Prophecy Day was again postponed. In-game time: 8 PM.

---

## TL;DR

The party wakes up at Reficul's apartment and plays a "get to know you" card game over apology omelets (Reficul's apology for Grungrak's actions against the slum community), revealing that Halcyra is 18 years old, Reficul learned to write before he could speak (his hands moved on their own), and George knows exactly who the original Grungrak was and grieves him quietly. After Grungrak delivers a speech that gives everyone temporary HP, the party heads toward the forest — but first witnesses a caravan from Velodorn (a city that appeared five months ago) get its entire shipment of goods coated in black inky goop the moment the wagons crossed through X-salent's gate. In the forest, they follow a trail of hardened candle wax and broken branches to a cave. Inside: a dead, emaciated body, seven burned-out torches, one burning torch, and an open journal. Then a creature materializes out of the walls (described in No-Face-adjacent terms — probably Spirited Away-inspired, not the character itself), consumes the corpse, and reveals itself — a "fucked up deer" amalgamation made entirely of the same black goop substance as the wall writing and the caravan, with hypodermic needle fangs, bear-like claws, and a second head forming. The fight nearly costs Inquizo his life — the creature grabs him in its mouth and flees. Reficul, cursed into kobold form, kills it with a mote of light to the skull: both heads explode, the body crumples to goop. Post-combat, Halcyra and Inquizo both have spreading goop in their bite wounds. On the return, the party confirms the goop is chemically identical to all the other mystery substance they've found, enchanters at the gate reveal runes on the bottom of the caravan wagons from the same alphabet family as the ruins, and the dead body matched the missing-person they were hunting. Quest complete. *(Note: The party was never going to get paid for this — no payment was ever actually arranged.)*

## Full Summary

### Scene 1 — Morning at Reficul's Apartment

The party wakes up in stages across the early morning hours:
- **Reficul** woke at **2 AM** (sad hours), realized he had failed his self-imposed push-up contract (he needed 30; managed 28), and channeled the guilt into making **apology omelets** for the group — the apology was for Grungrak's behavior against the slum community in the previous session
- **Inquizo** woke at **5 AM** and silently consumed multiple omelets without ceremony
- **Halcyra** woke at **7 AM** for morning prayers
- **Mira** woke around **9:30 AM**
- **Grungrak** woke last and was immediately, inexplicably energetic

### Scene 2 — Spin-the-Bottle "Get To Know You" Card Game

Before setting out, the party played a spin-the-bottle-style get-to-know-you card game that produced several major character reveals.

**Reficul's childhood defining moment:** He picked up an ink and quill as a child and *"they moved my hands."* He learned to write before he learned to speak. The writing was unintelligible to anyone else.

**Halcyra:** She is **18 years old**. She left home at 18 — there was a disconnect with her mother over her father (Apollo). She also revealed that she believes **George is part of the prophecy** and has quietly sworn to protect him.

**Mira, asked what she misses most about the original Grungrak:** *"It's not something I can ask of you to replicate."*

**Mira, asked about the original Grungrak** *(note: this quote is attributed to Mira, not George)*: *"He was a very talented storyteller... the most enthusiastic individual I've ever met. He was my best friend. He found me when I first came to the guild. Decided we were gonna be best friends, just by my attempts not to be. And now it's in our river."*

**Ages confirmed this session:**
- Halcyra: 18
- Reficul: approximately 25
- Grungrak: 61 (just had his birthday; dwarves live to 350)

**The hammer:** Grungrak destroyed his war hammer when he smacked a dragon over the head with it. It launched through a portal. Grungrak (the original) went with it — Mira believed he was dead, but he never truly died. *(Note: confirm this detail with Ian/Cooper if needed.)*

**Note:** Mira still has not been told the full prophecy. Halcyra has tried multiple times but Mira has always been unavailable.

### Scene 3 — Pre-Forest / Departure

Grungrak delivered a motivational speech to the party before leaving. Mechanical effect: everyone gains **6 temporary HP** for the day (Mira received only 4 due to her psychic package). The city was quiet as they departed — no incidents.

### Scene 4 — The Caravan Incident at the Gate

A **caravan from Velodorn** — a city that appeared in the world approximately five months ago — was entering X-salent. As the wagons passed through the city gate, every piece of cargo was instantly coated in black inky goop. Notably: the people and the horses were unaffected. Only the goods.

- Mira threw salt → it hardened to a "burnt caramel" consistency
- **Detect Magic** (Mira) → Transmutation school magic was on all wagons
- The effect was triggered by the act of **crossing the city gate boundary**
- The caravan had camped in the woods approximately 1 hour from the gate the night before

The caravan was a shipment of **bedding and silk**. Considerable local disruption at the main gate as a result.

### Major Lore Revelation: Salt Is "White Gold"

This scene prompted a worldbuilding reveal from the DM: **salt is one of the most valuable substances in the entire world.** It is only available from **two locations**. The surface oceans are freshwater — no salt water on the surface. As a result:
- Most people in this world have never cooked with salt
- Salt is worth more than gold
- **X-salent is a destination partly because it has food with salt** (it sits near saltwater falls)

### Scene 5 — The Forest: The Trail

Following the plan from Session 9, the party entered the forest. A trail revealed itself:
- Hardened **candle wax drips** → stumps of melted candles someone was carrying while moving
- Trail of **broken branches** — someone was running
- **Burned-out dropped torches** — more signs of panicked movement

The trail led to a **cave / old bear den**, approximately 35 feet wide.

**Mira found a Gavikwest berry** (one of the fetch quest items) on the way to the cave.

### Scene 6 — The Cave

Inside the cave:
- A dead, emaciated human body
- Seven burned-out torches arranged around the space
- One still-burning torch
- An **open journal** lying on the ground

### Scene 7 — The Creature (Combat)

Something pressed against the party's backs from outside — the DM described its initial form as similar to No-Face *(probably Spirited Away-inspired, though the creature itself is not No-Face)* — and passed through them directly into the cave. It moved toward the dead body and began consuming it.

As it ate, it **became visible**. The DM described it as a *"fucked up deer"* — a body suggesting chronic wasting disease, with serpentine elements, **hypodermic needle fangs**, **bear-like claws**, and a **second head beginning to form** from the substance of the first. It was made entirely of the same black inky goop. SCP/Magnus Archives-inspired (confirmed by DM); also possibly Spirited Away-adjacent in initial appearance.

**Halcyra's Divine Sense:** The creature is **NOT a celestial, fiend, or undead.**

**Initiative order (high to low):** George (19) → Reficul → Grungrak → Mira (8) → Inquizo → Halcyra (~5)

**Round-by-round notable actions:**

**The kill shot:** Reficul's mote of light hit the creature at the base of its skull. It let out *"the worst sound you've ever heard in your entire life"* — a mixture of nails on chalkboard, drowning sounds, and *"the wet sound of people smiling."* Both heads exploded simultaneously. The body crumpled into a pile of goop and began absorbing into the ground.

Inquizo was dropped from its mouth.

### Scene 8 — Post-Combat: Wounds and Investigation

**Bite wound aftermath:**
- **Halcyra:** Small puncture holes on her arm (snake-tooth pattern) with black goop spreading from them
- **Inquizo:** Goop around his entire midsection — he was in its mouth for multiple rounds

Halcyra cast **Cure Wounds** on herself (9 HP) and on Inquizo. The healing expelled some goop and sealed the wounds, but **dark shadow marks remained** at each bite point. The wounds felt like a "deep ache," like a shot still lingering. Mira noted she was out of 1st-level spells.

**Investigation of the creature's remains:**
- Mira sprinkled salt on the goop remnants → same coagulation reaction as the wall substance and caravan goop. **Chemically identical.**
- Creature anatomy: primarily a digestive tract. Muscle fibers started at the skull and dissipated into goop further down. The DM clarified this was **a new construct forming muscles**, not a reanimated corpse — *"it was built to be a stomach. Consumption was its top priority."*
- The killed body had been completely consumed — no remnant.
- Party collected: skull shards, snake teeth (evidence), a bottle of goop (Mira, for research)
- The goop slowly absorbed into the ground while they observed

**Mira grabbed the journal** (partially on fire during combat; Warding Wind extinguished the flames).

### Scene 9 — The Missing Person Confirmed

The DM asked: did the dead body match the missing-person description from the fetch quest? Answer: yes. **Average height human, dark complexion, appeared to have been a merchant degraded to homeless — extremely emaciated.** The party had found their mark. The creature that killed them is also dead.

The party can claim quest completion. They have: skull shards, teeth, and goop samples as evidence.

### Scene 10 — Return to X-salent

Returning to the city gate, the caravan was still blocking the entrance. Two groups were investigating it:

**The Alchemist Guild** — half-exploded clothing, covered in soot; testing via the combustion approach. When Mira approached, a guild representative told her: *"When you combine some agopyl matter with the ink and add some heat, the material becomes gaseous."* Her own research, confirmed independently by another party. The problem: they don't have enough agopyl to remove the goop from an entire caravan's worth of goods.

**The Enchanter's Guild** — older, more disciplined, using special eyeglasses, reading instruments under the carts. Also present: the ex-alchemist-guild member who lost his license (still has singed hair and soot on his cheeks, now trying to fit in with the enchanters).

**George overheard the Enchanters** discussing what they found: **runes on the bottom of the wagons**, from the **same alphabet family as the runes from the ruins**. The DM provided a rough translation:
- Coherent concepts: *"sleep," "return," "catalyst," "invite"*
- Noise in the signal: *"dinosaur cheesecake," "banana donkey"* (the language has never been deciphered; things get lost in translation)
- **Two distinct rune sets** exist: the ones on doors/windows (Session 8) and these ones on the wagon bottoms specifically

Mira rolled History (9) for additional insight — too low for specifics, just the general sense of meaning.

### Scene 11 — End of Session

**Time in-game:** Still morning — not even lunchtime yet.

The party discussed next steps:
- Mira wants to visit where the caravan camped the night before in the woods
- George/Grungrak speculated aloud: *"I think there is someone or something making goop constructs in the woods."*
- One player asked quietly (out of character): *"Is this the Nightmare King?"*

Session ended at approximately **3:28 real-time** for Hero Forge character creation.

## Lore & Worldbuilding Discoveries

- **Salt is "White Gold"** — only available in 2 places in the world. Surface oceans are freshwater. X-salent's proximity to saltwater falls makes it exceptional.
- **Velodorn** — a city that appeared in the world 5 months ago. Its caravan's goods were coated in the mystery goop upon crossing X-salent's gate.
- **The goop substance is unified** — the creature's body, the wall writing, the caravan goods, and the cave cave substance are chemically identical. Salt causes all of them to coagulate.
- **The creature** — a goop construct built primarily as a digestive system. Not undead, not a fiend, not a celestial. New construct forming muscles. Was invisible before it consumed the corpse. Extremely averse to light/fire. Bite drains spell slots.
- **The creature was tracking its target** — the dead body matched the missing-person description. The creature was not random.
- **Wagon runes** — runes found on the bottoms of the Velodorn caravan wagons. Same alphabet family as the runes from the ruins from earlier sessions. Rough translation: sleep, return, catalyst, invite. Possibly: the wagon crossing the gate triggered the Transmutation effect because it was marked with these specific runes.
- **There are two distinct rune sets**: the ones on building doors/windows (Sessions 7-8) and the ones on the wagon bottoms. Same language family, different placement.
- **The goop absorbs into the ground** — the creature's remains slowly melted into the earth. It doesn't leave a permanent trace.
