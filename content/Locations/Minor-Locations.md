# Minor Locations

A reference table for smaller or transitional locations that don't warrant their own full wiki page.

---

| Name | Type | First Visited | Status | Notable For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The Tavern** *(Session 1 meeting point)* | Tavern / social space | Session 1 | Left | Where all five party members first met. George introduced himself as "George" to Mira. Inquizo sold all the pillows and stole a rock. Name of the town never established. |
| **The Beach** *(Pmurt's Kingdom shore)* | Coastal beach / two sections | Session 1 | Left | The party woke up shipwrecked here. Two beaches connected around a cliff corner — the party didn't know that in Session 1. The pirate captain issued her "submarine or die" ultimatum at the second beach. |
| **The Submarine** *(Pirate Captain's vessel)* | Metal submarine/aircraft hybrid | Session 2 | Docked at underwater city — likely left behind | The pirate captain's transport; pre-programmed to auto-dock at the underwater city. Reficul got locked outside in shark form. Grungrak nearly drowned. Button 61 unlocked a back room with gear. The DO NOT TOUCH pirate hat came from here — Inquizo never actually took it; the DM keeps flagging it. |
| **The Holding Room** *(Underwater City)* | Prison cell with a view | Session 2 | Left | Where Reficul was imprisoned after being caught in shark form. Site of his long philosophical conversation with Sisyphus through the window. |
| **The Throne Room** *(Underwater City)* | Castle throne room | Session 2 | Left | Top floor of the underwater city castle; two seahorse guards; site of the full combat with Queen Ariel in Session 3. Ariel charmed half the party; Reficul died; Ariel was killed; the silence enchantment lifted. |
| **The Upper City of the Underworld** | Underworld civic level / urban | Session 6 | Left | Accessible via elevator from the Shrine of Persephone level. Skyscrapers with trees wrapped around them. Roads split between asphalt and flowing water (boat traffic like Venice). The Gate of Ivory is located here. The Psychopomp (Charon) docks here. Session 6 ended mid-boat with Urscylla surfacing. |
| **Velodorn Caravan Camping Site** | Forest campsite | Not yet visited | Planned — not yet visited | Where the Velodorn caravan camped the night before their goods were gooped at X-salent's gate. Goods were unaffected at camp; gooped on arrival. Party suspects the wagon runes may have been added here, or the creature followed the caravan from this site. |
| **Velodorn** | Unknown city | Not yet visited | Unknown location | A city that appeared approximately 5 months ago — same time as the world-breaking Shattering event. Origin city of the gooped caravan. Wagon bottoms were marked with ruins-alphabet runes. What is it? Who built it? Is it connected to Malafera, the goop constructs, or the ruins deity? |
