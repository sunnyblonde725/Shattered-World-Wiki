# Buttons Inquizo Has Pressed

*He sees a button. The button gets pressed. This is not negotiable.*

---

**Total buttons pressed / controls activated: 10** *(confirmed; miscellaneous panel buttons counted as one entry)*

---

Inquizo grabbed it the way he grabs everything — without asking what it was first — and accidentally turned back time. |

## Notes

**On Button 61 specifically:** This is the one where the number is known. Every other button was pressed with the same energy as pressing button 61 — the number just wasn't announced. Button 61 is the Platonic ideal of an Inquizo button press: specific, numbered, no reading of labels, consequence discovered retroactively.

**On the submarine panel broadly:** Mira found the user manual sitting on top of the buttons. She began labeling each one with parchment and quill. Inquizo did not consult the manual. He did not consult the labels after they were applied. He does not consult signs. He cannot read. (He can read. He just won't.)

**On the airship button:** The AI said "nice crown." Inquizo kept the crown. He is now considered, by a computer, to be King Pmurt. He has not corrected this.

**The DO NOT TOUCH hat** — the one behind the gear room door, which button 61 revealed — he has never pressed a button on it because he has never taken it. The DM has flagged it multiple times. It remains unclaimed. Something will happen with it.
