# A Shattered World — Campaign Summary

*The short version for someone who wants to know what's going on.*

---

## The Setup

Two worlds collided. Not metaphorically — literally. A cosmic event called **The Shattering** tore reality and smashed two different worlds together. People, places, histories, and gods from both worlds now occupy the same broken space. Some people ended up merged with others. Some gods went silent. Some places just... don't make sense anymore.

Nobody fully understands what happened or why. The party is trying to figure it out before someone — or something — makes it permanent.

---

## The Party

**Halcyra** *(played by Sunny/Lorenne)* — A paladin. Daughter of Apollo, though she doesn't know that yet. She holds a prophecy scroll that seems to be predicting events in real time. She recently had some kind of rash treated and has been watching the prophecy come true line by line.

**George / Grungrak** *(Cooper)* — A dwarf bard who goes by both names. He saw something in a statue that clearly meant something to him personally and hasn't talked about it.

**Reficul** *(Ian)* — A changeling bard who can look like anyone. He stayed behind in a city they recently left to help displaced people resettle. The party is currently four instead of five because of this.

**Inquizo** *(Charlie)* — A kobold rogue. The chaos engine of the group. He recently threw a glowing blue clock off a bridge, turned the sky red, and accidentally acquired a hamster. He now has ten jars of fire ants.

**Mira** *(Ele)* — A warlock, though she doesn't fully understand what that means yet. Her father sold his soul to save her life when she was five. The entity that made that deal has been speaking through her ever since. She recently got practical: she now has an alchemist's tool belt.

---

## What's Been Happening

The party has been tracking a man named **Numgis** — a manipulator who has been one step ahead of them the whole campaign. He's been setting up a system to unseal a **Nightmare God** who was imprisoned inside her own bad dreams. If he succeeds, the god's nightmares get unleashed on the waking world. The sleeping sickness spreading across the region is connected.

Along the way they've been uncovering the truth about five statues — gods whose faces were disguised by **Pmurt**. The party has now freed the faces from all five. One of those statues depicts a god from George's player's own world, and George recognized it but hasn't explained how.

They just arrived in **Alastair**, the capital city, after a wild train ride that involved being kidnapped, escaping from a wagon in cuffs, and surviving a stained glass dragon attack on a bridge (which, it turns out, was caused by Inquizo throwing a clock).

---

## Where It Ended Last Session

The party is standing on a platform at the edge of Alastair. The **Prophetess** — a figure the prophecy scroll has been pointing toward — is right there in front of them.

Standing between the party and her:
- A boy with **burnt skin and growing claws** who says *"you can't take her from me"*
- A knight in silver armor with a war-hammer engraved with trees and crystals

Two lines from the prophecy scroll came true *in real time* during this session. The scroll is not metaphorical. The sky turned red. A dragon made of stained glass attacked them. Both of those things were written on the scroll.

They're at a cliffhanger. Nobody knows what happens next.

---

## The Big Picture

Something or someone caused the Shattering on purpose. Numgis is working for — or with — the Nightmare God's shadow self, deliberately. There's a kindly old man figure who keeps appearing at the edges of every mystery and nobody knows whose side he's on. The prophecy scroll is becoming real. The gods are waking up.

The party is level 6 and in the capital city, about to have a conversation that could change everything.
