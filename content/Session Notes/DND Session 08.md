# Session 8 — The Writing on the Wall

## TL;DR

The morning after arriving at X-salent, the party is split and frayed. Reficul, Halcyra, and Inquizo spent all night searching for Mira; Grungrak passed out on stage with Penopolis. Halcyra visits Mira and shares the prophecy she's been following — the first real crack in her emotional armor. While exploring the slum district, Reficul spots a suspicious figure running through back alleys, telepathically invites them to a meeting, and then chases them into a crowd of homeless people, attacking them, triggering his curse to turn him into an Oni, and accidentally killing four people (all later revived by Mira and Grungrak). Separately, Mira and Grungrak investigate mysterious writing appearing inside people's homes — a dark, creature-focused poem written in an unidentified substance — and learn of a missing merchant who had been raving about a monster in the woods for months before disappearing. The session ends with a powerful reconciliation between Grungrak/George and Mira, and a promise: next session is Prophecy Day.

## Full Summary by Scene

### Scene 1 — Finding Mira (00:00–22:00)
Grungrak spent the previous night at the tavern drinking with Penopolis and passed out on stage. Reficul, Halcyra, and Inquizo spent all night searching the city for Mira. Inquizo's approach: going door-to-door collecting signatures on a petition for the river quest, accumulating signatures from multiple households across 10% of the city. The sun came up at 8am and they finally knocked on Mira's room.

Reficul used the river petition as a cover story; Mira (awake, not in good shape) told them to "just go away." Halcyra asked to come in. Mira said it was unlocked.

### Scene 2 — Halcyra and Mira's Room (22:00–30:00)
Halcyra entered alone. Mira was wallowing. Halcyra: "You okay?" Mira: "I will give you two guesses." Inquizo had his snout pressed to the door.

Halcyra told Mira she came because Mira is her friend. She then revealed the prophecy she's been following — mentioning that George feels like a big part of it, which is part of why she takes care of him. Mira said she couldn't help right now. Halcyra said they would need to go over it with the group eventually — and that George himself was the one who suggested coming to talk to Mira.

Mira asked to be left alone but said "come get me by end of tonight." Reficul cast Silent Image outside her door: a grandfather clock with a sticky note reading "You need time XOXO" and a smiling face.

### Scene 3 — The Slum District (30:00–01:00)
The trio walked into the poorer part of town. Numbers on doors got higher the further they went from the Guild Hall; buildings got closer together; green space disappeared. They found scattered handbills (the same river quest flyers) concentrated more heavily in the slums — "only poor people can help."

Reficul approached a street vendor selling tiny, crudely hand-carved protective totems for 3 gold each. Reficul bought one, then convinced the vendor to sign a contract where Reficul would serve as his lawyer and totem-seller for free (all profits going to the vendor). The vendor signed it, handed Reficul a "business card" (actually his room number slip), and walked away. Reficul now owns a useless piece of wood and is contractually an unpaid totem lawyer.

### Scene 4 — The Scream / Writing on the Wall (01:00–01:45)
Grungrak (at ~75% function, post-pickle-juice recovery) heard a distant scream from the richer part of town and booked it toward it. Mira (closer) also headed over, arriving shortly after.

They found a gathering around a wealthy Victorian-style mansion. A woman was sitting outside the iron fence, visibly freaked out. Guards were working the scene. She had woken up to find writing all over her bedroom wall.

A guard walked Grungrak and Mira (separately) through the scene. The writing said: **"We feed it. The shattered, the blessed, the defamed."** — written in huge two-foot letters in an unknown, dripping black substance.

Mira did two checks:
- **Investigation (14):** Found fine, kanji-like sigils on the outer window frame — written in the same substance as the wall text but in a completely different, much finer hand. These appeared to be magical.
- **Arcana (18):** Confirmed the sigils on the window frame are magical. The wall writing itself has **no arcane energy** in it. The sigils appear to be a different layer — potentially used to access the room.

Mira is not familiar with the script. She took an ink sample for analysis and noted the sigils for further research.

Grungrak nabbed the notebook the guard was using and copied down the full poem into his songbook.

### Scene 5 — The Full Poem (01:37–01:52)
The guard shared the complete accumulated text — the 12th writing in as many days — written in the same substance across different homes:

> *Take Carol, you sicken, do you outcast, do you ill?*
> *Take care all you sickened, you outcast, you ill.*
> *The nightmare surrounds us, its voice high and shrill.*
> *The dreamers, the doubters, the dead and the dim.*
> *The damaged, the drunkard shall all fall to them.*
> *The blessed shall wail, the vile take cheer,*
> *The creature it hungers and draws ever near.*
> *It scuttles, it slithers, it twists, and decays.*
> *The shattered, the splintered, the blessed and defamed.*
> *It lies in the wood, it lies past the gate,*
> *The monster that hunts us, it's lying in wait.*
> *The blood as it runs, the mind as it fades,*
> *We feed it, the shattered, the blessed, the defamed.*

The guard also shared a rumor: a merchant came to town months ago — after the world broke — claiming a monster was chasing them. They spread flyers (same ones the party has been seeing). Groups of adventurers went out to the woods and found nothing. The merchant became obsessive, raving that the creature was at the edge of the woods or waiting outside the gate. Nobody could find anything. The merchant refused any escort out of town and just... raved. About two weeks ago, they disappeared — rumor says they ran screaming into the woods.

**Description of the missing merchant:** Human, darker complexion (mixed Latino and Black), once wore very fine glasses now shattered. Came to the guild initially for trading. Appeared covered in mud and scratches when they first arrived.

### Scene 6 — Reficul Spots the Figure (01:52–02:25)
Halcyra (perception 17) spotted a figure running through the slum alleys about 50 feet away — moving deftly, running from the direction of the Guild Hall, ducking through tight back passages.

Reficul used telepathy (no line of sight required, same plane): **"Divinity needs your haste. Meet the tavern in two hours."**

Halcyra ran after them, rolling investigation (17) and following footprints into the heart of the slums where people lined the wall. Reficul found the same thing via Detect Magic (level 1 spell) and Survival (22): the figure had disguised themselves as a homeless person in a tattered gray blanket, sitting against the wall among others.

Reficul approached: "I will heal you." The figure: "I'm quite fine." Reficul tried to touch them to cast Starry Wisp (a tracking marker); they smacked his arm away: "Don't touch me." Reficul cast Words of Terror — the figure barely passed the wisdom save (coin flip). The figure kicked Reficul's feet out from under him. From the mud, Reficul cast Vicious Mockery + Psychic Blades: **12 psychic damage**. The figure clutched their head and the people around them rose to defend them.

Reficul reached for his rapier.

### Scene 7 — Chaos in the Slums (02:25–02:52)
- Halcyra cast **Calm Emotions** (20 ft sphere, DC 15 charisma save) — about half the crowd calmed down. The mysterious figure was not among them.
- The mysterious figure **teleported away** — disappearing completely, leaving blankets behind. Halcyra and Grungrak (perception nat 20 and 16) saw the figure disappear; Mira and Reficul did not.
- Grungrak tried to grapple Reficul (failed — still hungover; rolled 5 vs Reficul's 15).
- Mira smacked Reficul on the head with her stick.
- Reficul stood up and cast **Earth Tremor** (5 ft radius) — knocking the people directly around him prone. **4 homeless commoners dropped to 0 HP.**
- Reficul's devil's deal curse activated: **rolled wild transformation**, turned into an **Oni** (large, blue-haired, white-skinned, red-eyed giant).
- Halcyra cast **Command ("Approach")** on the Oni-Reficul — he failed the save and walked toward her.
- Grungrak cast **Spare the Dying** (cantrip) on two of the four people.
- Mira used **Healing Hands** and **Spare the Dying** on the others.
- All four people survived.
- Mira apologized to the community on behalf of the group. Gave them gold coins.
- As they left, a **Black dragonborn** (sitting against the wall, appeared to be connected to the group) grabbed Mira's arm briefly before letting go and sinking back. Mira took note of his appearance.

### Scene 8 — Mira Confronts Reficul (02:45–02:52)
Mira laid into Reficul: a person running through alleys doesn't make them a crime syndicate. The homeless community attacked him because he was verbally berating one of their own. "Have you considered that they were going to protect one of their community members from a stranger who was verbally berating him?" Reficul: "...And that's why I cast Spare the Dying." He was surprised by how brittle their bones were.

### Scene 9 — George and Mira Reconcile (03:00–03:15)
The emotional centerpiece of the session. **Mira** initiated the reconciliation — she pulled George aside for a quiet conversation and apologized first.

Mira's apology acknowledged:
- She had been too aggressive in trying to make him remember
- She cannot fault Halcyra and Reficul for the deception around Reficul's death — she wasn't ready to face it either
- Pretending Reficul was trapped was easier than facing the grief directly

George then opened up to her:
- He was there when Grungrak ran at the blue dragon and got eviscerated in a ball of light. The only things left were his butt and the hammer core.
- Grungrak told him to run. He listened. He has regretted that every single day since.
- Everyone he has ever loved is dead or might as well be.
- He lost one friend to a stupid sacrifice very recently — and he was not ready to handle Reficul's death in the palace either. Pretending Reficul was stuck in the ocean was easier than facing that he had let another person do the same thing he swore he'd never let happen again.
- He was not yet ready to accept that he had watched Reficul stab himself and dissolve into sludge.
- He acknowledges he has not treated Mira fairly, and that she deserves better.
- He has met Grungrak — in the Lethe river. "He was so kind. I can see it in his eyes. I see why he was your friend. I met him in a river. It was weird."
- He asked Mira to someday tell him "the tale of a jolly good fellow."
- He is still figuring out if part of who he is is Grungrak.

George said both he and Grungrak deserve to be treated as people — not extensions of grief or something to be fixed. Mira agreed.

They ended the conversation in a bear hug. George started crying again.

### Scene 10 — End of Session
Inquizo slipped away (Stealth 16) as soon as things got serious. Nobody noticed until later. He's on his own somewhere.

Session ended here — Charlie called it as a narratively satisfying stopping point.

**Next session:** Prophecy Day — the party will finally sit down and discuss Halcyra's prophecy together.

**Post-session notes:**
- Mira going to Alchemy Guild to analyze the ink sample and research the kanji-like sigils
- Grungrak going to get a new hammer at the blacksmith + return Penopolis's bowl
- Halcyra resting in Grungrak's room (exhausted, 48+ hours awake)
- Reficul still an Oni for some duration of his curse

## Lore & Worldbuilding Discoveries

- **The Writing Mystery:** A dark poem/creature-prophecy has been appearing inside people's homes in X-salent for weeks (12 instances in 12 days). Written in an unknown black substance. The wall text has no arcane energy; the accompanying sigils on windows are magical, in an unknown kanji-like script.
- **The Full Poem:** (See Scene 5 above) Describes a creature that "scuttles, slithers, twists, and decays," hungers, lies in the wood past the gate, and "feeds" on the shattered/blessed/defamed.
- **The Missing Merchant:** A human merchant (darker complexion, mixed Black/Latino, shattered fine glasses) arrived months ago raving about a monster in the woods. Groups found nothing. Merchant went mad, raving for weeks, then vanished ~2 weeks ago — reportedly ran screaming into the woods.
- **X-salent Geography:** Guild Hall is a horseshoe-shaped structure. Tavern and guild rooms are on different arms of the horseshoe. City extends from guild outward — wealthy homes close to guild, middle-class adventurer housing in the middle, slums along the outer wall. A river separates parts of the city. There's a whole city on the other side of the river.
- **The Oni / Reficul's Curse:** Reficul's devil's deal curse can transform him randomly into creatures (previously a shark; now an Oni). Physical/physiological abilities transfer but not magical ones. He can't turn into a dragon and breathe fire — he'd just look like a dragon and huff smoke.
- **The Prophecy (Halcyra's) — hinted:** Halcyra shared the existence of her prophecy with Mira; said she thinks George is "a big part of it" and has "felt right" since she started taking care of him. The full prophecy was not read aloud this session — that's next session (Prophecy Day).
- **The different prophecy copies:** DM noted there is a reason one copy doesn't have scribbles on it. The copies that are scratched out are scratched out for a reason — different people's copies differ.

## Cliffhangers & Unresolved Threads

- **Who was the mysterious figure?** They can run, disguise, and teleport. They received Reficul's telepathic message ("meet the tavern in two hours"). Will they show up?
- **What is the creature in the poem?** It's in the woods past the gate. Scuttles, slithers, twists, decays. The missing merchant saw it. What happened to the merchant?
- **The magical sigils on windows:** Mira has an ink sample and the sigil script. She plans to use Comprehend Languages and check the alchemy guild. What does the script say?
- **The Black dragonborn:** Why did he grab Mira's arm? What did he say/want?
- **Inquizo is missing.** Nobody knows where he went. He snuck off during the serious conversations.
- **Next session: Prophecy Day.** The full party (presumably including Mira) will sit down and discuss Halcyra's prophecy together.
- **Reficul as Oni:** How long does the transformation last? When does he turn back?
- **Grungrak's hammer core:** Mira offered to examine it with the ink. Can it be turned into a weapon? The staff keeps telling him to plant things.
- **The three quests:** The party has two guild quests + Mira's extra quest but hasn't formally discussed them yet.
