## Appearance & Personality

Nineteen years old. Started working in [[NPCs/King-Pmurt|Pmurt]]'s palace kitchen at age 14 — taken from childhood. His family is dead. His childhood friends in the kitchen are gone. He ended up on an execution platform because the crowd mistook him for one of Pmurt's loyalists; it was a childhood friend who grabbed him.

He has not had an easy life. He is cautiously trusting — the party freed him, and he is traveling with them, but there's a wariness that hasn't fully resolved yet.

---

## Backstory

He was a palace cook who cooked Pmurt's English breakfasts and overheard everything. This makes him, accidentally, one of the most informed people the party has encountered about [[NPCs/Durf-Numgis|Numgis]]'s operation.

Key intelligence from Dex: Numgis was told in his own dreams to free Yomi (the God of Dreams); Numgis was building a global network of "disciples of dreams" with Pmurt as the first disciple; the Doctor spoke to Numgis directly. None of this was information Dex understood — he just cooked breakfast and heard things.

[[Character Profiles/Halcyra|Halcyra]] confirmed that his recurring nightmares, which feel very real, are not ordinary dreams — they are accidental contact with the dream realm post-Shattering. He was in a dream tavern the night of a major world event. He doesn't know what this means.

---

## Relationship to the Party

Halcyra freed him from the execution platform using Invisibility. He provided significant intelligence about Numgis. He came aboard the airship. He is sleeping. The party doesn't fully know what he is yet — a traumatized kid, an accidental dream-realm contact, or something more.
