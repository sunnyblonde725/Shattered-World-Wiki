## Description

Picture Rito Village from Wind Waker, but with more complicated politics. Voxus is the home of kenku and other avian peoples — feathered, winged, living on structures built for beings who can fly. The chief of Voxus is Ravalli, an eagle king, and the power here runs through him. Gunj, the shadow therapist, lives and practices here. The air smells like salt and height. Classical kenku lore says they can't speak — in Voxus, post-Shattering, some can. The rules changed when the world broke.

---

## Significance

Voxus is where the campaign's psychological layer got its own dedicated session. The shadow therapy sequence in Session 12 gave the party — and the audience — the most direct look at every character's interior life so far. The Valu manifestation in Session 13 confirmed that a wind god is real and paying attention, that the Thorin statue mechanic works, and that someone cosmic is pleased with what the party is doing (enough to hand them long-distance communication tools).

Voxus also holds a darker thread: two days before the party arrived, a man with wire glasses was setting up a "sleep system" in Ravalli's nest. That man was almost certainly Numgis.
