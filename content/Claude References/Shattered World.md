## Campaign Basics

- **Setting:** The Shattered World — three DMs’ worlds have collided into one
- **Known Worlds:** SKRP (Charlie’s world), Planetos (Cooper’s world), Anthropocene (Ian’s world)
- **Overarching Goal:** Restore the shattered worlds and fulfill the prophecy
- **Prophecy:** Full text in [[Prophecy]]. Halcyra is the child of prophecy and the one fated to fuse the fractured fields — she does not know this. Each party member has stanzas dedicated to them. The ending requires the hero to surrender something irreplaceable. See [[Campaign Narrative]] for full annotated breakdown.
- **Previous Campaign:** Some characters (Grungrak, Mira) carry over from a previous campaign with existing history and relationships
