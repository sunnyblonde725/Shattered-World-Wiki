## Appearance & Personality

A fleshy and robotic hybrid. Bioluminescent bugs live in her eyes. She is connected to the airship's systems via pipes that run through her chest — she literally breathes for the ship. The fans on the ship run because she breathes. The runes on her body are Malafera's language: hello, goodbye, and "life" were the last words her mother taught her.

She can't feel pain anymore. She gets emotional, and when she does, the ship changes. When [[Character Profiles/Grungrak|Grungrak]] hugged her — carefully, around the pipes — she accepted it.

She is cautiously hopeful. She has been in that glass case for a long time.

---

## Backstory

Starglass was placed in the airship by [[NPCs/The-Doctor|The Doctor]], who modified her and fused her into the ship's systems. Her mother [[NPCs/Malafera|Malafera]] — creator of the rune alphabet and the constructs — taught her the rune language. The Doctor arrived, spoke to Malafera, caused fighting, and ultimately took Starglass. She was traded to [[NPCs/King-Pmurt|Pmurt]] along with the ship (Pmurt gave his crown in exchange).

Her brother is [[NPCs/Haki|Haki]]. He heard a voice carried on the wind telling them to fight back, and went north with the prophetess. *"He's all I have left of my mother."* She gave the party a glowing token to identify them to Haki — it stopped glowing when it left her hand.

She hesitated noticeably when the party mentioned Sypium.

---

## Relationship to the Party

[[Character Profiles/Halcyra|Halcyra]] found her, asked good questions, and established trust. [[Character Profiles/Inquizo|Inquizo]] pressed her button to free her from the case (which triggered a system reboot). [[Character Profiles/Mira|Mira]] recognized the runes on her body as Malafera's language. [[Character Profiles/Grungrak|Grungrak]] hugged her.

She is not a party member in the traditional sense — she is literally fused to the ship. But she is an ally, an intelligence source, and someone who has been through something and come out the other side still cautiously open to the world.
