## Description

Built into the ocean floor and somehow full of breathable air. Paths of pearly white and gold wind between buildings, past tidal pools where citizens lounge. Massive windows look out into the surrounding water — schools of fish, distant dark. At the center: a tall castle with a spiral staircase that climbs three floors to the throne room at the top. On the walls, everywhere: clams. They function as surveillance cameras. The city called them "Security Glabra." There is no privacy here.

It's beautiful in the way that only places run by absolute power can be: perfectly maintained, impeccably lit, and quietly terrifying if you read the fine print.

---

## Significance

A women-only society, formerly at war with winged beings (the "bird folk" — likely connected to Voxus). Queen Ariel ruled with a silencing enchantment over the whole population — no one below her could speak freely. The mermen (including Sisyphus, and the man who would become [[NPC Registry#Hamish|Hamish]]) were bound by magical contracts. The party killed Ariel, lifted the enchantment, named a freed merman, and then left. The city is now headless.
