## Appearance & Personality

Only one side of a complete form — one arm, a half-halo, nails in the right palm. It rose from ankle-deep blood in the underground (Hell) space. It had been collecting glass-shard memories of hundreds of lives from the island above, which it could use to rebuild itself mid-combat. It was not a mindless monster — it spoke with purpose, claimed [[Character Profiles/Mira|Mira]] specifically, and whispered a name to [[NPCs/Arcus|Arcus]] that enraptured him.

It called itself number 8. It said it was not alone.

---

## Backstory

Unknown origin. It was waiting underground. It had been gathering and cataloguing the glass-memory fragments of hundreds of people from above — the island's history, or what remained of it. During combat, it used those memories as armor, reassembling itself from collected lives.

It claimed Mira: *"You're already mine."* It whispered "Bina" (capital B, Infernal — a name, not a word) to [[NPCs/Arcus|Arcus]], which triggered an enrapture response and transformation in him. When near death, it was shown [[Character Profiles/Mira|Mira]]'s reflection from during her illness — something about that image caused it to speak to her directly and then self-destruct. In doing so, it stole her memories of the time before her sickness.

The blood angel called its king/creator/father "K.L.I.S." / "K-Less" / "Kaelas" — possibly the same entity as Caelus (the sky deity who divorced Cybele and is the blood angel's father). The entity imprisoned in the black orb is NOT Caelus (Ian confirmed), so K.L.I.S. may refer to a separate, still-unknown figure.

---

## Relationship to the Party

Hostile. Fought the entire party. Specifically claimed [[Character Profiles/Mira|Mira]]. Specifically targeted [[NPCs/Arcus|Arcus]] with the Bina whisper. Stole Mira's pre-sickness memories on self-destruct. Its destruction left open wounds in the party that haven't healed.
