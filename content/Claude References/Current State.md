## Key Revelations (Session 13)

- **Kenku gave their voices to a god** — In-world explanation for classical kenku lore; Voxus kenku can speak because post-Shattering rules differ
- **Valu / Aeolus is the wind god** — Connected to the Thorin statue; briefly manifested; gave out sending stones
- **The rune alphabet is Malafera's language** — Hello, goodbye, and "life" — the circles-and-clouds writing seen since Session 8 belongs to Starglass's mother
- **Starglass is the ship** — The airship "Starglass" is named after the entity inside it; she literally breathes for the ship
- **The Doctor traded the ship to Mert** — Mert gave his crown; received the ship (with Starglass inside). The Doctor spoke to Nungus/Derv.
- **The Doctor was "a different man underneath his skin"** — Possible shapeshifter, possessed person, or dual identity
- **Nungus's network is about spreading dream-power, not loyalty** — Disciples of dreams = nodes of dream-power across the fractured world; Mert was #1
- **Yomi is imprisoned** — Confirmed again; Nungus was told in dreams to free her; also separately trying to free a second unnamed "him"
- **Alias is beached and wounded** — The pirate captain from the campaign's beginning is in danger
- **Haki and the prophetess** — Starglass's brother is protecting a small glowing girl in all white, heading north; the prophetess is supposedly universally known but unnamed
- **Second glass case on the ship** — Under a tapestry showing sun/rays/wings/sheep; foggy blue glow; not opened
