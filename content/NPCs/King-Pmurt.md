## Appearance & Personality

Pmurt is a large, heavy man — too large and too stubborn to walk on his own two feet. He travels everywhere by floating chair. His castle is ostentatious even by royal standards: gold-plated decorations, a town square with four god-statues, and a throne room complete with a chandelier and enough guards to fill a theater. He rules with the casual cruelty of someone who has never been told no and deeply resents anyone who tries.

His personality is summed up by his list of complaints: fish, birds, and people being annoying. He is the absolute monarch of a kingdom that officially believes only humans exist and only their kingdom matters. He is also obsessed with the English breakfast to a degree that his palace chef described as almost theological.

---

## Backstory

Pmurt has ruled his isolated coastal-cliff island kingdom for long enough that its entire worldview has warped to match his preferences. The kingdom has no concept of other races, other civilizations, or other gods — only Pmurt's town square statues and Pmurt's crown.

Somewhere between Session 1 and Session 11, he entered into partnership with [[NPCs/Durf-Numgis|Durf Numgis]], the Nightmare prophet. Under their joint rule, the island city became a demonstration of what Pmurt's power really looks like: bodies displayed on portcullises as warnings, a prison building packed with women, birds, and mermaids, and Numgis communicating policy via a waterfall-and-light projection system.

He was enrolled as Disciple #1 in Numgis's global dream-network — "the disciples of dreams" are meant to be nodes of dream-power across the fractured world. He received the airship from [[NPCs/The-Doctor|The Doctor]] in exchange for his crown. He had been planning to fly north to Sypium before the party showed up and derailed everything.

## Relationship to the Party

The party has dealt with Pmurt twice. In Session 1, he received them dismissively in his throne room and sent them off to Numgis. In Session 11, they came back to fight him.

He was interrogated under Zone of Truth. He summoned a phantasmal spectator as a Phase 2 combat move. He was dealt approximately 250 total damage. [[Character Profiles/Mira|Mira]] hit him with her pact sickle at a particularly sensitive moment before he triggered his contingency mechanic and vanished.

[[Character Profiles/Inquizo|Inquizo]] is now wearing his crown, which tricks the airship AI into greeting Inquizo as "King Pmurt." Pmurt does not know this yet. When he finds out, it will not improve his mood.
