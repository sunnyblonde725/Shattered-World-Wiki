# Larkin's Observational Report — Jolon Lozen

*Luminary of Mechanical Regulation, Royal Council of Aerolon. Year 1718.*

---

**Subject:** Jolon Lozen
**Occupation:** Chief Medical Theorist, Founder of the Lozenlind Advancement Circle
**Affiliation:** Rayen Chapel, Independent Herbal Consortiums

---

Jolon Lozen is, without exaggeration, one of the most formidable medical intellects presently active within Aerolon.

Operating primarily out of **Lozenlind**, Lozen has devoted his career to the study of herbological acceleration of human adaptation. Unlike traditional physicians, who seek to restore the body to its original state, Lozen's work is predicated on a more radical premise:

*That the human form is not fixed. That it can and should be improved.*

Through careful cultivation of rare plant compounds and controlled alchemical refinement, Lozen has developed a series of ingestible formulations designed to stimulate latent biological potential. The most widely distributed of these is a product known as **Kinlind**.

Publicly, Kinlind is marketed as a wellness supplement.

Privately, it is something far more ambitious.

---

Preliminary observations suggest that prolonged exposure to Kinlind results in subtle but measurable changes:

- Increased cellular regeneration rates
- Enhanced sensory acuity
- Improved resistance to environmental stressors

In isolated cases, more pronounced adaptations have been reported, though documentation remains inconsistent.

Lozen himself refers to these outcomes not as side effects, but as **progression**.

His stated goal is nothing less than **the guided evolution of humanity** — achieved not through mechanical augmentation, but through the refinement of nature's own mechanisms.

This places him in philosophical contrast to many of Aerolon's leading engineers. Where they seek to elevate humanity through external systems, Lozen seeks to transform humanity from within.

---

The implications are significant.

Unlike mechanical innovation, which can be regulated, distributed, or revoked, **biological change, once introduced, cannot easily be undone.**

Kinlind has already begun circulating beyond Lozenlind, finding its way into Quimgandala's agricultural workforce and even certain military circles seeking performance enhancement.

This widespread adoption raises several concerns:

- **Lack of Standardization:** Dosage and purity vary between batches distributed outside official channels.
- **Long-Term Effects:** Insufficient longitudinal data exists to determine the full consequences of sustained use.
- **Sociological Impact:** The emergence of a biologically "enhanced" population may introduce new forms of class division.

---

It must be stated that Lozen does not appear reckless.

On the contrary, his work is conducted with meticulous care and a clear internal logic. He believes, quite sincerely, that he is preparing humanity for a future in which adaptation is no longer optional.

What remains unclear is what future he is preparing them for.

There is a quiet intensity to the man that I find difficult to categorize. He speaks not with the arrogance of one who seeks recognition, but with the certainty of one who believes the outcome of his work is inevitable.

Such certainty has, historically, been both the engine of progress and the prelude to catastrophe.

I recommend continued observation of Kinlind distribution networks and further inquiry into Lozen's private research archives.

If his theories prove correct, he may redefine the limits of the human condition.

If they do not, the consequences may already be irreversible.

*— Dr. Alistair Larkin*
*Year 1718*
