## Description

An old bear den, about 35 feet wide, smelling like damp earth and something else — something chemical and wrong. The trail leading here was breadcrumbed by accident: hardened candle wax dripped along the path, broken branches, and burned-out torches stuck in the ground like someone was trying to find their way back and failing. Inside when the party arrived: eight torches (seven burned out, one still lit), the body of the missing merchant, and an open journal on the ground. The creature that killed him materialized from the walls.

---

## Significance

The cave is where the writing mystery from X-salent got its first concrete answer — and immediately raised five more questions. The goop creature wasn't an animal. It was a construct, deliberately built to consume. It was not celestial, fiend, or undead. Someone made it. The missing merchant saw something he wasn't supposed to see, which is why he came out here, which is why he's dead.

[[Character Profiles/Mira|Mira]] recovered his journal. Its contents will be revealed later.
