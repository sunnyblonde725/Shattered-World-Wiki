# NPCs

Everyone the party has encountered — gods, monsters, allies, enemies, and the people who don't fit neatly into any of those categories.

---

## Allies & Friendly Faces

| NPC | Who They Are |
|-----|-------------|
| [[Starglass]] | A fleshy-robotic construct who literally breathes for the airship — still traveling north, still hoping. |
| [[Penopolis]] | A sentient pine cone bartender and unlikely philosopher who grounds the party with warmth. |
| [[Justin]] | The eternally unfazed receptionist at X-salent's Adventurers' Guild, caffeinated into enlightenment. |
| [[Reverie]] | An underground resistance member who helped the party infiltrate King Pmurt's palace. |
| [[Haki]] | Starglass's brother — a free construct heading north alongside The Prophetess. |
| [[The-Horse\|The Horse]] | A metal horse descended from Poseidon, carrying a blade and granting Athletics advantage to Halcyra. |
| [[Charon-Karen\|Charon (Karen)]] | The underworld's ferryman, now amnesiac after Grungrak tackled him into the River Lethe. |

## Antagonists & Threats

| NPC | Who They Are |
|-----|-------------|
| [[King-Pmurt\|King Pmurt]] | A large, floating, petulant king — badly wounded and furious after the party's raid on his kingdom. |
| [[The-Blood-Angel\|The Blood Angel]] | A number-8 entity risen from Hell's blood, who claimed Mira and stole her pre-sickness memories before self-destructing. |
| [[Durf-Numgis\|Durf Numgis]] | A Nightmare prophet building a global network of dream disciples to unseal the God of Dreams on his own terms. |
| [[Queen-Ariel\|Queen Ariel]] | A tyrannical underwater queen — her death lifted a silencing enchantment and left her city headless. *(deceased)* |
| [[Naamon]] | A pompous opportunist who seized power overnight and was punched to death by Reficul within a day. *(deceased)* |
