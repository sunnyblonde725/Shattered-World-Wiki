## Description

A marble staircase leads down, dead hybrid seeds — willow and grain — scattered across every step. The space below is vast and quietly sacred. Two hundred and forty-seven sheep. A stone altar holding a statue carved from petrified wood, the clothing rendered in flowing red and blue mosaic — Cybele, or Avani, depending on who you ask. A newer stone cairn beside the altar held the sickle. Until they visited, a magical heartbeat pulsed through the whole chamber. The moment Arcus activated the sickle's clockwork mechanism, it stopped.

---

## Significance

This place is the hinge between the world above and Hell below. The statue of Cybele/Avani (goddess of agriculture, grain, and earth) stands here as the last faithful remnant of the agricultural agreement that once governed the crops and the children. The sickle "Castrator of the Sky" — an artifact with a name that does exactly what it sounds like — rested in the cairn. Arcus's handwritten prayer is on the altar base. He knew this place. He may have built it.

Lorya's secret tunnel, accessed through or near this temple, is the literal entrance to the underworld.
