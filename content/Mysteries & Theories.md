## Active Right Now

*The most pressing open questions — things happening at this moment in the story.*

---

**The Prophetess and the monster-boy**
Session 16 ended with the party on a platform in Alastair, the Prophetess in front of them, and two figures standing in the way: a boy with burnt skin and growing claws who said *"you can't take her from me,"* and a knight in silver armor with a war-hammer engraved with trees and crystals. Who is the monster-boy? Why is he protecting her? And why does keeping her feel personal to him?

> *Current theory: The monster-boy is Haki — Starglass's brother, who went north specifically to protect the Prophetess. The description matches someone mid-transformation. Something happened to him on the way.*

---

**The Alastair clock tower**
A glowing blue clock tower is pulling pieces of the capital city toward it. Shadow tendrils. The city is coming apart at the seams. OOC confirmed: Numgis went to the capital and created this. Breaking clocks makes it worse — Inquizo already learned that the hard way. The clock tower is the source, but no one knows how to stop it without making things worse.

---

**The sleeping sickness**
An albino man with a dreamcatcher vest was trailing elephant-creatures through Eustace. The sleeping gas that knocked out the party was connected to him. Is the sickness spreading? Is Eustace still afflicted? Is this Numgis's work, or the Nightmare God's, or something else entirely?

## The Party

*Personal mysteries — things each character doesn't know about themselves.*

---

**Halcyra doesn't know who she is**
She is the daughter of Apollo and the child of a prophecy that has been predicting real events in real time. She has been carrying that prophecy since she was fifteen without connecting it to herself. She has been searching for the "chosen hero" it describes. She is the chosen hero. Apollo called her name in a dream and she turned toward it without knowing whose voice it was. Valu named Apollo as the god of prophecy and she was standing right there.

When does this converge? And when it does — what does she do with it?

> *The scroll is not metaphorical. Two lines came true in Session 16 alone. Something is accelerating.*

---

**George's amnesia may not have been an accident**
He doesn't know who he is. He was once Grungrak — a miner from a mountain town, someone Mira called her best friend. At some point he forgot. The prophecy says: *"A mind broken by design / Holds the rites of the divine / Memories tangled, hidden from time."* By design. Someone may have broken his mind on purpose. He holds divine knowledge without knowing it.

He also ate pomegranate seeds in Persephone's underworld. Multiple seeds. The DM knows what that means mythologically. George does not.

> *Theory: the amnesia is connected to whatever is inside the broken hammer. The core was stolen by Inquizo. The shards are with George. Something about having them separated is keeping him from remembering.*

---

**Mira is dead**
She doesn't know. She was a character from a previous campaign who was reinvigorated by an unknown force and dropped back into the story. In the pit area she moved at 1/15th her normal speed, heard a voice say *"you're not supposed to be here,"* and had her blood tugged toward something. She has been walking around being the most competent person in the room while technically not existing.

What reinvigorated her? What happens when she finds out?

---

**Mira's patron is a dragon, and her father paid for it**
When she was five, her father signed a contract: *My soul for hers.* The entity that made that deal has been speaking through her in a masculine voice, manifesting a sickle in her hand, taking over her body during death magic. In the shadow dream, the patron appeared as a blue dragon with green veins. Grungrak was inside it. She walked into the dragon rather than fighting it.

Her father's soul is somewhere in that arrangement. Is he alive? Is he in hell? Does the dragon hold him?

---

**Reficul's devil's deal**
He made a deal with the King of Hell. He cannot die until a job is complete. He is 1/100th of the way there after Session 7. He would rather not talk about it. What is the job? What does "done" look like — peace, or death?

He also has a chest of his clothes that was already waiting for him inside Persephone's shrine before he arrived. Someone knew he would be there. Who?

---

**Inquizo's sister**
During the shadow therapy, his shadow showed him his sister's silhouette in his path and he ran through it without stopping. The shadow called it failing her again. Is she alive? Is she someone the party has already met without knowing it? Is she already part of the story?

---

**The black orb**
Inquizo is carrying a trapped entity in his backpack. Its identity is unconfirmed — NOT Caelus (Ian clarified this explicitly). Who is it? Why is it in an orb? Does it know Inquizo has it?

## The Villains

---

**Who is Numgis?**
He has been one step ahead of the party the entire campaign. He calls himself the Nightmare prophet. He spoke with the God of Dreams's nightmare directly. He went north before the party arrived at the island city, traveled to Alastair, and created the clock tower problem. He also reportedly needs to free "him" — a second imprisoned entity, separate from Yomi (the God of Dreams). Who is Numgis working for? Is he a true believer in the Nightmare, or is he using it for his own ends?

> *Theory: Numgis is not a servant of the Nightmare God — he is trying to control her. The unsealing is on his terms, not hers.*

---

**Who is the Doctor?**
He modified Starglass, wired her into an airship as its breathing mechanism, then traded the airship to Pmurt. His dossier shifts mid-document into Malafera's rune alphabet. Starglass said he is "a different man underneath his skin." Galveston had an insect fused into his eye — Mind Spike dislodged it. If the Doctor uses insect parasites to control people, is he also controlled? Is "the Doctor" even a single person?

> *Theory: The Doctor is a host body operated by something else — the insect (or what the insect represents) is the real Doctor. Multiple bodies, one entity.*

---

**Pmurt is alive and coming for revenge**
He escaped via contingency poof after 250+ damage and a partial castration. He is wounded, humiliated, and very personally motivated. He knows the party's faces. He had infrastructure across multiple cities. He will not forget what happened in that throne room.

---

**Malafera**
Starglass's mother. She created the rune alphabet, the goop constructs, and at least two children (Starglass and Haki). The Doctor's forces attacked her. She ran, was taken, or is in hiding. Where is she? Is she still alive? And who is her sister — the one the Deep entity called the one who "breathes war"?

## The Gods

---

**The Nightmare God and the unsealing**
The God of Dreams is female. She was sealed inside her own nightmare — by her own shadow. Numgis is working with that nightmare to unseal her, presumably in a way that benefits him. The unsealing involves something in the next town the party was heading toward. The shadow therapist Gunj believes it doesn't require destroying the town, but his chief disagrees. Either way: if Numgis unseals her on his terms, the God of Dreams wakes up and Numgis is the one holding the leash.

---

**Apollo and the prophecy**
Apollo is the god of prophecy in this campaign's cosmology. He is Halcyra's father. He is "an asshole," per Valu. He is also reaching toward Halcyra — his voice called her name in a dream, described as familiar and kind. The scroll she's carried since fifteen was likely written or overseen by him. There are beings even above Apollo that Valu doesn't fully know.

What does Apollo want? Why hasn't he told her who she is?

---

**The five statues**
Five god-statues were created by Pmurt's kingdom — their faces disguised to hide who they were. The party has freed them all.

| Statue | God | Status |
|--------|-----|--------|
| Kenku statue | Valu / Aeolus (wind god) | Freed — returned to his people in Voxus |
| Weeping statue | Thorin | With the party — heading north to Sypium |
| "God of the Seas" | Alias | Statue location unknown; Alias is beached and wounded |
| "Poppa Poseidon" | Unknown sea god | Last known: the pirate captain was hired to retrieve it from an island tribe — status unknown |
| 5th statue | A kindly old man with an open book and play/pause insignia | Cooper (George's player) identified this as a god from his own world — not George's in-game backstory |

The 5th statue is the newest mystery. Who is this god? Why does a god from Cooper/George's world have a statue here?

---

**Alias**
Alias is a sea god from SKRP — not the pirate captain from Sessions 1–2. She is beached on her own ship, wounded, leaking black ooze. She appeared in Halcyra's dream and charged at her screaming *"What are you doing here? You're not supposed to be here."* She knew Halcyra by a version of her name: "Hakira." Something is attacking her. Is it connected to her statue not having been returned to her people?

## The World

---

**The Shattering**
Two worlds collided. People, places, gods, and histories from multiple worlds now share the same broken space. The Shattering is not understood — nobody knows what caused it or whether it was intentional. But something or someone did this on purpose. The sleeping sickness, Numgis, the Nightmare God, the statues — they may all be downstream effects of a single deliberate act.

> *Theory: The Shattering was engineered to create the conditions for the Nightmare God's unsealing. It wasn't an accident.*

---

**The goop constructs and the writing**
Dark poem-like writing has appeared inside homes across multiple towns. A chemically identical substance was found in a caravan goop attack, in cave creatures, and on the walls of ruins. Runes from the same alphabet family appeared on the bottoms of merchant wagons. A rough translation: *sleep, return, catalyst, invite.* Someone is building goop creatures in the woods — purpose-built to consume, to carry, to mark. Malafera created the rune alphabet. But who is directing the constructs?

---

**The clocks and time distortion**
Three grandfather clocks have appeared across the campaign. Inquizo turned one back and reversed time. The mine ride caused a 10-minute trip to last two hours for someone waiting outside. The Alastair clock tower is now pulling the city apart. Something is actively wrong with time in this region. Is the Doctor responsible? Is it connected to Numgis? Is the Shattering itself causing temporal instability?

> *Mira identified the pattern in Session 15: first time reversed (Session 14 clock), then time moved forward (manor), then distortion at the mine. Something is escalating.*

---

**Kinlind**
The party consumed a supplement described as producing irreversible biological enhancement. The changes cannot be undone. Nothing has visibly manifested yet. Or has it, and they haven't noticed?
