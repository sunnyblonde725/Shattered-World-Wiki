## Appearance & Personality

A small girl clad in all white. She glows. That is the entire physical description.

[[NPCs/Starglass|Starglass]] says everyone knows her name — but Starglass herself was never told it. She is with [[NPCs/Haki|Haki]] heading north. The voice carried on the wind that told Haki and Starglass to fight back may be connected to her, or may be something else entirely.

---

## Relationship to the Party

The party knows she exists. That's it. She is heading toward the same destination they are.
