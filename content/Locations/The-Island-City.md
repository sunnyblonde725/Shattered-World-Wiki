## Description

An island city built for control. Two portcullises at the entry points, bodies hanging from both — a message, not an accident. A market district where ordinary commerce happens alongside ordinary brutality. A palace with enough guard layers to require a whole session of infiltration. A prison holding mostly women, birds, and mermaids. And underneath the town square, until recently: a statue of Thorin, buried under a platform that was built overnight.

This is what a city looks like when fear has been the civic infrastructure for a long time and then, suddenly, it isn't.

---

## Significance

Pmurt ruled this city with Numgis as his spiritual backer — the "Nightmare prophet" lending divine weight to a brutal regime. The party infiltrated the palace in Session 11, fought Pmurt (he escaped), and returned in Session 13 to find the city mid-revolution under a man named Naamon. Reficul killed Naamon and two others in public. He then stayed behind to try to help the former prisoners figure out what comes next. It is, as of the last session, a city with no leader, a lot of grievances, and one warlock/changeling trying to hold things together until backup arrives.
