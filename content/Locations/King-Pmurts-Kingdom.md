## Description

Picture a kingdom that genuinely believes it is the entire world. Gold-plated decorations on a castle that looms over a modest town square. Stone stairs climbing from a beach below — the only way in or out, as far as the locals know. At the center of civic life: four statues of gods, the kind of monuments that tell you everything about what a society worships and what it fears. It's small, it's proud, and it has absolutely no idea that anything else exists.

---

## Significance

This was the party's first real location after washing up shipwrecked — and it set the tone immediately. King Pmurt runs a society hermetically sealed from the outside world. Only humans live here. Only this kingdom exists. Only Pmurt rules. The party infiltrated the castle, charmed their way through, met the king and his advisor [[NPC Registry#Numgis|Numgis]], and then jumped off a balcony and stole the fishtail god-statue on their way out. Not subtle. But effective.

The fishtail statue was the first job in a longer chain — the pirate captain had sent them in specifically to retrieve it. What the kingdom's citizens made of the theft afterward is unknown.
