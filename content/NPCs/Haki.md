## Appearance & Personality

Never directly encountered. Known only through Starglass. He heard a large, loud voice "carried on the wind" telling him and Starglass that they were going to fight back. He was ready. He went.

[[NPCs/Starglass|Starglass]] gave the party a glowing token — it stopped glowing when it left her hand, but she says he will know not to hurt them if he's okay. That "if" is doing some work.

---

## Backstory

Brother of [[NPCs/Starglass|Starglass]]; son of [[NPCs/Malafera|Malafera]]. He was not captured or fused into the airship the way Starglass was — he is free, which means the Doctor either didn't find him or couldn't catch him. He departed to accompany the [[NPCs/The-Prophetess|prophetess]] — a small girl in all white who glows — heading north.

---

## Relationship to the Party

The party doesn't know him yet. They have a token from Starglass that (used to) glow and is meant to identify them to him. They are all heading north toward Sypium. They will probably meet.
