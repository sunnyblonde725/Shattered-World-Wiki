## What We Know

Alias is a sea god from Charlie's world, SKRP. She is **not** the pirate captain from Sessions 1–2 — those are two separate characters.

One of the four god-statues from Pmurt's kingdom is **Alias's statue** — called "God of the Seas." Like Valu, she was presumably imprisoned in it. Her statue's location is currently unknown. If the same mechanic applies, returning her statue to her people may be what frees her.

In Session 13, Ian flagged her out of character as a detail he almost forgot to mention. Alias is currently beached on her own ship, wounded, and actively fighting something invisible that keeps slamming her into walls. Her wounds are leaking black ooze. She was aware enough to throw a dagger at Halcyra through a dream connection — she wanted Halcyra out and not watching.

Her wounded/beached state may be connected to her statue not having been returned to her people yet.
