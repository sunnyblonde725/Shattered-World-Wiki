## Appearance & Personality

A manticore woman with multiple rows of sharp teeth. She wears a dark brown cloak with black spots — this cloak is functional: when wrapped around others, it allows them to pass through the magical barrier that protects the underground. Her most striking accessory is a necklace made from the remains of her family members. *"This is my mother... my grandfather... my brother."* She wears her grief literally.

Nails in her right hand cover a tattoo described as "biblically accurate angel wings." Like [[NPCs/Arcus|Arcus]], her right hand is modified or marked.

She is the more combat-forward of the two siblings — tactical, direct, and guided [[Character Profiles/Grungrak|Grungrak]] during the blood angel fight with specific cues: *"Swing right here."*

---

## Backstory

Daughter of Cybele/Avani (whose name neither sibling can speak aloud). She and her brother [[NPCs/Arcus|Arcus]] are described as "the last two reasonable ones" among their kin. When the party needed to reach the underground, Lorya led them through a secret tunnel and used her cloak to get them through the magical barrier.

[[NPCs/Arcus|Arcus]] gave her a bracelet or anklet at the end of Session 4, buying a few more weeks before the agricultural agreement in their region collapses.

---

## Relationship to the Party

Allied and active in the fight. She helped the party descend, guided them in combat, and wore her full grief on her body without apology. The party first encountered her as a terrifying manticore-shaped beast in Session 3; their relationship improved significantly from there.
