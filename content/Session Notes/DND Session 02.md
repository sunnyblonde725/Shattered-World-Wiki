# DND Session 2

*Linked to: [[Shattered World]] | [[Halcyra]] | [[Grungrak]] | [[Reficul]] | [[Inquizo]] | [[Mira]]*

---

## TL;DR

The party is forced into a pirate submarine and accidentally drives it to an underwater city ruled by a red-haired mermaid queen who is also a god — and just as they finish delivering their (very confusing) diplomatic report to her, she declares combat.

---

## Pre-Session Recap

The party started on a beach under pressure from a female pirate captain who gave them a simple choice: get in her submarine, or die. They chose the submarine, which immediately went sideways when [[Inquizo]] started pressing every button on the control panel. After flooding the vessel, opening the hatch onto the ocean floor, and turning [[Grungrak]] into a near-drowning incident, [[Reficul]] transformed into a bull shark to save him — and got locked outside in the process.

The submarine (pre-programmed) delivered them to an underwater city built mostly for women, where men are treated as lesser beings and winged creatures are enemies of the state. [[Halcyra]]'s wings made her a liability and she had to be hidden. [[Grungrak]] was left in the submarine for a nap. [[Reficul]] (still in shark form) was grabbed by guards and thrown in a holding room, where he spent the session telepathically trying to recruit a brainwashed merman guard to anarchy.

[[Mira]] and [[Inquizo]] navigated the city, talked to a puffer fish historian, and learned that a statue belonging to one of the city's gods had been stolen — and while the queen blames the bird folk, the men on the nearby island actually have it. They climbed to the throne room and delivered this information to the queen. She thanked them by announcing combat.

## Full Summary

### The Beach — A Choice Between Debt and Death

The session opened mid-scene on a beach. The party was already in the company of a female pirate captain who had no love for at least one of them. She offered assistance (access to the mountain, underwater travel equipment) but always at the cost of more debt. After some chaotic beachside hijinks — including [[Inquizo]] burying his head in the sand and nearly drowning, requiring [[Grungrak]] to yoink him out by the legs and launch him into the air — the pirate made her offer concrete: take the submarine (her dinghy) or die on the beach.

They took the submarine.

### The Submarine — Button Hell

The submarine was a metal aircraft/submarine hybrid. It had a full control panel, which [[Inquizo]] immediately began pressing buttons on at random, with no regard for consequences. The session became a comedy of errors:

- Pressing buttons turned off the lights (magical darkness), opened windows, activated windshield wipers on sealed windows, and opened both the top hatch (flooding the cabin) and the bottom hatch (through which [[Grungrak]] began to fall feet-first into the ocean).
- [[Mira]] found the user manual sitting on top of the buttons and began speed-reading it and labeling each button with parchment and quill.
- [[Reficul]] transformed into a bull shark to push [[Grungrak]] back up through the bottom hatch before it closed. He succeeded, but was then locked outside the submarine in shark form.
- On the way out, someone pressed button 61 — which unlocked the back door into a second room.

The back room was the pirate crew's quarters and gear storage. It contained flight suits, scuba diving equipment, and ornate pirate costumes (all sized for tall women). [[Inquizo]] immediately claimed a black pirate hat with a giant pink feather, despite the words "DO NOT TOUCH" inscribed on the inside brim — which he couldn't read. He now has three hats stacked: the forbidden pirate hat, his bard hat, and a tiny mystery hat that was already nested underneath his bard hat.

Someone gave [[Grungrak]] the pirate hat as a gift, along with a sash worn like a scarf. His resulting smile was described as "the most pure smile."

Outside, [[Reficul]] (bull shark) was joined in the water by the merman guard who had been knocking on the submarine. The merman was terrified of the shark, peed in the water, and accidentally communicated telepathically — which [[Reficul]] took advantage of immediately.

The submarine, now navigated using the user manual, arrived at its destination: an underwater city built into the ocean floor.

### The Underwater City — Politics and Disguises

The submarine docked by floating up into the underside of the city. The party emerged into an air-filled section with pearly white and gold paths, tidal pools where citizens relaxed, and windows looking out into the surrounding ocean. The city was populated almost entirely by women.

Key problems:
- **[[Halcyra]]** has wings. The city is at war with winged beings (the "bird folk"). Her wings made her a political target. She spent much of the session wrapped in a trench coat, passed off as [[Inquizo]]'s "exotic pet" or kept out of sight.
- **[[Grungrak]]** is a man. In this city, men are treated as lesser beings or enslaved. He was left in the submarine to nap and avoid incident.
- **[[Inquizo]]** is small, non-human, and of indeterminate gender presentation — he passed as a "quiet" (exotic pet/kobold). *("What are you supposed to be?" "A cobalt quiet." "Effectively a pet, truly.")*
- **[[Reficul]]** was grabbed by city guards while in shark form and thrown into a holding room with a window overlooking the water.

[[Reficul]] used the window to telepathically communicate with his assigned merman guard — a man he named "Sisyphus" after the guard couldn't remember his own name (brainwashed). Over the course of a very long, very philosophical conversation, [[Reficul]] learned:

- Mermen in this city were once regular men whose legs were cut off and replaced with fishtails against their will.
- They are brainwashed and forced to serve 300-year contracts, signed with fishbones they no longer remember signing.
- The ruler of the city is both a queen and a god. Her authority is apparently divine.
- Sisyphus has served 10 of his 300 years. One guard before him finished his contract and got his legs back.
- [[Reficul]] offered to be Sisyphus's lawyer, invoked the King of Hell as his legal authority, and declared the brainwashed contract "inhumane and illegal." Sisyphus politely declined to defect.

Meanwhile, [[Mira]] (with [[Inquizo]] on her head) navigated the city and found the historian — a puffer fish NPC — who shared crucial political information before going suspiciously quiet (possibly killed for it).

### What the Historian Revealed

- The city had a **statue of one of their gods stolen** within the last few weeks. They blame the bird folk.
- The statue is NOT with the bird folk. **The men on the island — between the sea and the mountain — have it.** [[Mira]] saw the statue during the party's time on the island, and its face had been replaced with the face of the island tribe's leader.
- The **pirate crew arrived around the same time the statue disappeared** — not a coincidence.
- **Clams on the walls are surveillance devices** ("Security Glabra"). [[Mira]] noticed this.
- The city has a complicated relationship with all neighboring powers: they hate the bird folk (at war), they hate the island men (sexist tribe), and the pirates are an unknown wildcard.

After the historian, the group climbed a spiral staircase through three floors of the castle and arrived at the throne room.

### The Throne Room — Diplomacy, Briefly

Two seahorse guards flanked a massive arched door. The group entered.

**The queen:** a beautiful, red-haired mermaid with a giant crown. Immediately compared to "Evil Ariel." She is apparently also a god — her authority overrides ordinary orders and she is described as "Judge, Jury, Executioner."

[[Mira]] handled most of the diplomacy, explaining:
- They were sent by the pirate captain (choice: submarine or die).
- They have information: there is a new tribe of humans on the island between the sea and the mountain, led by a "rightfully horrible, horrible man."
- That tribe has the stolen statue — and possibly others.
- The pirates seem to be running retrieval operations connected to the statues.

The queen listened. Then announced combat.

**Session ended before combat began** — it was almost midnight. Combat picks up next session.

## Character Moments & Development

**[[Halcyra]] (Lorenne/Sunny):**
Halcyra's wings were a source of constant tension in this session — the underwater city is at war with winged beings, making her a political liability. She adapted by using her wings as an umbrella to shield the group from the city's brightness, and spent the throne room approach either hidden in a trench coat or walking with eyes down behind [[Mira]]. She also revealed she has healing hands *(unconfirmed: possibly Paladin's Lay on Hands)* and used them on Grungrak after his nose-bonking incident.

**[[Grungrak]] (Cooper):**
Grungrak was this session's most chaotic presence — ironically by just existing. His amnesia led to a long comedy sequence around the castle's phallic tower (he doesn't know what a penis is). He was physically launched, nearly drowned (in two feet of water), got his head bonked into someone's knee being freed from a dive suit, and was left to nap in the submarine. He was also given a pirate hat as a gift and smiled his purest smile. New lore revealed: Grungrak was once a miner who lived in the mountain town.

**[[Reficul]] (Ian):**
Reficul's session was almost entirely in shark form and then in a holding cell, but his conversation with Sisyphus showed his full personality — philosophical, manipulative, charming, theatrical. He invoked the King of Hell as legal authority, offered anarchy as a lifestyle, tried to name the merman guard, and genuinely seemed to enjoy himself. His changeling nature confirmed: clothes shift with him during transformation.

**[[Inquizo]] (Charlie):**
Maximum chaos, minimum consequences. Inquizo pressed every button he could find, acquired three hats (one of which says DO NOT TOUCH), briefly piloted a dwarf from inside a diving helmet, was presented as a legitimate exotic pet, and squeaked at the city's citizens when they couldn't determine his gender. His illiteracy remains one of his most useful character traits. *(Confirmed: Inquizo can't read, and knows he can't read.)*

**[[Mira]] (Ele):**
Mira was the session's unsung hero. She found the user manual, labeled every button, handled all diplomacy, talked the group into a historian meeting, translated political information across cultural lines, and managed three chaotic travel companions while also hiding a winged Aasimar in a trench coat. She is chronically ill and has a strength modifier, but she's still the most competent person in the room.

## Lore & Worldbuilding Discoveries

- **The underwater city is at war with "bird folk"** (winged beings). This directly affects [[Halcyra]] who has wings.
- **The city's ruler is both a queen AND a god** — her authority overrides ordinary divine orders. She is described as "Judge, Jury, Executioner." Possibly named Lydlock *(unconfirmed, garbled in transcript)*.
- **Mermen guards were originally human men** whose legs were forcibly cut off and replaced with fishtails. They serve 300-year brainwashed contracts signed with fishbones. This is apparently legal in the city's framework.
- **A god-statue has been stolen from the city** — recently, within the last few weeks. The city blames the bird folk, but the men on the island between the sea and the mountain actually have it. The face was defaced and replaced with their leader's.
- **The pirates are connected to the statue situation** — they appeared around the same time the statue went missing, and their submarine was pre-programmed to dock at this city.
- **Clams on the walls are surveillance cameras** ("Security Glabra").
- **SKRP worldbuilding (Charlie's world):** The surface oceans of this world are freshwater. The saltwater oceans are secret/underground. This is relevant because [[Reficul]]'s bull shark form functioned fine in fresh water.
- **There is a tribe of humans on an island** between the sea and the mountain — led by a "rightfully horrible, horrible man." They hate both women and birds. The party encountered them previously.
- **The worlds colliding was unknown to the city** — they didn't know there was an island connected to the mountain above them.
