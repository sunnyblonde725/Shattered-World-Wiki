## Description

Here's what the party knows: Sypium is a city to the north. There are mountains near it. Somewhere in or near those mountains, people live in caves. The Doctor wrote about those cave people, and partway through his dossier the handwriting shifted to Malafera's rune alphabet and the tone shifted to something the party's notes describe only as *expressing violent hostility*. Numgis was heading there. Pmurt was originally heading there. Haki and a glowing girl in white are heading there. The God of Dreams unsealing mechanism is in or near there.

The party doesn't know what they're walking into. Everyone else in the story seems to.

---

## Significance

Sypium is the convergence point for everything the campaign has been building since Session 11. Multiple factions, multiple threats, and at least one imprisoned god's unsealing mechanism are all pointed at this location. The Doctor has expressed interest in the cave civilization near Sypium — and that interest is written in a language that belongs to Starglass's creator and sounds like a threat. Whether Sypium itself is a victim, a target, or something else entirely is unknown.

What is known: Numgis is ahead of the party, and the party's chief at Voxus believes reaching the unsealing mechanism requires destroying the town.
