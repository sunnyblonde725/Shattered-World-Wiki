## Appearance & Personality

Details are sparse. Reverie is described as a member of the resistance operating inside the island city against [[NPCs/King-Pmurt|King Pmurt]]'s rule. They helped the party plan and execute the palace infiltration, provided intel, and supported the mission from inside enemy territory. The resistance is small, and Pmurt's response to dissent has been public and brutal — bodies on portcullises, the prison building packed with rebels.

---

## Backstory

Reverie exists inside a city that displays its executions as policy. The fact that there is any organized resistance at all speaks to something in the people of that city that Pmurt's regime couldn't fully stamp out.

Allied. They helped the party get inside and helped them succeed. With [[Character Profiles/Reficul|Reficul]] staying behind in the city after Session 13, Reverie may become a key contact and ally for him as he tries to stabilize things.
