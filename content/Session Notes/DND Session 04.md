# A Shattered World — Session 4

*Linked to: [[Shattered World]] | [[World State]] | [[Halcyra]] | [[Grungrak]] | [[Reficul]] | [[Inquizo]] | [[Mira]]*

---

## TL;DR

The party explored an abandoned town, discovered the identities and backstory of their mysterious allies Just Me (real name: **Arcus**) and Naph/Reasonable (real name: **Lorya**), and learned they are the children of **Cybele/Avani** — a fertility goddess whose statue sits in the underground temple. The party descended into what may be Hell via a secret tunnel, where they fought a half-blood-angel entity in ankle-deep blood. The angel stole a piece of Mira's memory (her life before sickness — gone forever), and the session ended on a cliffhanger: **Phase Two of combat** picks up next session.

## Pre-Session Recap

The DM recapped Session 3: the kids were peeing off the submarine; Reficul died and went to hell; the party reached the field/pit area; Just Me walked into the crop field and descended underground. The DM clarified that "Just me" was always the character's nickname — Grungrak calls him "Justin."

## Full Summary

### The Abandoned Town

The party explored a cluster of rotting wooden buildings covered in fungus near the crop field. The buildings were empty and long since abandoned. Notable finds:

- **Murals on the outside:** A winged figure descending; nails digging into the ground and raising it up.
- **Interior — Nursery:** An unused crib. Murals of giant glowing horns and a black-winged angelic being. The children who should have been here were taken.
- **Jade fragment:** Inscription reads "Birth" in Infernal (9th-accent dialect).
- **Locked box:** Contained a topaz gem with a carving of a man and a Celestial inscription.
- **Fancy chest with fog:** Contained the black orb — which turned out to be a prison for an entity. *(Note: The entity inside the orb was later clarified not to be Caelus — Ian indicated it was someone else. Identity uncertain.)*

### Caelus and the Black Orb

Inquizo made contact with the entity inside the orb. The entity speaks in riddles and contradictions. It described the orb as "a terrible box without a blankie." Key details:

- It has been inside the orb "forever" and was given immortality by an entity called **Kailess/Kaelas**.
- Agents called "Agents of Kaelas" entrap minds. The Celestial inscription on the topaz carving references them: *"Agents of Kaelas, entrap the minds of the divorced."*
- Inquizo was pulled inside the orb and his consciousness became trapped there until sunrise (approximately 2 hours away in game time). His body walked around zombie-like; Grungrak carried it.

*(Note: The entity was described during session as Caelus, but Ian later clarified this was not correct — it is someone else. Identity unconfirmed.)*

### Arcus and Lorya: The True Names

The sickle revealed the real names of the party's two mysterious companions when Grungrak wielded it:

**Arcus** (formerly called "Just Me" / "Justin" by Grungrak):
- White and black long hair concealing brown horns, which glow when Grungrak sings.
- Nails in his right hand cover a serpent double-infinity symbol tattoo.
- Cannot speak his mother's name aloud due to a barrier/agreement.
- Made murals to remember his mother.
- He and Lorya are described as "the last two reasonable ones" in their group.
- His altar inscription read: *"Mother, I miss you. I pray that I will never have to use what you did to see you again. I'm sorry for costing so much blood for my existence."*
- It was implied that Arcus had previously wielded the sickle and descended into Hell — the sickle said: *"The last time someone like you went down there, they didn't come back up. Who wielded you then? My son. Arcus did."*

**Lorya** (formerly called "Naph" / "Reasonable" / the manticore):
- A manticore woman with multiple rows of sharp teeth.
- Nails in her right hand cover a "biblically accurate angel wings" tattoo.
- Wears a necklace made from the remains of family members ("this is my mother... my grandfather... my brother").
- Wears a dark brown cloak with black spots that allows others to pass through the magical barrier when wrapped in it.
- Helped the party reach the underground via a secret tunnel beneath the temple.

Both Arcus and Lorya confirmed their mother is **Cybele/Avani** — the goddess statue — by raising their right hands.

### The Temple and the Sickle

Beneath the abandoned field, a marble staircase led down to a stone temple with 247 sheep, a stone altar with the Cybele statue, and a newer stone cairn that held the sickle.

**Cybele/Avani's statue:** Wings cut off at the back, ankles, and wrists. Made of petrified wood clothing that looks like lava — red and blue mosaic. A fertility goddess.

**The Sickle — "Castrator of the Sky":**
- Silver/mirror-like blade with clockwork engravings and a sundial element.
- Inscription in Infernal (9th-accent dialect).
- The face of a woman is visible in the blade — same bone structure as the Cybele statue.
- The sickle is sentient. It speaks: *"I am Vengeance. Against the sky. Against love loss."* It described itself as "a piece of his mother put in here."
- When Grungrak grabbed it, he felt immediate bloodlust: eyes went red, muscles contracted. He made his Wisdom save, but the sickle's pull is persistent. It craves flesh.
- When Arcus twisted the clockwork mechanism on the sickle, the magical heartbeat pulsing through the temple (felt like a whale's heartbeat) stopped.

**The Celestial mural inscription** was revealed in three parts when Grungrak sang and the sheep's horns glowed.

### The Barrier and the Descent

The field above was protected by an illusion-type barrier: invisible spider-silk strands connecting the wings of winged beings to the sky, preventing descent into the underground. After Arcus activated the sickle:

- **Mira** was freed from the barrier and could move normally.
- **Halcyra** and **Inquizo** passed through by being wrapped in Lorya's cloak.

The party descended via Lorya's secret tunnel and fell into an underground space: ankle-deep liquid that turned out to be dark, viscous **blood** — and very likely a layer of Hell. The sickle had confirmed: *"Down" is Hell.* The party was now in another plane of reality.

Inquizo's orb was with Grungrak (Inquizo's zombie body was being carried). Combat was called.

### Combat — The Blood Angel

Rising from the blood was "a silhouette of half of an angel" — an entity that seemed to be only one side of a complete form. It had one working arm, a half-halo, and nails in its palm (right side only).

**Initiative order:** Inquizo → Grungrak → Halcyra → Mira → Angel → Arcus → Lorya

**Round 1:**
- **Inquizo:** Touched the blanket inside the orb-space, waking his consciousness. Grabbed the orb, wrapped it in spare clothes, put it in his backpack. Tried to go invisible — crit fail. Just his arm vanished.
- **Grungrak:** Charged forward dragging the sickle through the blood. Swung at the angel — missed. The sickle cut a fracture through reality like broken glass (no blue light inside). The cut passed straight through the middle of the angel without connecting.
- **Halcyra:** Skipped giving her normal pre-combat inspiration speech (Grungrak's bloodlust made him charge before she could).
- **Mira:** The angel pointed into the darkness, showing fragments of glass-shard memories: hundreds, maybe thousands of lives once lived on this island — people playing together, children growing old. Mira demanded to know why it had taken her memories. The angel responded cryptically, mentioning "K.L.I.S." as its king, creator, and father. When asked its name, it answered only: *"Underneath."*
- **Angel:** Threw a nail up into the air with one hand, caught the hammer with the same hand, and smacked the nail midair — it flew directly into Arcus's chest. The angel whispered *"Bina"* (capital B, an Infernal name) to Arcus. Arcus became immediately enraptured, surrounded by a blooming flower nest formed from the swirling blood.
- **Arcus:** Crit failed initiative. Trapped in the blood-bloom.
- **Lorya:** Guided Grungrak: "Swing right here." He swung. Hit — 12 damage (8+4 across two d8s). The sickle immobilized the angel momentarily, slashing off a leg.

**Round 2:**
- **Inquizo:** Dashed to reposition.
- **Grungrak:** Swung again — missed. The angel had fallen into the blood, making it hard to see where to strike. **Bonus action:** Called out to Arcus and cast Healing Word on him (+6 HP).
- **Halcyra:** Moved forward. Rolled a natural 20. 13 damage (d10, double dice on crit). Cleaved off the angel's other arm. The angel was now reduced to a torso, half a face, and a halo. As it fell splashing into the blood, it smiled.
- **Mira:** Cast Toll the Dead. She swung her staff in a half-circle through the blood, creating a wave. Rolled 11 damage (d12). — *The voice that came out of Mira was not her own.* A masculine voice emerged from her face. Her patron or some other entity spoke through her.
- **Angel:** The half-halo glowed crimson. A trail of gold extended from the Arcus blood-bloom toward the angel. The angel crawled to lean up — failed athletics. Veins, sinuses, and glass-shard memory fragments began reforming its missing half like a golem being built from the island's memories.
- **Arcus:** The blood-bloom flower opened fully, and Arcus transformed into something else: *"a massive fluffy sheep-like beast... with a shaved heart on its head that looks like the sun."* A small **green sheep** popped its head from the fluff. Arcus approached the angel with subservience (Insight: not hostility).
- **Lorya:** Guided Grungrak again. Another swing.

**Round 3:**
- **Inquizo:** Nat 20 perception. Spotted nails in the angel's palm (right side only). Saw the nail spike still embedded in the angel's chest/body. Decided to fly toward it to pull the nail out.
- **Grungrak:** Swung again — missed. The angel scoffed in his face. Glass memory-fragments continued rebuilding its form.
- **Mira:** Cast Mind Spike. The angel failed its Wisdom save. 9 damage. A piercing through where its lungs would be — it could barely breathe or speak. Still crawling at the ground.
- **Angel:** As the glass reformed around its face, Mira saw **half her own reflection** — her face as she was during her illness, years ago. The angel spoke: *"You're already mine. I have a number. It's 8. And I'm not alone."* Then — it took the hammer and nail and **spiked itself in the chest**. Its neck snapped back. Fell into the blood. The glass fragments forming its second half shattered and dispersed. The angel was dead.

**Aftermath:**
- **Arcus:** The light went out of his eyes. Something happened to him during the blood-bloom encounter. He is present but changed.
- **Mira:** She forgot something. The angel stole her memory of "when she wasn't sick" — the time before the illness. Gone permanently. (DM caveat: *"Except for the maybe hope of getting it back."*) This memory was from early childhood, so she may not have consciously held it anyway.

**Session ended here. Phase Two of combat begins next session.**

## Lore & Worldbuilding Discoveries

- **Arcus and Lorya are Cybele/Avani's children.** Both confirmed this by raising their right hands when the sickle asked.
- **The sickle is a piece of Cybele/Avani herself** — "a piece of his mother put in here." It is Vengeance against the sky and against love lost.
- **Arcus previously wielded the sickle and descended into Hell.** The sickle said the last person who went down didn't come back. His son. Arcus.
- **"Down" = Hell.** The underground space beneath the temple is Hell or a layer of it. The party passed through "more than just a hole."
- **There are ways out of this plane other than death:** The angel mentioned "passing through rifts in reality" as one option. Death as another.
- **The abandoned town's children were taken** because the seed cycle was broken — seeds stolen → field can't be replanted → creatures go hungry → take children. Arcus gave Lorya a piece of jewelry (bracelet/anklet impression) to "give a couple more weeks" before the agreement collapsed.
- **"Bina"** — An Infernal name (capital B) whispered by the blood angel into Arcus's ear. What it means and to whom it refers is unknown. History check of 4 wasn't enough to learn more.
- **K.L.I.S. / Kaelas** — The angel called its king/creator/father by this name. The orb entity was trapped by this same figure (or figure connected to it). They may be the same. *(Note: The orb entity's identity is uncertain — described as Caelus during the session but Ian later indicated otherwise.)*
- **The blood angel's memories** — The entity had collected glass-shard memories of hundreds of lives on this island. It was literally rebuilding itself from them. It was also only half of something — "half of an angel."
- **Mira's masculine-voiced patron** — When Mira cast Toll the Dead, a masculine voice came out instead of hers. Something else speaks through her when she draws on death magic.
- **The angel had a number: 8.** "I have a number. It's 8. And I'm not alone." Meaning unclear. There are others.
- **Mira's real name is Cassidy.** She was called "Mira" after she recovered — a "miracle child."
- **Rifts in the different worlds look like the event that caused them** — Post-session DM note: rifts connected to different worlds have different visual signatures. One world's rifts are full of blue electricity. Another has shattered glass. A coup-based world's rifts are fire.

## Cliffhangers & Unresolved Threads

- **Phase Two of combat** — The blood angel is dead, but the DM confirmed: "Phase two combat next session." What comes next is unknown. Arcus's transformed state and the blood-floor setting are still active.
- **Arcus's eyes went dark** — After the angel died, the light went out of his eyes. The blood-bloom flower and the "Bina" whisper both did something to him. What?
- **"I have a number. It's 8. And I'm not alone."** — The angel identified itself as number 8. There are more of them.
- **Mira's stolen memory** — Her memory of life before sickness is gone. The DM hinted at "maybe hope of getting it back." What was in that memory, and who/what now holds it?
- **The masculine voice through Mira** — A male voice spoke when she used Toll the Dead. Her patron? Her death-state speaking? An entity attached to her since her near-death?
- **"Bina"** — An Infernal name whispered to Arcus. Who or what is Bina?
- **The nail in Arcus's chest** — The angel nailed him. Is it still in there? What did it do to him?
- **Inquizo's plan to pull the nail from the angel** — He was flying toward it when the angel died. The nail was in the angel's palm (right side). With the angel dead, does it matter?
- **The orb entity / Kaelas / K.L.I.S.** — The entity in the orb (identity uncertain — not confirmed as Caelus), and the entity the blood angel called king/creator/father — are these the same? What is K.L.I.S.?
- **Arcus's transformation** — He became a large fluffy beast with a green sheep inside him. Is this a second form? A curse? His true nature?
- **The green sheep** — A small green sheep appeared inside Arcus's transformed form. Is the sheep part of him? *(Note: Arcus is not the same creature as the white-haired NPC from Session 3 who grabbed a green sheep — they are different individuals.)*
- **The agricultural agreement** — Arcus gave Lorya a jewelry imprint to buy "a couple more weeks" before the agreement collapses. What happens when it collapses?
