# DND Session 9 — The House That Screamed

*Linked to: [[Shattered World]] | [[Halcyra]] | [[Grungrak]] | [[Reficul]] | [[Inquizo]] | [[Mira]]*

---

## Pre-Session Recap

Session 8 ended with the party splitting — Mira investigating the mysterious wall writing (finding magical sigils on window frames and collecting an ink sample), Reficul causing chaos in the slums (attacking a mysterious fleeing figure, accidentally killing four homeless people with Earth Tremor, and transforming into an Oni), and the party reuniting with a major reconciliation between George and Mira. Inquizo slipped away during the emotional conversations (Stealth 16) and nobody noticed until later. Next session was promised to be Prophecy Day.

---

## TL;DR

The party splits to pursue research and recklessness in equal measure. Mira spends the day at the Alchemy Guild and the city library, discovering that the mysterious substance is not from SKRP and reacts violently with a rare biological compound found in maple trees — turning gaseous, then solidifying with salt. She also copies foraging notes and checks out key pages from an ancient ruins text suggesting the marked ruins are purely magical in origin and tied to an unnamed deity. Meanwhile, Reficul wanders into a Victorian manor crawling with writing and immediately gets into a brawl with an adventuring party already inside — one of whose members appears to be possessed by the house itself, going berserk with glowing red eyes and shapeshifting weapons. Grungrak saves the day with a clutch Sleep spell, the party flees, and the house screams behind them. Grungrak then delivers a deeply earnest speech to Reficul about second chances. Halcyra prays at the temple district and watches the YOMI letters glow with heat and light. Prophecy Day does not happen. It is now 8 PM and the party is heading to Reficul's apartment for a long rest.

## Full Summary

### Scene 1 — Mira at the Alchemy Guild (Morning) *(~07:03)*

Mira arrives at the Alchemy Guild to continue her research on the mysterious ink-like substance from the ruins. She is assisted by two alchemists:

- **Altair** — an elf alchemist, calm and methodical, who helps her with preliminary testing
- **Leander** — another guild alchemist, more enthusiastic and prone to explosive results

Key findings from Mira's research:
- The substance **beads up when heated**, indicating it does not behave like conventional ink or organic material
- The substance is **not from SKRP** — this is confirmed through elimination testing
- When combined with **Agopyl** (the inner spongy membrane of maple tree stems, produced by a condition called the "Syrup Virus" or caused by an unidentifiable mineral called "Woodchip"), the substance **turns gaseous**
- Adding **salt** causes it to re-solidify into a **graphite-like solid**
- The substance's reaction profile is unique and does not match any catalogued material the guild has on record

Mira completes a full write-up of her findings with guild documentation.

### Scene 2 — Mira at the City Library (Afternoon) *(~2:13:26)*

Mira visits the city library to research the ruins further. She is helped by:

- **Ny'e** — a goblin librarian with notably tiny legs, friendly and efficient

Research at the library:
- Mira finds the book **"Ruins from Ancient Ages"** and focuses on **pages 35–40**
- These pages confirm that the marked ruins are **purely magical in origin** — not architectural, not natural
- The text links the ruins to **a deity**, but the author deliberately refuses to speculate between two possible candidates (author notes they would not name either)
- **Halcyra checks out pages 35–40** from Ny'e *(note: Halcyra, not Mira, checked out these pages)*
- Mira also **copies 18 plant entries** from a foraging guide for future field use

### Scene 3 — Reficul and the Victorian Manor *(~1:10:15)*

While Mira handles research, Reficul ends up investigating the houses with writing in the street. Reficul enters a Victorian-style manor that is covered in the mysterious script. *(Note: Grungrak and Inquizo were outside — only Reficul went inside; the dragonborn was already inside with their party.)*

Inside, Reficul encounters **an adventuring party** already exploring the house:
- A **dragonborn** with a glowing purple rapier
- A **tiny mouse NPC** (a party member, not a pet)
- A **minotaur in mage robes**
- A **sorcerer woman** (who later mentions her warlock "just left one day")

A confrontation begins. The dragonborn's rapier **shifts into a warhammer** and his eyes begin **glowing red** — he becomes berserk, seemingly possessed by the house. *(~2:04:38)* The house appears to have active, malevolent intelligence. The mouse party member pins Reficul using an **invisible boulder**.

Grungrak casts **Sleep** — the dragonborn (below 30 HP at this point) goes down. The party flees. *(~2:06:09)* As they escape, **the house screams** — a loud, unnatural sound that follows them out. *(~2:08:37)*

### Scene 4 — The Spaghetti Incident *(~51:37)*

Reficul discovers **rancid spaghetti hidden inside a hollow brick in a wall** at the edge of town near the slums (not inside the manor itself). Inquizo and Grungrak attempt to use it as a prop for a religious "ascension" ritual outside. At some point, the **brick of spaghetti becomes an improvised weapon** and strikes one of the adventuring party NPCs.

### Scene 5 — Grungrak's Speech *(~2:09:29)*

After the escape from the manor, Grungrak pulls Reficul aside and delivers a sincere, emotionally earnest speech about believing in him and the importance of second chances. Significantly, Grungrak **uses Reficul's own words back at him** — referencing Reficul's prior statements about giving others second chances. The speech is genuine and lands hard.

### Scene 6 — Halcyra at the Temple District *(~3:14:29)*

Halcyra goes to the temple district to pray to **Apollo**. She brings with her the crossed-out letters — the YOMI papers — from the mysterious bundle discovered in earlier sessions.

During prayer:
- The letters **begin glowing** — emitting heat and light
- Mira is present and rolls **12** to detect arcane energy, confirming magical energy is present
- Two theories emerge: either **Apollo responded** to the prayer, or the **ink in the YOMI papers reacted to heat** (from the candles in the temple)
- No definitive answer is reached

*(Note: A Calm Emotions fail was noted during this scene but does not appear to have been Halcyra's cast — attribution uncertain.)*

### Scene 7 — End of Session *(~3:24:44)*

It is now **8 PM in-game**. The full party is heading to **Reficul's apartment** for a long rest.

**Prophecy Day did not happen.** The plan to discuss and interpret the prophecy together was derailed by the spaghetti/manor incident.

The DM pauses the recording at approximately 3:24 real-time to get food and take photos.

**Plan for next session:** Go to the forest in the morning.

## Lore & Worldbuilding Discoveries

- **The mystery substance is not from SKRP.** Its origin remains unknown but the source is narrowed.
- **Agopyl** — the inner spongy stem membrane of maple trees — is produced by the "Syrup Virus" or an unidentifiable mineral called "Woodchip." Causes the substance to turn gaseous.
- **Salt coagulates the gaseous form** of the substance back into a graphite-like solid.
- **The ruins are purely magical in origin.** Pages 35–40 of "Ruins from Ancient Ages" confirm they are not architectural or geological — they are linked to a deity whose identity the author would not name, noting two possible candidates.
- **The houses with writing are actively malevolent.** The Victorian manor possessed at least one person (dragonborn) and screamed audibly upon the party's escape.
- **YOMI letters glow** — the crossed-out letters from the mystery bundle react to either divine proximity or heat with visible light and warmth. Mira detected arcane energy (roll 12).
- **The adventuring party's sorcerer** mentioned her warlock companion "just left one day" — potentially relevant to larger warlock/patron lore.
