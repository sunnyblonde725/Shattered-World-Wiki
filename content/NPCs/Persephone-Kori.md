## Appearance & Personality

Twelve feet tall. Has a three-headed dog puppy (a proportional baby Cerberus). Has Truesight — she saw through [[Character Profiles/Inquizo|Inquizo]]'s Invisibility the moment she appeared. She carries a smartphone and texts people mid-conversation without explanation.

She is not hostile, but she is not warm either. "Begrudgingly cooperative" is the most accurate description. She negotiated efficiently, accepted the sickle as payment, and held up her end of the deal. She addressed [[Character Profiles/Grungrak|Grungrak]] as "beautiful" after he called her "really pretty vine lady," which may be the most personality she showed in the entire encounter.

---

## Backstory

Kori (the name used by some in the session) is the niece of [[NPCs/Queen-Ariel|Queen Ariel]]. Ariel's dead husband Eric had been stuck in her soul pool — a situation that required someone living to come retrieve him, since the dead cannot separate themselves from the dead. Persephone needed the party to solve a problem she couldn't solve herself.

She emerged from her dryad forest shrine after [[Character Profiles/Grungrak|Grungrak]] cleared it with the sickle (the sickle "Castrator of the Sky," previously wielded by [[NPCs/Arcus|Arcus]]). She ensnared Grungrak with vines. Then she negotiated. She accepted the sickle as payment for: retrieving Eric from the soul pool, giving the party a new and accurate map, and escorting them to the Gate of Ivory.

The sickle, named "Vengeance," is a sentient artifact with its own agenda. What Persephone intends to do with it is an unresolved thread.

---

## Relationship to the Party

A transactional power relationship. She needed them. They needed her. They exchanged. She should be treated as a major deity — not an enemy, not a friend, not someone whose patience should be tested.
