## Appearance & Personality

Long white-and-black hair that he keeps swept forward to conceal a set of brown horns — those horns glow faintly when Grungrak sings. There are nails driven into his right hand covering a serpent double-infinity symbol tattoo underneath.

In Session 3 he arrived in a field accompanied by a manticore and hundreds of hyenas, all of which deferred to him completely. Tasha's Hideous Laughter'd by Grungrak. Ate laxative gum. Grabbed a sheep. Despite that introduction, he turned out to be genuinely cautious and thoughtful — one of two siblings who have chosen not to follow the more destructive path of their kin.

He cannot speak his mother's name aloud because of some kind of barrier or agreement. He has built mirrors to help himself remember her.

His altar message: *"Mother, I miss you. I pray that I will never have to use what you did to see you again. I'm sorry for costing so much blood for my existence."*

---

## Backstory

Arcus and his sister [[NPCs/Lorya|Lorya]] are described as "the last two reasonable ones" — implying there were others who were not. His mother is Cybele (also called Avani), a deity he cannot name aloud. He previously wielded the sickle that is now called "Castrator of the Sky" — the sickle itself remembered: *"The last time someone like you went down there, they didn't come back up. Who wielded you then? My son. Arcus did."*

He has been maintaining an agricultural agreement that, without intervention, will collapse — meaning the area's food supply and possibly the safety of children in the region are at stake. He gave [[NPCs/Lorya|Lorya]] a piece of jewelry to "buy a couple more weeks" before that collapse.

---

## Relationship to the Party

Cautiously allied. He stayed above ground with the 247 sheep while the party descended into Hell. During the blood angel combat underground, he was enraptured — the angel whispered "Bina" (a capital-B Infernal name) to him and a blood-bloom flower appeared, transforming him into a large fluffy beast with a green sheep inside him. When the angel died, the light went out of his eyes entirely.

The party named him "Justin" first, then learned his real name from the sickle.
