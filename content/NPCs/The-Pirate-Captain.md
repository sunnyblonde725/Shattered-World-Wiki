## What We Know

The pirate captain opened the campaign by giving the party an ultimatum on a beach: get in the submarine, or die. She is the captain of an all-women pirate crew whose ship can both fly and dive.

She was hired by [[NPCs/Queen-Ariel|Queen Ariel]] to retrieve two stolen god-statues — "Poppa Poseidon" and "God of the Seas" — taken by an island tribe. To do that job, she needed the party to deliver a diplomatic report to the underwater city first. She sent them via a pre-programmed submarine and they are in her debt as a result.

Ariel is now dead. Whether that ends the pirate captain's obligation to retrieve the statues — or whether she continues for her own reasons — is unknown.

She has no confirmed name. She is **not** the sea god Alias — those are two separate characters.
