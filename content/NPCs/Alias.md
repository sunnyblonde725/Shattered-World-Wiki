## Appearance & Personality

An all-women pirate crew's captain who opened the entire campaign by giving the party a "submarine or die" ultimatum on a beach. Her ship can both fly and dive — a detail that distinguishes her from ordinary pirates and suggests significant resources or magical backing. She is transactional and direct: she was hired to do a job (retrieve two stolen god-statues for [[NPCs/Queen-Ariel|Queen Ariel]]), she needed people to do a different job, and the party was available.

---

## Backstory

She was hired by [[NPCs/Queen-Ariel|Queen Ariel]] to retrieve "Poppa Poseidon" and "God of the Seas" — two god-statues stolen by an island tribe. To do that job, she needed the party to deliver a diplomatic report to the underwater city first. She sent them via a pre-programmed submarine. They are in her debt as a result.

Ariel is now dead. The original contract is severed. Whether that ends Alias's obligation to retrieve the statues — or whether she continues for her own reasons — is unknown.

In Session 13, it was confirmed out-of-character that Alias is currently beached, wounded, and fighting something. The nature of the threat attacking her is completely unknown.

---

## Relationship to the Party

The party owes her. She got them where they needed to go in the campaign's opening chapter. She gave them a submarine. She issued an ultimatum that turned out to be the beginning of everything. The question of whether their diplomatic report delivery satisfied any portion of the debt is unresolved.

She hasn't appeared in person since Session 2. Session 13 revealed she's in danger.
