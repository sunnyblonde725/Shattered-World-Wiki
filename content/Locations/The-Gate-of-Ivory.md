## Description

A sandy beach hemmed in by cliffs on both sides, with no obvious way out except the portal embedded in the cliffside — the Gate of Ivory itself. The boat crashed here too (the party is starting to have a type). Sisyphus's cliff is accessible from the beach; the boulder was already at the top when the party arrived. The Saltwater Falls — a pit of surging water — is approximately 100 feet away. Above the beach, somewhere up those cliffs, the world waits.

It's the finish line. It just has a sea monster guarding it.

---

## Significance

The Gate of Ivory is the classical mythological exit from the underworld — through this gate pass *false* dreams, which is a hint the DM has planted and left sitting. The party had to fight Urscylla on this beach before they could reach the gate. [[Character Profiles/Reficul|Reficul]] briefly reappeared here (alive, somehow, briefly). The boulder from Sisyphus's cliff was thrown at Urscylla and pinned it. The River Styx pearls — the exit toll — were crushed, and the party leveled up. Chapter 1, technically over.
