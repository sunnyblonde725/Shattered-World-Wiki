## Appearance & Personality

Pompously dressed. Ornamental headpiece. Set up a platform overnight and began giving speeches about the uprising. Had crude drawings of all five party members made into revolutionary banners, which suggests either admiration or a very particular kind of showmanship.

His actual governance style was public executions and arena gladiator deaths. He had been in charge for approximately one night and was already killing people.

---

## Backstory

Nobody knows where Naamon came from. [[NPCs/King-Pmurt|Pmurt]] escaped, the city fell into chaos, and Naamon appeared on a platform. He was not part of the resistance. He was not part of the palace. He was an opportunist who saw a power vacuum and filled it with spectacle and blood.

---

## Relationship to the Party

He held [[NPCs/Dex|Dex]] on an execution platform. [[Character Profiles/Reficul|Reficul]] punched him off it. [[Character Profiles/Mira|Mira]] caught his body and he died in her arms. He had put up banners of the party's faces, which is a strange legacy.
