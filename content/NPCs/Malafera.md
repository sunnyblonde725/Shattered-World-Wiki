## Appearance & Personality

Never seen by the party. Known entirely through what [[NPCs/Starglass|Starglass]] has told them and what the party has observed across many sessions without knowing her name.

The rune alphabet — circles and clouds writing — that has appeared on walls, on the Velodorn wagons, in the goop constructs, and on Starglass's own body since Session 8 is Malafera's language. The confirmed words: hello, goodbye, life. "Life" was the last word she taught Starglass before they were separated.

She has "a lot of children" — Starglass and Haki are named, but there are more. She had a sister who went somewhere with "a king." She was attacked by [[NPCs/The-Doctor|The Doctor]] and his men, there was fighting, and she ran.

---

## Backstory

Everything the party knows about her has been filtered through Starglass. She created constructs, taught them language, and built a family. Then the Doctor came. She spoke to him, the conversation turned violent, and she was either taken or fled.

The goop constructs the party fought at X-salent — the creature in the cave, the substance on the Velodorn wagons — are built from her language and possibly her methods. Whether she made them, or someone else did using her techniques, is unknown.

---

## Relationship to the Party

She is the invisible architect behind a significant portion of the campaign's mystery. The party has been encountering her work since Session 8 without knowing it. [[Character Profiles/Mira|Mira]] was the one who connected the runes to Starglass's mother in Session 13.
