## Description

You fall through the tunnel and land in something that is not a cave and not a room. It's open, dark, and ankle-deep in blood — not pooled, not flowing, just *there*, dark and viscous and wrong. The air is wrong too. The DM confirmed it directly: "You passed through more than just a hole getting here." The sickle [[Character Profiles/Grungrak|Grungrak]] carried confirmed it — the direction they were traveling was Hell. There is no mistaking where you are. The question is just whether you make it back out.

Ways out, as far as the party could determine: pass through a rift or reality tear, or die. Neither option is great on its own.

---

## Significance

Hell is where the campaign's mythology went from interesting to *cosmologically unhinged*. A blood angel rose from the blood on the floor, announced itself as "Number 8" (implying at least seven others), and fought the party. The party won Phase One. Then Arcus's eyes went dark. Then a blood-bloom enveloped him and the angel whispered a name — *Bina* — into his ear. Then Mira's pre-sickness memories were stolen. Phase Two was announced. This is the place that broke things open.
