# DND Session 12 — The Shadow Selves

*Linked to: [[Shattered World]] | [[Halcyra]] | [[Grungrak]] | [[Reficul]] | [[Inquizo]] | [[Mira]]*

**DM:** Cooper
**Players:** Ian (Reficul), Charlie (Inquizo), Lorenne/Sunny (Halcyra), Ele (Mira), Cooper (Grungrak/George)

---

## Pre-Session Recap

Session 11 ended with King Pmurt escaping via contingency teleport — partially castrated by Mira's sickle, alive, with his emergency medical salve. The party was in the throne room with ~20 terrified remaining guards, ~40 dead guards, scattered baked beans, and a shattered chandelier. Durf Numgis had already left for the northern continent. The prison was still full of captive rebels (women, birds, mermaids). Mira's pact weapon sickle materialized in her hand without her summoning it. The party was leveling up to 6.

---

## TL;DR

Session 12 began in the wreckage of King Pmurt's throne room and ended on a couch with five characters weeping at what they had just seen of themselves. The party briefly explored the aftermath of Session 11, encountered a mysterious liberated prisoner with dragon eyes, met a group called the Liberators (a Scarlet Pimpernel-style troupe who sprung from a book), and got pointed toward the Bird Kingdom (Voxus). But the center of the session — and the most emotionally significant hour of the campaign so far — was Gunj's shadow therapy sequence, in which each character faced a dark mirror version of themselves and was forced to argue, flee, negotiate, or walk into a dragon about the choices they had made. Everyone received a named item from their shadow self. Everyone cried. The DM also revealed that Numgis is working with the nightmare that sealed the God of Dreams, and that unsealing her is connected to the next town.

## Full Summary

### Scene 1 — Throne Room Aftermath

The party opened in the ruined throne room, picking through the carnage of Session 11. Cooper recapped: Pmurt had gone north before the party arrived, Numgis had gone north for unknown reasons, and Pmurt escaped via contingency teleport mid-castration. Under Zone of Truth, Pmurt confirmed he only knew Numgis was north — he had no further information about where or why.

The party investigated for Numgis's room. Investigation checks ranged from 9 (Ian/Reficul) to 24 (Ele/Mira). A neon merch sign — Vegas-style lights all around the throne room — had been knocked crooked and now pointed down the hallway toward the prison, which happened to be the same direction as Numgis's room. The party correctly interpreted this as a lead.

**Key beat:** The throne room had neon merch signs around it because that's just the kind of king Pmurt was. Nobody had noticed during the combat.

---

### Scene 2 — The Prison and the Liberators

The party entered the prison. The situation was mostly resolved: most cells were open and empty. The giant fish tank in the back had a pipe hammered through the glass, draining the water and creating a tube to the beach outside. The mermaids and birds had apparently already escaped via the tube.

Only one cell remained occupied — three men inside. One of them stood out immediately: his eyes shifted from brilliant gold to silver mid-conversation, and his accent changed at the same time.

**The prisoner with shifting eyes:** A farmer named Bant. He had been struck by lightning on his farm six months ago, saw paper tearing across reality, heard a machine sound, and then woke up in the prison cell. He described the Shattering the way someone who lived through it would — as a physical experience, not a history lesson. Mira rolled a history check (14) and confirmed that while there are no records of bio-matter being merged in the Shattering, there were library whispers about gods not answering normally, or appearing differently than they used to. The implication: the prisoner's shifting eyes were not innate — arcana check confirmed it was cast magic, not a personal trait.

When the party started to explain the Shattering to him, his eyes snapped back and he said "I need to figure out what's going on. I will find him." Then he jumped through the fish tank and got sucked up the tube.

**The Liberators:** Outside on the beach, the pastel-colored men the party had heard about were waiting. They introduced themselves through their spokesperson, Elton — about 5'6", well put-together, clearly the talker of the group. The Liberators are a Scarlet Pimpernel-style troupe: heroes from another world who were told there were people in danger and deployed accordingly. They came out of a book in a library on a mountain, sent by a 7-foot old man. Their assignment was to liberate three locations: the island city (Pmurt's), the Bird Kingdom (Voxus/birds), and the fish.

They had already dealt with the birds — they went to the eagle king's nest (Ravalli's), found another man with wire glasses already there, and that man and Ravalli were "setting up a system" to help with something. The word Elton remembered was "sleep." They left satisfied the birds were handled.

**The Liberators' book man:** The old man who sent them was 7 feet tall, "jovial," and would sign things XOXO. The party recognized this immediately as their own X-salent contact. The man was from a grand library on a mountain — and walked out of a rock when he left. This is the same figure who has been adjacent to the campaign's larger mysteries. *(Note: Details of the book man and his connection to X-salent are uncertain — flag for manual review.)*

**The man with wire glasses at Ravalli's nest:** Present two days ago. Described as "very well put together" with wire-rim glasses. Setting up a system with the eagle king (Ravalli) related to sleep. This is Numgis — or someone adjacent to the God of Dreams situation. *(Note: Identity of this figure is uncertain — may or may not be Numgis.)*

During the escape via the prison tube, George (Dwarf Bard) hit the water and immediately sank like a rock. Reficul rolled a nat 20 Athletics and pulled him out.

### Scene 3 — Town, Statues, and Departing the Island City

The party returned to the island city's main square. Two statues remained — the bird (falcon-esque, gold face recently uncovered where giant claw marks had torn Pmurt's gilded face off) and the buff man with a staff. The Poseidon statue was gone (the party had stolen it previously). The pirate lady statue was also gone — the pirates presumably retrieved it.

Halcyra flew up to the bird statue and popped the gold head off with minimal effort — the face of a very majestic falcon underneath. History check (13): Mira confirmed it was someone significant to the bird people, but couldn't name them. The gold head was removed and the true statue revealed.

The party gave the liberated guards a chance to stand down from Pmurt's authority, freed anyone still technically imprisoned, and prepared to depart for the Bird Kingdom (Voxus). **The party commandeered King Pmurt's airship** as their transport — this is how they traveled to Voxus.

---

### Scene 4 — Travel to the Bird Kingdom / Voxus

The party traveled to the Bird Kingdom using a combination of the Liberators' connections and their own resources. Details of the exact travel method are not fully clear from the transcript (the DM had cut some planned dungeon content). They arrived at Voxus — the bird people's mountain homeland, modeled on the Wind Waker Rito rather than Breath of the Wild Rito (the DM confirmed this explicitly in the post-session discussion).

**Ravalli:** The giant eagle chief of Voxus. He runs the Bird Kingdom. The party met him. He was already aware that something was being arranged regarding sleep. He admitted to cooperating with the man with wire glasses (Numgis or his agent) on something, then acknowledged the party catching him in it. He was not hostile but was constrained — he mentioned having duties. Gunj is his right-hand (an owl who serves as shadow therapist for the kingdom).

**General Os and General Fal:** Referenced as figures of authority in the Bird Kingdom or connected to its military/political structure. Not major players in this session but named.

**Bant (also called Beetle):** The prisoner with shifting eyes who saw the world end — confirmed to be the same person as Beetle. Encountered in or around Voxus after escaping through the prison tube. See full description in Scene 2 above.

The visit to the Bird Kingdom was cut short when Ravalli directed the party toward Gunj — a figure who could help with the shadow situation. The dungeon originally planned for this location was dropped entirely; Cooper had decided it was better to get to the shadow therapy sequence.

---

### Scene 5 — Gunj's Couch

**Gunj:** A figure who functions as a shadow therapist — someone who works with the nightmare side of consciousness in a structured way. The party arrived at his space and he guided them into a shared unconscious experience, sitting on a couch together. He described it as similar to breathing exercises, then faded them to black individually and walked them through encounters with their shadow selves.

The setup: each character would meet a version of themselves — older, or an alternate-path self — who would challenge them on their choices. Some shadows wanted to take over. Some wanted to argue. Some just wanted to be heard. Each character had a binary outcome: accept the shadow or resist/suppress it, each yielding a different item. No one fully accepted — though Mira came closest; Cooper considered her approach a pass, even though she did not technically accept her shadow. The "dusk" and "dawn" item labels are specific to Halcyra's situation: the Bow of Dusk (what she received) vs. the Bow of Dawn (the acceptance version). The DM had designed an entire alternate-acceptance path that went unused.

Shadow names (revealed post-sequence):
- **Mira's shadow:** The Hollow Heir
- **Halcyra's shadow:** The Crown of Golden Lies
- **Reficul's shadow:** The First and Last Witness
- **Inquizo's shadow:** The Warden Brother
- **George's shadow:** No dedicated shadow sequence — Cooper (DM) did not roleplay with himself. George received his item after the other shadow sequences concluded.

## The Dreams — Full Detail

### Mira's Dream (first pass)

Mira found herself in a graveyard. A version of herself stood there — well-dressed, bright, doing better. The shadow self was tethered to Mira by something physical, like chains or rope.

The shadow challenged Mira: *You could have healed him. You had the ability.* Mira pushed back — she had never had the penchant to heal others, only herself. The shadow escalated: *You don't have the control. You don't have the guts.* Mira's response was clinical: she hasn't done the study. The shadow's final line before the scene shifted: *Study? As if books have the answer to everything. You just don't put in the effort.*

Then — before Mira's full sequence concluded — the scene went dark and shifted to Inquizo's sequence, then Halcyra's, then Reficul's, cycling back. (The DM ran them in rotating segments.)

### Inquizo's Dream

Inquizo arrived somewhere snowing. He could smell what he recognized as someone's breakfast. A voice from a doorway called: *Quizzy, is that you?* He did not respond. He looked outward, non-responsive. A version of him appeared — wearing a cloak, differently composed — and Inquizo decided immediately to run. He started sprinting. The DM said "okay" with complete acceptance.

As he ran, the shadow effortlessly copied his every movement. The shadow showed him an image: the ethereal silhouette of his sister appeared in the path. Inquizo, still running, plowed directly through her — and that's why the image dissipated. He ran through it. The shadow said: *You fail her again.*

Inquizo tried to hide. Rolled a 19, found an alley. His shadow appeared next to him effortlessly — helping him hide. *My shadow's helping better than I am,* Inquizo noted. *(Note: The details of this hiding sequence are uncertain — flag for manual review.)*

Then chains began wrapping around his arms and pulling him down. The shadow spoke: *What kind of big brother are you?* The figure of his brother appeared. Inquizo covered his ears (he doesn't have ears as a kobold) and screamed: *What do you want?*

The shadow's answer: *I want what you want. I want to protect him.*

Chains went around his neck and burned at the scales. Inquizo said: *I already tried that and it didn't work and I just made things worse.* More chains. He began hyperventilating, sobbing. He curled into a ball.

**Outcome:** Inquizo successfully suppressed his shadow — which is one of the two possible endings. He gave up. The shadow faded into bracelets — shackle chains — around his wrists. He woke up muttering *I'm sorry* over and over. By the end of the session, the muttering shifted to *there's nothing I could have done.* He was in a low-key catatonic state. Mira picked him up like a cat and held him.

### Halcyra's Dream (first pass)

Halcyra was flying — the brightest, sunniest day she had ever experienced, utterly free, wings fully out. An older version of herself flew beside her and asked: *Why did you ever stop yourself from feeling this free?*

Halcyra: *I don't have a choice. I'm a good kid. I follow the rules.*

The older Halcyra: *Imagine how much better your life would have been if you didn't hide who you are. Feel that warm, glorious sun. Enjoy this light.*

Halcyra, flying: *I love it on my wings.*

Then: *Why did you stop yourself from flying as a child? You let people hide who you are.*

Halcyra: *Because mother told me to hide.*

At which point the older Halcyra's face switched to Halcyra's mother and screamed: ***Why have you left me?!***

Then Halcyra crashed — fell and landed in front of her own farmhouse. Her mother stood before her.

**Mother confrontation:**
- *Why have you left me?*
- *I was upset with you. You never spoke of father.*
- *I didn't have a choice.*
- *And something that felt like him called me.*
- Simultaneously, a booming, familiar, kind voice called: *Halcyra, I need your help.*
- The voice was coming from outside, and up — from the sky.

*What if I needed you here? You can't protect me from what's out there.*

Halcyra: *How are you supposed to protect me, Mom? Do you even understand the amount of hurt you've caused me?*

Mother: *Yes. You have no idea how much suffering it caused me to see you suffer. And being told you can't — I didn't have a choice.*

Halcyra turned around. The session moved on to another character temporarily.

### Reficul's Dream

*(Note: The order of events in this sequence may be slightly backwards — flag for manual review.)*

Reficul found himself being shown what his life would have looked like if he had followed the rules. His shadow self had no contract, no debt. Just a small cottage. Children laughing outside. His name called lovingly. Simple life. No spotlight.

Reficul's shadow asked: *Would you like to hear something about life?* Then described everything Reficul couldn't have — the peace, the relationships, the days where nothing was owed.

Reficul's response was magnificently him. He acknowledged the beauty of it, then said: *But why are you telling me everything I cannot have? Everything is already set in stone. I have my regrets.*

The shadow: *You had a regret. I'm impressed. Well done. You didn't make that mistake.*

Reficul: *I see you haven't made a mistake yet. So you haven't learned anything yet.*

The shadow tried to claim Reficul had no pride because he hadn't earned pain. Reficul disagreed: *I am not you. I could have been. Too bad.*

Then the shadow revealed it was looking for someone — a deity, possibly shape-changing, that someone had told Reficul might be relevant. When pressed on whose rules Reficul follows, Reficul turned the question back: *I've been asking who you are. You've put your life on display. Tell me — I am your regrets. I am what you hide.*

Reficul: *And what form are you? Are you made of the flesh of wrath?*

Shadow: *I am but a shout.*

The shadow then merged with Reficul — the tattoo of darkness across his left arm became permanent. The shadow said: *You can have others meet their own shadow. Mark allows others to meet me.*

**Outcome:** Reficul's shadow fused with him, gaining the ability to summon others' shadows physically (living people within 30 feet) or, through Whispers-style mechanics, project deceased people's appearances.

### Mira's Dream (second pass — the dragon)

Mira's shadow, frustrated, screamed that Mira was *not supposed to do that* — a reference to Mira walking toward her father rather than away. The shadow grew. Mira's green veins shot into giant wings. Her shadow's head shifted into a dragon head. And the black tendrils that the shadow had been holding — the chains between Mira and the things she was tied to — became part of the wings.

**Before Mira stood a blue dragon with green veins pulsing through it.**

Cooper asked: *Who is she screaming at?*

Mira asked, critically: *Is Grungrak part of that dragon?*

Cooper: *He sure is.*

The dragon — which is the patron, or a manifestation of the patron — said: *Lost seven years. How much more have you lost to me? What else have you abandoned?*

Mira: *I have not lost anything. I stay in control.*

Dragon: *Your pitiful friend's attempts at slowing me down — the only reason that even worked — I was truly the one in power. I am behind it. Which is to say, I am you. You are the one behind each of these deaths.*

Mira's response: she walked into the dragon. Not toward it. Into it. She attempted to merge with it.

Cooper, genuinely caught off guard: *I was expecting Mira to be in denial. Mira does not deny; Mira deflects.*

Inside the dragon: dark. Cold. Screams — not screams Mira recognized. Her mother's screams. Screams she didn't know.

Then Mira found Grungrak inside the dragon.

*Was it the Grungrak from the mirror? What did she do differently?* Mira asked.

Cooper: *You told her to run. What did she do instead?*

Mira: *She saved.*

The dragon screamed until Mira faced Grungrak directly. Black tendrils came from Mira and restrained the dragon — held it still while Mira dealt the final blow. She was not disintegrated because of her own restraint.

Cooper: *Are you happy?*

Mira: *I am my best friend.*

She found the tether — physical, real. She summoned her pact weapon and severed the cord.

**Grungrak became physical for a moment.** Not ethereal. Real. He embraced her.

*Thank you. Thank you for letting me be me.*

He slowly faded.

**The table cried.** All of them.

### Mira's Dream (third pass — the contract)

*(Note: Some dialogue in this section may be wrongly attributed between Shadow and Mira — flag for manual review.)*

Back to her shadow, which now held two last tendrils. The shadow said: *You see yourself holding tight to two last tendrils.*

Shadow: *Get them back. Why not control them?*

Mira: *That is their choice to make. If they do not choose, I choose for them — it is no longer them.*

Shadow: *You never had them. You had an image of them.*

Then the tendrils came out and Mira saw a contract. A beautiful contract with her father's signature at the bottom.

The terms: *My soul for hers.*

Date: shortly after Mira's fifth birthday, when she first got sick.

**The contract was signed by her father, trading his soul to save her life.** The contract was what gave the patron control. The patron Mira carries — the masculine voice, the sickle — is connected to a deal her father made to keep her alive.

Shadow: *Don't you know what your dark power is? It's this.*

Then the other tendril — the one not connected to the contract — pulled forward toward Inquizo. The shadow asked how much easier it would be to simply control Inquizo rather than let him make choices.

Mira: *I am not letting them go. I am not stopping them from leaving. I am no god.*

She severed that tendril too.

Then her shadow shrank to a small, scared little girl.

*What am I when they all leave?*

Mira: *You are more than Mira.*

*Am I enough?*

*You are you. Enough for what?*

*Enough for them.*

*Were they enough for you?*

*I hope so. I feel like I failed. I feel like he did his best.*

Shadow: *You are living. You are thriving. Look at you — out of bed, outside our home. You've done so much more than father could have dreamed.*

*Then perhaps I'll give you father's final gift.*

The shadow reached into her pocket and pulled out a necklace — a lightning bolt pendant.

*You are more than your broken pieces.*

She put the necklace in Mira's hand. Everything faded.

**The Stormwake Sigil.** A lightning bolt necklace. Mira's item.

### Halcyra's Dream (conclusion)

Halcyra, having turned her back on her mother, heard the booming voice louder: *Halcyra, I need you now. The world is at stake. The worlds are at stake.*

Her mother begged for one more minute. Halcyra turned back: *Mom — is the reason you sheltered me so much because of a reason bigger than you?*

Mother: *Yes.*

Halcyra: *Whatever is calling me out there is bigger than me. I have to go.*

Mother: *Please — save me one minute. It will help. It's related to your father.*

The voice boomed a third time: *The worlds are at stake.*

Halcyra went to her mother, gave her a hug, said barely above a whisper: *I promise I do love you.* And walked out the door.

Outside: an old man — the same old man who helped her take her paladin oath before. He pulled out a box and gave her a bow.

*You gotta finish your adventure.*

**The Bow of Dusk.** A +2 longbow. Does no piercing damage — only radiant. Named Dusk because Apollo is strongest at dawn, not dusk, meaning this bow is not Halcyra's final form — it will become the Dawn Bow when she gets there. The DM told her directly: *You just gotta get through the night first.*

### George's Item

George did not have his own shadow dream sequence — Cooper (his player) is also the DM and did not want to roleplay with himself. George received an item, but it was mentioned after the shadow sequences rather than emerging from a dedicated dream of his own.

George received a two-sided necklace that spins. One side shows an olive branch. The other side shows a forte symbol for music. The necklace is either the **Necklace of Tranquility** (olive branch side) or the **Power of Courage** (music side), depending on which way it faces. George holds the deal — he can choose which way to use it.

*(Note: The Grungrak who appeared inside Mira's dragon was a presence connected to the original Grungrak/the patron — not George-as-Grungrak doing a separate sequence. When George woke up, he was smiling through tears.)*

## Lore & Worldbuilding Discoveries

- **Mira's patron is connected to a dragon.** The patron manifested as a blue dragon with green veins (Mira's own veins). Grungrak is somehow part of it — "is Grungrak part of that dragon? He sure is." The exact nature of this is not fully clear, but the patron and the dragon and Grungrak's story are entangled.
- **Mira's father sold his soul.** Signed shortly after her fifth birthday. The contract reads: *My soul for hers.* This is what gave the patron access to Mira. She did not choose a warlock pact — she was born into one, or had one born into her, paid for by her father.
- **The God of Dreams was sealed by her own nightmare / shadow.** The God of Dreams is female (Gunj uses "she"). She is currently sealed — and Numgis is actively working with the nightmare that sealed her in order to unseal her in a controlled way that benefits him.
- **Numgis is working with the nightmare, for the nightmare.** His prophet role is not incidental. He is an active collaborator with the thing that sealed the God of Dreams.
- **Unsealing the God of Dreams involves the next town.** Part of the unsealing process is in the next location the party will travel to. Gunj's chief may believe this requires destroying the town. Gunj does not believe that is accurate — but he can only stall.
- **The Bird Kingdom is called Voxus.** The bird people's giant eagle chief is Ravalli. His right-hand is Gunj (an owl, shadow therapist). Their political structure includes military figures (General Os, General Fal).
- **The Liberators came from a book.** A Scarlet Pimpernel-style troupe deployed by a 7-foot jovial old man who signs XOXO. They came out of a grand library in/on a mountain. The same figure the party has encountered adjacently throughout the campaign.
- **The farmer Bant was merged in the Shattering.** His shifting eyes (gold to silver) represent two entities or aspects that got merged when the Shattering sent him from his world to this one. The arcana check confirmed it was a cast magic effect, not innate — meaning whoever or whatever was layered onto him was doing so deliberately or was carried along.
- **The man with wire glasses at Ravalli's nest is Numgis (or his agent).** He was setting up a sleep-related system with Ravalli two days before the party arrived. This is almost certainly Numgis.

## Cliffhangers & Unresolved Threads

- **Numgis and the God of Dreams.** He is actively working with the nightmare that sealed her, and part of the unsealing mechanism is in the next town. The party must get there before either Numgis completes it or Gunj's chief decides to destroy the town.
- **Mira's father's contract.** Her soul was purchased by her father's soul. What happened to her father? Is he alive? Is he in hell? Does the patron have him? Mira has the necklace from her shadow — her father's "final gift."
- **The dragon and Grungrak.** The patron is a dragon. Grungrak is somehow inside or part of it. The exact nature of this connection — whether it is symbolic, literal, or about the original Grungrak's death-by-dragon — is unresolved.
- **The man with shifting eyes (Bant).** He disappeared up the stairs. He was merged in the Shattering. Who or what is layered on top of him?
- **The Liberators** are still operating — they have a third location to handle (the fish). They will be encountered again.
- **The Bow of Dusk vs. Dawn.** Halcyra has the dusk version. The dawn version exists. Getting there requires something — growth, time, or specific events.
- **Inquizo's Chains of Eternity.** He suppressed his shadow, which is one of two endings. His shadow told him: *You can't let this hold you down forever. You can't let me be gone forever.* The shadow is suppressed, not resolved.
- **Gunj's chief.** Someone above Gunj believes destroying the next town is necessary to unseal the God of Dreams. Gunj is stalling. The party needs to move fast.
- **The bird-falcon statue.** Mira identified it as someone historically significant to the birds. Its true identity is unknown.
