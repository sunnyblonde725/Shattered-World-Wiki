## Description

A thoroughfare full of games and vendors, noise and color, culminating in a large big top tent at the far end. Cotton candy. A strongman game. The smell of sawdust and something sweet. The kind of place that feels like pure fun until it suddenly, violently, isn't. The last thing the party remembers before waking up shipwrecked: red fog and figures in hoods.

---

## Significance

The carnival is where the entire campaign began — and also where the party's freedom ended, temporarily. Someone specifically targeted this place (or used it as a hunting ground) to abduct the party. The "Chosen One" child moment happened here, which may or may not be a throwaway gag or a seed planted for later. [[Character Profiles/Reficul|Reficul]]'s Nat 20 performance triggered a spontaneous dwarf circus, which is one of the better things to ever happen in this campaign.

The red fog and hooded figures are the real mystery. They weren't carnival staff. Someone sent them.
