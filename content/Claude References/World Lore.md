## World Lore & Discoveries

**Surface oceans are freshwater on this world** — The saltwater oceans are secret or underground. Relevant because Reficul's bull shark form functioned in the city's water. *(Session 2)*

**The underwater city is at war with "bird folk"** — Winged beings are enemies of the state. This directly affects [[Halcyra]], who has wings. *(Session 2)*

**The city's ruler was both queen and god** — Her authority overrode ordinary divine orders. She is now dead. *(Session 2–3)*

**Mermen were once human men** — Their legs were forcibly cut off and replaced with fishtails. They serve 300-year brainwashed contracts signed with fishbones they no longer remember signing. At least one guard completed his contract and had his legs restored. Ariel's death may have voided the contracts. *(Session 2–3)*

**Two god-statues have been stolen from the city** — "Poppa Poseidon" and the "God of the Seas." Ariel hired the pirates to retrieve both. The men on the island have at least one; its face was defaced and replaced with the tribe leader's face. *(Session 2–3)*

**The pirates are connected to the statue situation** — Ariel hired the pirate captain to retrieve the stolen statues. The submarine's pre-programming confirms this. *(Session 2–3)*

**Clams on the walls are surveillance devices** — Called "Security Glabra." [[Mira]] noticed these during the city visit. *(Session 2)*

**There is a tribe of humans on an island** — Located between the sea and the mountain. Led by a "rightfully horrible, horrible man." They hate both women and birds. The mysterious white-haired NPC mentions this island has horses. *(Session 2–3)*

**The worlds colliding was unknown to the city** — They did not know there was an island connected to the mountain above them until Mira told them. *(Session 2)*

**Grungrak's backstory fragment** — Was once a miner who lived in a mountain town. Has amnesia about many things, including basic anatomy. *(Session 2)*

**Ariel's origin** — She was once a kind mermaid who earned legs and fell in love with a man named Amos. A sea witch drowned Amos. Grief and rage transformed her into a tyrant. Revealed by [[Hamish]] in Session 3. *(Session 3)*

**Ariel maintained a city-wide silencing enchantment** — She suppressed the speech of all "inferior" creatures. It dissolved instantly when she died. [[Hamish]] could now speak freely for the first time. *(Session 3)*

**Reficul's true changeling form** — When he dies, he reverts to his true form: a small figure (~3 ft), fiendish horns, long white hair, blood that shifts from green to blue based on temperature. His death mechanic: no death saves — he goes to hell when he dies. *(Session 3)*

**Reficul's cause of death is unknown** — A medicine check of 21 confirmed he did not die from the self-inflicted stab wound or from drowning. Cause unexplained. *(Session 3)*

**Mira is dead (or caught between states)** — She does not know. She is "reinvigorated" by some unknown force but "not supposed to be here." Her blood is tugged in the pit area. She cannot heal or pray. She moves at ~1/15th normal speed in the pit location. *(Session 3)*

**Puffer fish communicate via bubble-size writing** — And can speak, when not silenced. *(Session 3)*

**The nail in the mysterious NPC's kneecap** — Striking it with a hammer produces green foam. What is inside him is unknown. *(Session 3)*

**The Good Boy of the North** — A moon-associated entity reachable through sincere religious prayer. Real. The high-pitched laughing from the northern forest is connected to it. The party successfully contacted it via a religious roll — it acknowledged the prayer but did not appear, as it "didn't need to right now." *(Session 3)*

**A manticore-shaped beast and hundreds of hyenas live in the northern forest** — They deferred to the mysterious white-haired NPC during Session 3's encounter. *(Session 3)*

**Laxative gum can trigger physical transformations** — The mysterious NPC's rose-wing transformation was triggered by consuming laxative bubblegum from [[Grungrak]]'s MRE military rations. *(Session 3)*

**Arcus and Lorya are children of Cybele/Avani** — The fertility goddess whose statue is in the underground temple. Both confirmed this by raising their right hands when the sickle asked. *(Session 4)*

**The sickle "Castrator of the Sky" is a piece of Cybele/Avani herself** — "A piece of his mother put in here." Sentient — says "I am Vengeance. Against the sky. Against love loss." *(Session 4)*

**Arcus previously wielded the sickle and descended into Hell** — The sickle said the last person who went down didn't come back up. His son. Arcus. *(Session 4)*

**"Down" is Hell** — The underground space beneath the temple is Hell or a layer of it. The sickle confirmed this. The party passed through "more than just a hole." *(Session 4)*

**The agricultural agreement is collapsing** — Seeds were stolen → field can't be replanted → creatures get hungry → they take children. Arcus bought a few more weeks by giving Lorya a piece of jewelry (bracelet/anklet impression from a box). *(Session 4)*

**Caelus / K.L.I.S. / Kaelas** — A sky deity imprisoned in the black orb was given immortality by Kaelas. The blood angel in Hell called its king/creator/father "K.L.I.S." These may be the same entity or connected. A Celestial inscription reads: "Agents of Kaelas, entrap the minds of the divorced." *(Session 4)*

**"Bina"** — An Infernal name (capital B) whispered by the blood angel to Arcus, causing him to become enraptured. Meaning and identity unknown. History check of 4 was insufficient. *(Session 4)*

**There are multiple blood angels numbered** — The angel claimed: "I have a number. It's 8. And I'm not alone." There are at least 7 others. *(Session 4)*

**Mira's pre-sickness memories were stolen** — The blood angel took her memories of life before her illness. Gone permanently, "except for the maybe hope of getting it back" (DM teased). Since it was early childhood, she may not have consciously held them anyway. *(Session 4)*

**A masculine voice speaks through Mira when she uses death magic** — When she cast Toll the Dead, a male voice emerged from her instead of her own. Her patron or some entity tied to her death-state. *(Session 4)*

**Rifts between worlds look like the event that caused them** — Post-session DM lore note: different worlds' rifts have distinct visual signatures based on the catastrophe that created them. One world's rifts = blue electricity. Another = shattered glass. A coup world's rifts = fire. *(Session 4)*

**Mira's real name is Cassidy** — She was called "Mira" (short for miracle) after she recovered from illness. She was a "miracle child." Also referred to as "Natamira." *(Session 4)*

**Ways out of Hell (or the underground plane)** — The blood angel mentioned: passing through rifts/tears in reality, or death. *(Session 4)*

**The underworld has multiple layers** — The Fields of Asphodel are one layer. Above them: an upper civic level with skyscrapers, water roads (Venice-style boat traffic), and asphalt streets. An elevator connects the levels. *(Session 6)*

**The four gates of the underworld** — Gate of Ivory (viable exit, upper city), Gate of Horn (viable), Gate of Dusk (requires Tartarus — strongly avoided), Gate of Dawn (now broken — cleaved by Mira and the sickle in Session 5). *(Session 6)*

**The River Lethe ("forget water") flows through the underworld** — Fall in and you forget everything. Nearly claimed Grungrak in Session 6 when the steering wheel came loose. *(Session 6)*

**Eating pomegranate seeds in Persephone's domain** — In Greek mythology, eating pomegranate seeds in the underworld binds you there (Persephone herself ate six seeds and must spend six months a year in the underworld). **Grungrak ate multiple seeds** during Session 6. **Reficul also ate pomegranate.** Neither character has been told what this means. The DM knows. *(Session 6)*

**The soul pool in Persephone's shrine** — A swirling mass of souls (not water). Semi-corporeal dead can be recovered from it. The process is dangerous — Reficul died twice retrieving Eric. *(Session 6)*

**The Psychopomp (Thanatos)** — An extremely tall cloaked figure with antlers, perpetually drowsy, stationed in the upper city. He can guide the dead (and near-dead) to the appropriate gate. Accessible when Persephone vouches for you. *(Session 6)*

**Ariel's husband Eric** — Drowned trying to breathe underwater to be with Ariel. Is now semi-corporeal and traveling with the party. Knows the party killed Ariel. Can sail a boat excellently. *(Note: Eric is a completely separate NPC from George. George is the clay copy of Grungrak who is a party member. Eric is Ariel's husband — these are two different characters.) (Session 6)*

**Ariel is confirmed as Persephone's niece in some sense** — Persephone referred to the situation as "my niece brought a problem." The genealogical connection is unclear but canonical. *(Session 6)*

**The metal horse is the grandson of Poseidon's horse** — Its collar reads "..." (source). Poseidon is implied present in this campaign via the stolen god-statues. Bringing his horse's grandson out of the underworld on a boat may not go unnoticed. *(Session 6)*

**The sickle "Castrator of the Sky" / Vengeance is now in Persephone's possession** — Given as payment for the party's passage out of the underworld. It was a sentient artifact with its own agenda (Vengeance against the sky, against love lost). What Persephone intends to do with it is unknown. *(Session 6)*

**Scylla guards the river near the Gate of Ivory** — Six dog-heads above water, six tentacles below. Surfaces on both sides of the boat simultaneously. *(Session 6)*

**The River Lethe causes memory loss and self-duplication** — Entering it makes your body "duplicate" — you see smaller versions of yourself. The river contains something like "an ocean of alcohol." Entering causes the Lethe to draw out reflections of who you are. Eric lost all memory of Ariel after entering. Grungrak encountered a duplicate of himself ("George"). The effects on Mira (who is technically already dead) are unresolved. *(Session 7)*

**River Styx Pearls allow passage through the Gate of Ivory** — Small pearls associated with the River Styx. Ariel distributed them to the party during the Scylla fight. Crushing one in your mouth makes your body go ethereal/numb for ~10 minutes — except your "Achilles heel" (one mortal spot remains: Grungrak's pinky toes, Inquizo's ear fins). The effect doesn't transfer between planes. *(Session 7)*

**Sisyphus is real and guards the Gate of Ivory cliffs** — The actual Sisyphus from Greek mythology has been pushing his boulder at the Gate of Ivory cliffs since eternity. Grungrak pushed it over for him for the first time ever (nat 20). It landed on Scylla. Sisyphus wept. *(Session 7)*

**Scylla's voice can steal voices** — She stole Grungrak's voice by luring him into trying to cast Healing Word (which failed). The voice was contained in a glowing orb and orbited around Scylla's body. Grungrak recovered it after the boulder pinned Scylla. *(Session 7)*

**Scylla was pinned by the boulder — not confirmed dead** — The boulder from Sisyphus's cliff landed on Scylla after Grungrak threw it. Her tentacles retreated and she was pinned. Whether she is dead or merely immobilized is not confirmed. *(Session 7)*

**Inquizo's jade/emerald necklace went to Scylla** — Inquizo, charmed by Scylla's singing aria, gave her a necklace depicting a snake eating itself (or a bird, or a bird eating itself). Scylla smiled when she received it — eels don't smile. The significance of this item is unknown but was clearly not nothing. *(Session 7)*

**The world has changed drastically in the last 6 months** — From X-salent's perspective: "huge arcs of lightning broke the land." Mountains and volcanoes appeared that were not there before. This is a major world-shaping event that the party was not present for (they were in the underworld or portaling). It is connected to the campaign's larger shattered-worlds narrative. *(Session 7)*

**SKRP is the world of origin for Grungrak and Mira** — Confirmed. Halcyra is from Planetos. Mira and Grungrak are both from SKRP. The world that includes hell may be called Baviosuit (mentioned but uncertain). *(Session 7)*

**X-salent guild structure:** The Adventurers' Guild at X-salent has multiple sub-guilds including Cartographers (Guild Room 5) and Alchemists (notorious for blowing things up). Former housing has been converted to guild rooms as the guild grew. Quest rewards are placed in numbered GEO (Guild Office) boxes. Quest board is a patchwork of cork boards nailed together over the years. *(Session 7)*

**The saltwater river at X-salent is drying up** — A saltwater river flows through/past X-salent and normally falls into "the pit" (Saltwater Falls). It has recently been stopping ~100 feet before it should. This is the party's primary available quest. Reward: Auto Map Paper (self-updating, but only works in already-mapped areas). *(Session 7)*

**Three quests available at X-salent:**
1. **Drying River** — Investigate why the saltwater river is stopping. Reward: Auto Map Paper. (From Cartographers.)
2. **Gather Ingredients** — Collect Yapon Holly and Persher's Zouan (both mildly poisonous in high doses; found near dense saltwater mist). Drop in GEO Box 7. Reward: an item called "Zahua" in GEO Box 3.
3. **Monster/Desperate Quest** — "I will give you anything. Get rid of the monster. No water is safe. Please, I just want to go home." Author unknown. *(Session 7)*

**Halcyra has been searching for a "chosen hero" from a prophecy she grew up hearing** — She shared this in the X-salent tavern. She grew up feeling judged by how she looked, her wings fell at 18, and she has been following this prophecy ever since. She pulled out the prophecy paper at the end of the session — the party was about to read it. *(Session 7)*

**Reficul made a deal with the devil** — Reficul revealed this to George in the tavern: he cannot find peace, and cannot die, until a job for the devil is complete. He is 1/100th done after Session 7. Grungrak did not make a devil's deal — only Reficul has this mechanic. *(Session 7)*

**Mystery writing is appearing inside people's homes at X-salent** — For approximately 12 days, writing has been found on the interior walls of residences across the city (12 known instances). Written in an unidentified black substance (non-magical in the wall text itself). A second layer of fine, kanji-like magical sigils appears on outer window frames in the same substance — these appear to be how the writer accessed the rooms. No signs of ordinary breaking and entering. The city guard has been investigating with limited success. *(Session 8)*

**The full poem found on the walls:**
> Take care all you sickened, you outcast, you ill. The nightmare surrounds us, its voice high and shrill.
> The dreamers, the doubters, the dead and the dim. The damaged, the drunkard shall all fall to them.
> The blessed shall wail, the vile take cheer, the creature it hungers and draws ever near.
> It scuttles, it slithers, it twists, and decays. The shattered, the splintered, The blessed and defamed.
> It lies in the wood, it lies past the gate. The monster that hunts us, it's lying in wait.
> The blood as it runs, the mind as it fades. We feed it, the shattered, the blessed, the defamed.
*(Collected over 12 incidents; written by Charlie; the DM is proud of it. Session 8)*

**The creature in the poem is in the woods beyond the city gate** — The poem describes something that scuttles, slithers, twists, and decays. It hungers and draws near. It lies in the wood, past the gate. It "feeds" on the shattered, the blessed, and the defamed. The missing merchant insisted they could see it at the edge of the woods. The creature has never been found by any search party. *(Session 8)*

**The poem's language echoes the party's own situation** — "The shattered, the blessed, the defamed" mirrors the party composition: Mira (dead/shattered), Halcyra (the blessed/divine), and potentially others. The language may or may not be coincidental. *(Session 8)*

**Reficul's devil's curse has produced three known transformations** — Bull shark (Session 2), a very tall naked man/true changeling form (Session 6), Oni (Session 8). The Oni form triggered during the slums chaos. Physical/physiological abilities of the form transfer; magical abilities do not. Duration of transformations is unclear. *(Across sessions; Oni confirmed Session 8)*

**X-salent's homeless community is organized and self-protective** — The people living along the outer city wall in the slum district form a de facto community with their own informal leaders. They protect their members from outside harassment. They also appear to have had contact with (or may include) the mysterious figure Reficul tracked — who used them as cover. *(Session 8)*

**The mystery substance is not from SKRP** — This was confirmed through testing at the Alchemy Guild. Its precise origin remains unknown, but one major candidate location has been eliminated. *(Session 9)*

**The mystery substance reacts with Agopyl** — Agopyl is the inner spongy membrane of maple tree stems. It is produced by a condition called the "Syrup Virus" or caused by an unidentifiable mineral called "Woodchip." When the mystery substance contacts Agopyl, it turns gaseous. Adding salt to the gaseous form causes it to re-solidify into a graphite-like solid. Its reaction profile does not match any catalogued material the Alchemy Guild has on record. *(Session 9)*

**The ruins are purely magical in origin and deity-linked** — Pages 35–40 of "Ruins from Ancient Ages" (city library) confirm that the marked ruins are not architectural or geological. They are purely magical and linked to a specific deity. The author of the text deliberately declined to name the deity, noting two possible candidates but refusing to speculate between them. The identity of both candidates is unknown. *(Session 9)*

**The houses covered in writing are actively malevolent** — The Victorian manor in X-salent's wealthy district is not merely a site of strange script — it appears to have an active, hostile intelligence. A dragonborn adventurer inside the manor had his weapon shift shape and his eyes glow red, seemingly possessed by the house. The house screamed audibly when the party fled. A magical aura surrounds these structures. The writing and the malevolent presence may be connected to the deity linked to the ruins. *(Session 9)*

**The YOMI letters glow near divine proximity or heat** — The crossed-out letters spelling "YOMI" from the mysterious bundle of notes began glowing with heat and light when Halqira prayed to Apollo at the temple district. Mira confirmed arcane energy (Arcana roll 12). Two unresolved theories: (1) Apollo responded to Halqira's prayer, or (2) the ink in the letters reacted to the temple's candle heat, similar to how the mystery substance reacts to other stimuli. *(Session 9)*

**A sorcerer in the adventuring party mentioned her warlock companion "just left one day"** — One of the four adventurers encountered in the Victorian manor noted that her warlock party member departed without explanation at some point. This may be connected to larger patron/warlock lore in the campaign. *(Session 9)*

**Salt is "white gold" — only two sources in the world** — Surface oceans on this world are freshwater. Salt is only available from two locations in the entire world. Most people have never cooked with it. It is worth more than gold. X-salent is a destination partly because it sits near saltwater falls — its food has salt. *(Session 10)*

**Velodorn appeared 5 months ago** — A new city called Velodorn appeared in the world approximately 5 months ago — at the same time the world broke and new mountains/volcanoes appeared. Its caravan's goods were coated in the mystery goop upon crossing X-salent's city gate. *(Session 10)*

**The goop substance is unified** — The wall writing, the caravan goods, and the cave creature are all made of the same substance. Mira confirmed via salt test: the cave creature's goop coagulates identically to the wall writing goop and the caravan goop. They are chemically the same material. *(Session 10)*

**The cave creature is a goop construct, not undead** — Halcyra's Divine Sense confirmed it is NOT a celestial, fiend, or undead. Investigation of its remains revealed a partially formed digestive tract (its most developed feature) with nascent muscle fibers dissipating into goop further from the spine. It was a **new construct forming muscles** — built primarily to consume. Invisible before eating; became visible once it had mass. Completely averse to light and fire: screamed in multiple overlapping voices (including drowning sounds) when hit with Burning Hands and desperately tried to put out a phantom fire. On a bite, it drains magical energy — Halcyra lost a level 1 spell slot when she failed a Constitution save. *(Session 10)*

**Wagon runes found on the Velodorn caravan** — Enchanters found runes on the bottom of the Velodorn caravan's wagons. These runes are from the same alphabet family as the ruins runes seen in earlier sessions. Rough translation (the language has never been formally deciphered): sleep, return, catalyst, invite. There is also noise in the translation ("dinosaur cheesecake"). This is distinct from the door/window runes (Sessions 7–8). These two sets are in the same language family but in different physical locations. The wagon runes may have triggered the Transmutation magic when crossing the gate boundary. *(Session 10)*

**Someone or something is making goop constructs in the woods** — George's end-of-session theory, stated aloud. The creature was a construct, not a natural beast. The missing merchant likely encountered it or the maker. The goop also appears on caravan goods from Velodorn (a 5-month-old city). The threads converge on something deliberately creating these constructs. *(Session 10)*

**Reficul's curse has now produced four transformations** — Bull shark (Session 2), very tall naked man/true form (Session 6), Oni (Session 8), kobold (Session 10). As a kobold, he could walk through creatures and other entities, which allowed him to position for the killing blow. The curse forms appear to be random (coin flip) but may have pattern significance. *(Session 10)*

**The island city is ruled through fear and Nightmare-backed authority** — King Mert operates with the backing of Nungus, who presents himself as the prophet of the Nightmare entities (the shadow/psychic constructs encountered in earlier sessions). The regime enforces compliance by hanging dissenters from portcullises with "Do Not Rebel" signs and imprisoning rebels (mostly women, birds, and mermaids) in a dedicated prison building. A small resistance exists and made contact with the party. *(Session 11)*

**Nungus has departed north** — Before the party arrived at the island city, Nungus ("the prophet") had already left, heading north for unknown reasons. His communication system — a waterfall-and-light projector that works like a holographic display ("Star Wars-ing it") — was still functional at the palace. His location and intentions are unknown. *(Session 11)*

**King Mert had a contingency mechanic** — Earlier in the throne room fight, Mert tapped his shoulder — a gesture the party did not immediately recognize as significant. This was him activating a prepared contingency spell. When he was near death (after approximately 250 damage total and mid-castration), he teleported away. Mert is alive, badly wounded, and partially castrated. *(Session 11)*

**Mira's pact weapon is a sickle** — During the King Mert fight, Mira's warlock pact weapon manifested as a sickle without her fully consciously calling it. She used it to partially castrate Mert before he escaped. She is an unknowing warlock — she does not understand what is happening to her or who her patron is. The patron's presence is undeniable at this point. The sickle is notable given that the last major sickle in the campaign was the "Castrator of the Sky" — now in Persephone's possession. Whether there is a connection is unresolved. *(Session 11)*

**The party dealt ~250 damage to King Mert** — The throne room fight accumulated: Searing Smite fire burns (Halcyra), Dissonant Whispers 32 damage (Reficul), Earth Tremor (George), English breakfast blinding (Inquisio), Starry Wisp killing the spectator (Reficul), Thunder Wave wiping 20 guards (George), and sustained melee damage. This is the highest single-encounter damage total the party has accumulated against a named target. *(Session 11)*

**George's Thunder Wave killed approximately 20 palace guards** — A single Thunder Wave cast wiped out a wave of guards that had been summoned into the throne room during the Mert fight. ~20 deaths in one action. This is the largest single-action kill count in the campaign to date and will likely have political consequences in the island city. *(Session 11)*

**Zone of Truth was used on Mert** — After the fight, Mira cast Zone of Truth. The interrogation yielded some information before Mert completed his escape. What was confirmed under the Zone of Truth is partially documented in Session 11 notes. *(Session 11)*

**Mira partially castrated King Mert** — In the throne room, after Zone of Truth, Mira used her pact sickle to partially castrate King Mert. He escaped before it was complete. He is alive and has personal, visceral reasons to pursue the party. This act was deliberate. *(Session 11)*

**Reficul's curse has now produced five transformations** — Bull shark (Session 2), very tall naked man/true form (Session 6), Oni (Session 8), kobold (Session 10), bullywug (Session 11). The bullywug form was active during the island city section of Session 11. Pattern of forms remains unclear. *(Session 11)*

**The party leveled to 6** — All five characters are now level 6. New abilities not yet documented for any character. *(Session 11)*

**The three cobalts quest item** — The party has one of three cobalts. The location of the other two and what all three together accomplish remains unknown. Mert or the Nightmare/Nungus connection may have information about the other cobalts. *(Referenced Session 11)*

*Session 13 lore additions:*

**Kenku voice sacrifice lore** — In this world's history, the kenku (bird people) classically gave their voices to a god. This is the in-world explanation for why kenku in classical D&D can only mimic and not speak original words. The Voxus kenku (Gunge, the chief) can speak because post-Shattering rules are different — worlds merged and the old rules don't fully apply. *(Session 13)*

**Valu / Aeolus — the Wind God** — A god connected to the Thorin statue (one of the four statues from King Pmurt's original kingdom). Described as flighty, ADHD-ish, briefly manifesting before departing again. He gave the party sending stones. His connection to the Thorin statue suggests the four-statues-and-gods mechanic: bringing a statue to its people allows the god inside to manifest or be freed. Valu's personality fits the wind itself — present and gone. *(Session 13)*

**The "four statues = three gods" mechanic is being revised** — The four statues from King Pmurt's kingdom each contain or represent a god. Bringing the Valu statue to the kenku (birds) allowed Valu to manifest. Bringing Thorin's statue to its people may vitalize or release Thorin. The party has confirmed: Valu's statue is distinct from Thorin's statue (different materials, different maker). They are now carrying the Thorin statue north toward Sypium. *(Session 13)*

**Nungus's dream network** — Nungus's actual long-term project is establishing a global network of "disciples of dreams" across the fractured world — spreading dream-power, not building an army of loyal followers. Mert was the first disciple. Nungus was told in his own dreams (by an entity communicating to him while he slept) to free Yomi, who is trapped. He has been acting on those instructions. He may not be the ultimate villain — he may be a zealot following instructions from something larger. *(Session 13 — Dex's testimony)*

**Yomi / God of Dreams is trapped in her own nightmare** — Confirmed again: Yomi (the God of Dreams) is trapped and her nightmare/shadow is what sealed her. Nungus is trying to break this prison. The party suspects his methods are wrong even if his goal might be defensible. Nungus additionally mentioned wanting to "free him" — a second imprisoned entity, distinct from Yomi — suggesting two separate goals. *(Session 13)*

**The Rune Alphabet belongs to Malafera** — The circles-and-clouds rune writing the party has encountered since Session 8 (on building walls, wagon bottoms, the abandoned town murals) is the language of an entity named **Malafera** — Starglass's mother. The runes mean "hello" and "goodbye" to her constructs, and the last word she taught was "life." The goop substance, the constructs, and the rune writing all trace back to Malafera. She has "a lot of children." *(Session 13 — Starglass)*

**Starglass / The Ship is alive** — Mert's airship (named "Starglass") is sentient because **Starglass herself** — a construct daughter of Malafera — breathes for the ship via pipes connecting her to the hull. The Doctor modified her and installed her as the ship's "breathing apparatus." When she gets emotional, the ship changes. She has been in the glass case since the Doctor delivered her to Mert. *(Session 13)*

**The Doctor** — An unknown figure who arrived at Malafera's home, spoke with her, caused violence, then modified Starglass and traded her to Mert in exchange for Mert's crown. He was in contact with Nungus/Derv. Starglass described him as "a different man underneath his skin" — suggesting shapeshifting, dual identity, or possession. His dossier papers on the airship shift mid-document from colonial journal entries to the rune alphabet, and the tone becomes violently hostile toward Sypium. His hat and mask (labeled "dr.l") were found in the airship. *(Session 13)*

**Sypium / Scipium** — The city north of the former island city, past several islands, near mountains. This is where Mert was originally heading, where Nungus is heading, and where Haki and the prophetess are heading. The Doctor's dossier is violently hostile toward it. A cave civilization lives in the far mountains nearby. The next session will take place here. *(Session 13)*

**Alias is in danger** — The pirate captain from the beginning of the campaign is currently beached on her own ship and wounded, fighting something. This is an urgent unresolved threat. *(Session 13 — Ian OOC)*

**The glowing prophetess** — A small girl clad in all white who glows. Everyone supposedly knows her name, but Starglass was never told it. She is being protected by Haki (Starglass's brother) heading north. Her nature suggests divinity or prophecy-adjacent significance. *(Session 13 — Starglass)*

**Dex had a dream-realm experience** — Dex (the 19-year-old chef from Mert's palace) experienced a dream-tavern encounter the night of a major world event — Mira confirmed this means he accidentally entered the God of Dreams' realm through sleep. He has been having vivid, disturbing nightmares since. This is the first confirmed case of an ordinary person accidentally touching the dream realm post-Shattering. *(Session 13)*
