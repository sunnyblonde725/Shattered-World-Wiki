# DND Session 15 — Stuart Returns

---

## TL;DR

The party explored Bermuda Foundation's residential district and found Constance — a seventeen-year-old girl running a manor full of fifteen orphaned children, waiting for her missing brother Iskander. Her locked study held a second grandfather clock, a time-loop slime creature, and THETA — a former judge imprisoned in fog by a "metal individual" — who turned out to be an old ally and a crucial translator of Dr. Larkin's notebook. Then the party took Constance to Canary Park for what was supposed to be a fun festival day: they met Arcus's daughter, George drank an unconscionable amount of whiskey, Gbot'n declared economic war on a tavern, and Stuart the sheep reappeared — now transformed into a fire-breathing dragon possessed by an insect-like scarab on his skull. After the party freed Stuart and stopped a glowing blue clock in the mine ride, a mysterious winged woman fell into their lives, offered them her safehouse, and immediately ran away. The DM called it a beach episode. It was not a beach episode.

---

## Full Summary

### Scene 1 — Bermuda Foundation, Los Alamos District

The party disembarked from Starglass and entered the residential district of Bermuda Foundation — a neighborhood of blue stone buildings that looked like they had been built by someone who had *heard* a city described to them but never seen one. The streets were quiet after the fire they had arrived to find in Session 14.

A seventeen-year-old girl in a yellow dress named **Constance** — "but you can call me Liz" — appeared and hailed them. She had been caring for **fifteen orphaned children** at a large house called **Paradise Manor** nearby. She was looking for her older brother **Iskander**, who had been missing for some time. She was the right kind of seventeen: competent, a little overwhelmed, completely unafraid of the strangers she'd just invited home.

---

### Scene 2 — Paradise Manor

The party followed Constance into Paradise Manor — a large, well-maintained house whose residents were clearly managing without any adult supervision. Fifteen children occupied it at various levels of chaos.

Constance brought the party to a **locked study door** she had never been able to open — three separate locks of different types. The party investigated and discovered each lock required a musical activation:

- **Lock 1:** A carved flute on the door — George played it and chanted
- **Lock 2:** A carving of Mira's exact face [NEEDS CONFIRMATION] — required a falsetto vocal match
- **Lock 3:** A dragon carving — Inquizo pressed his tongue against it

All three performed simultaneously. The locks disengaged.

---

### Scene 3 — The Fog Study

Inside was a fog-filled room. At its center: a **second grandfather clock** — different from the one aboard Starglass. The fog contained a **slime creature** that cycled through forms: slime → skeleton → a naked middle-aged man with a bewildered expression.

The man was **THETA** — a former judge the party had encountered in an earlier session. He had been trapped in the study, his form deteriorating through the time loop, for an indeterminate period. He was not hostile. He was, in fact, relieved.

Under **Zone of Truth**, Theta revealed:
- He had been **tasked to protect a child** — named **Ariel**, sometimes called **"Byly"**
- He was connected to **Millaphera** (the construct-maker whose history has been shadowing the campaign)
- He had been locked in the study by a **"metal individual"** — identity not confirmed, but the description points toward one of Millaphera's automatons or someone associated with the Doctor
- He had knowledge of the **Deep language** — the script found in Larkin's notebook

Theta translated the **second half of Dr. Larkin's notebook** (the part written in Deep, which the party could not previously read): it described an **expedition that killed Millaphera's family** — and how that loss was the origin point for the automatons that have been following the party since Session 8.

Also in the study: a **drawer of undelivered letters** between Iskander and Liz (Constance). Neither had ever received them. The party read them. [FULL CONTENT IN DOCUMENTS FOUND GOOGLE DOC]

Separately, **Starglass** provided the party with a **translation key** for the Deep's cloud language — a written cipher she had stored from an earlier era.

---

### Scene 4 — Canary Park

Constance offered to take the party to a festival event she knew about: **Canary Park** — an amusement park at the base of a mountain range that was apparently built by the **Tempest family** and Bermuda Foundation as a "humanitarian effort to make existence a little more fun." Birds of every kind filled the sky above it. Roller coasters. A Ferris wheel that was half underground. Shooting ranges and fortune teller tents. Students in uniform.

The ticket booth was staffed by an **old man with googly-eye glasses** who said: *"The deeper you go, the less the world belongs to you."* He smiled while pocketing an extremely generous overpayment.

---

### Scene 5 — The Eustace and Alia

Before reaching the festival proper, the party passed **The Eustace** — a community of people who can only eat bread for sustenance (everything else passes through them without nourishing). They grow vegetables they can taste but not absorb. They grow grain because the bread keeps them alive. It was functional and strangely peaceful.

In The Eustace, the party met **Alia** — the daughter of **Arcus** (the lighthouse keeper from the island in an earlier session). She had been brought here by travelers when her island became dangerous. She was building a boat to go back and visit her father. She did not know the way and was planning to consult **"The COG"** — a place with cartographers.

She gave Halcyra a set of **wooden pan pipes** she had made herself. When Halcyra played them, **three strangers appeared** — all of whom introduced themselves as "Aspen." They joined the party's walk to the park. (These Aspens remained present through the mine ride and were deployed by Halcyra to help stop the clock.) ### Scene 6 — The Tavern (No Name)

The party found an unnamed tavern inside Canary Park — a building made to look like it was constructed from canoes. Diverse clientele. A half-elf and a halfling were having a competition in one corner. Gambling in another. Children discussing politics in a third.

**Gbot'n the telepathic pug** arrived, stood on the table, and declared: *"This is the place that put me in ruin. I will destroy it by economic downturn. I will create the finest restaurant the world has ever seen."* He then floated out the window before Mira could finish explaining that Constance's manor was full of children who didn't know how to cook, and that Dex was on the ship and could teach. [BORDERLINE PLAN — the offer may or may not have registered with Gbot'n before he left]

**George** (Grungrak) engaged in:
- Three consecutive whiskey-bottle-drinking contests vs. a **man in Gambison armor named Anson** — CON saves: 19, 16, 16 (with advantage). He won all three, earning approximately 400 gold.
- A wrestling match vs. Anson — George used Cutting Words (Bardic Inspiration penalty of 8 to Anson's roll), won by a tie-break. Earned another 100 gold.
- Total: very drunk, very rich, wrestling champion of an unnamed tavern.

**Inquizo** received a kid's meal. The prize was a **plastic canary toy** — press the beak and it flaps its wings. He was thrilled.

Liz returned with **cotton candy** for Mira.

---

### Scene 7 — The Mine Ride (Ikinawai's Gentle Descent)

The party boarded the **Ikinawai's Gentle Descent** — a mine train that ran through the mountain. The tour guide's voice described the history of Canary Park and the Tempest family. They were instructed to tie themselves in with rope.

The train derailed. The guide said: *"Oh no, it looks like the carts are separating. What a pity. Good luck."* George threw up immediately. The vomit arc hit a stranger sitting next to Liz; Liz dodged; the stranger knocked himself out. Liz — now alone in her cart — found herself on a diverging track and rode straight down. The rest of the party continued forward.

At the end of the track: a **glowing blue clock** — the third grandfather clock the party has now encountered. Skeletons began dropping from a hole above, and then **STUART** appeared: the green-skinned, red-eyed, finger-legged sheep from the Session 5 cult encounter, now transformed into a **sheep-dragon** with fire breath, wings growing from his wool, and a **glowing scarab** attached to his skull by what appeared to be an organic tendon.

**Halcyra** cut the scarab off Stuart's skull with her sword (STR check 13 → success with cutting action). Stuart was freed — the dragon features receded. The skeletons that had been attacking **immediately stopped and became neutral** when the scarab was removed, which suggests they were controlled by the same mechanism.

**Mira** used **Mage Hand** to stop the second hand of the clock. **George** cast **Plant Growth** — vines grew into the clock's interior and caused the clock face to fall off. A cuckoo popped out of the cuckoo door. The clock stopped.

**Inquizo** flew to the clock and grabbed the hour hand — was spun in circles repeatedly but functioned as a brake, slowing it enough for two of the now-docile skeletons to help stop it completely.

Two skeletons — confirmed neutral once the scarab was gone — helped hold the clock still. They then dissolved. Stuart also vanished once freed.

---

### Scene 8 — The Winged Woman

After the clock stopped, the party discovered **a woman hanging off a railing** over a large pit. She was pulled up by Halcyra — STR check 13, success.

The woman: metal armor, black hair (heavily dandruff'd), **orange eyes**, painter's cap, small locket necklace around her neck. She sprouted wings — used them to fly the party (those who couldn't fly) up out of the mine — and then ran away immediately.

Before running: she mentioned she had a **safehouse in Los Alamos** (the district where Paradise Manor is), that she had left "a trail behind her" when she was adopted, and that she had **promised to look after people when she split up with "him"** — identity of "him" unclear, potentially Iskander or the Doctor or someone else entirely.

She also explained that some **reporters** had been coming to the manor, so she had decided to be "her own reporter" on herself to deflect them from the children in her care. This suggests she is a known figure, possibly with a public record.

---

### Scene 9 — Outside the Ride

Liz had been outside waiting for **two hours**. The party had been inside for approximately **ten minutes**. More time distortion — consistent with the red sky events in Session 14 and the clock patterns throughout the campaign.

The party debriefed, noted that grandfather clocks keep appearing everywhere and seem to cause time to go wrong, and agreed to head back to Paradise Manor and then to the winged woman's safehouse. Session ended as the group began walking in that direction.

## Lore & Worldbuilding

- **The Eustace** — A community within Bermuda Foundation where residents can only be nourished by bread. They grow other food they enjoy but cannot absorb. The group was brought here when their island became too dangerous to live on.
- **Millaphera's origin** — Theta's translation confirms: an expedition (associated with the Doctor or his predecessors) **killed Millaphera's family**. The automatons that have been following the party since Session 8 are a consequence of that loss.
- **The scarab possession mechanism** — Stuart the sheep had a glowing scarab attached to his skull by an organic tendon, which was causing his transformation and aggression. Galveston had an insect in his eye (Session 14). The Doctor is described as "a different man underneath his skin." This is a pattern: possession or parasitic control via insect/scarab, consistent across multiple NPCs.
- **Grandfather clocks = time anomalies** — Three encountered: Starglass's ship clock, Paradise Manor's study clock, and the mine clock. Each is associated with time disruption. They appear where the party arrives. Losing Reficul (who turned the first clock) and now being "haunted by grandfather clocks" was noted by the table.
- **Canary Park lore** — Built by the Tempest family in partnership with Bermuda Foundation as a humanitarian project. Students from a nearby institution attend regularly. The old ticket booth man's googly-eye glasses matched the googly eyes on the "leader" skeleton in the mine — connection unexplained.
- **Ikinawai's Gentle Descent** — The mine ride's full name as heard over the radio. Not gentle.
- **The COG** — Mentioned by Alia as a place with cartographers; she plans to visit to get a map to her father's island.

## Cliffhangers & Unresolved Threads

- **Who is the winged woman?** — Orange eyes, painter's cap, locket, metal armor, adoption trail. Has a safehouse. Made a promise to someone when she and "him" split up. Reporters were coming to the manor. She is a known figure and currently fleeing.
- **Where is Stuart now?** — Freed from the scarab, reverted to sheep form, vanished. Is he okay? Does he remember the dragon?
- **What is the scarab?** — Organic tendon, glowing, possessed Stuart. Third instance of the insect-possession pattern (Galveston's eye, the Doctor's "different man underneath," Stuart's skull).
- **What are the Aspens?** — Summoned by pan pipes Alia made and gave to Halcyra. Three people who appear when played. No explanation given. Possibly divine/magical in origin. Helped stop a clock.
- **Where are the undelivered letters?** — The party read the letters between Iskander and Liz. What do they say? Does Constance now know? Is Iskander alive?
- **Where is Iskander?** — His study was locked from outside. His letters were in a drawer. He has not been found.
- **The Tempest family** — Built Canary Park. Name has weight in context of this world. Connection to the party or the Doctor?
- **The Googly-Eye Connection** — The ticket booth man had googly-eye glasses. The "leader" skeleton in the mine had the same googly eyes. Is this the same entity? A manifestation?
- **Time keeps getting broken** — 10 minutes inside the mine = 2 hours outside. Red sky + clock = time reversal (Session 14). More clocks keep appearing. Is something actively manipulating time?
