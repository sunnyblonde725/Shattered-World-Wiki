## Inquizo

**Player:** Charlie | **Race:** Kobold | **Class:** Rogue (Thief subclass) | **From:** SKRP

---

### Who He Is

Inquizo is chaos incarnate. He presses every button, claims every hat, releases spiders at strategic moments, and can read but chooses not to — playing dumb about it with zero shame. He has the highest confirmed Persuasion roll in the campaign (24), used to argue that his jar of spiders was legally part of his body. He once turned the clock hands counter-clockwise and accidentally reversed time. He threw a glowing blue clock off a bridge and turned the sky red. These are not unrelated events.

He carries a black orb with an unidentified entity inside. He stole the core of George's broken hammer and still hasn't given it back. He saw a smaller version of himself reaching out from the River Lethe and ignored it. His shadow — the Warden Brother — told him he was failing his sister, then wrapped chains around his wrists and merged with him as a permanent reminder. He woke up from that muttering "I'm sorry" over and over, and Mira picked him up like a cat and held him until it passed.

He is hard to read and easy to underestimate. His stealth roll was 28 during the palace infiltration. His Persuasion is near-perfect. He accumulates hats with the focus of someone pursuing a divine mission, and something about all of it feels narratively intentional.

---

### How He Fights

Inquizo is a Rogue built around stealth, surprise, and creative improvisation. He hits precisely when he needs to and disappears when he doesn't.

**Damage & Offense**
- **Sneak Attack** — 3d6 extra damage with a finesse or ranged weapon when he has advantage or an ally is adjacent
- **Cunning Strike** — after Sneak Attack: can inflict Poison (CON save), Paralyze, or force the target to move away
- **Chains of Eternity** — shackle bracelets; he can launch them to restrain a target, but he is restrained at the same time (Marley-style); used on Galveston in Session 14 (14 damage taken while restrained); up to proficiency bonus uses
- **Flight** — used to dive at the blood angel in Session 5 and rip out the final nail
- **Improvised weapons** — threw an English breakfast at King Pmurt and blinded him briefly in Session 11

**Stealth & Utility**
- **Invisibility** — used repeatedly; notably invisible for an hour during the underworld gatehouse (Session 6); invisible while following the kidnappers in Session 16; Persephone saw through it with Truesight
- **Cunning Action** — bonus action Dash, Disengage, or Hide; rolled Stealth 24 during the Galveston fight
- **Stealth 28** — highest stealth roll of Session 11; critical for the palace infiltration
- **Uncanny Dodge** — reaction: halve damage from one attack per round
- **Steady Aim** — bonus action to gain advantage on next attack (can't move that turn)
- **Voice mimic** — imitated Pmurt's voice on the airship to direct the ship to "Sypium" (Session 13); exact source of ability uncertain
- **Chandelier lever** — pulled the lever to drop the throne room chandelier in Session 11; coordinated with Halcyra's positioning
- **Turned the clock back (Session 14)** — grabbed the wildly spinning grandfather clock hands below deck; turned them counter-clockwise; time reversed to before the Galveston combat; red sky cleared; Galveston and his Dobermans walked off with no memory of the fight; the party kept their memories
- **Stopped the mine clock (Session 15)** — grabbed the hour hand and held on; was spun in circles; functioned as a brake combined with Mira's Mage Hand and two docile skeletons

**Social**
- Persuasion 24 — argued that his jar of spiders was legally part of his body
- Deception 9 (expertise) — routinely passes as an "exotic pet" when convenient
