# Reficul's Death Counter

*He has a deal with the devil. The deal says he cannot truly die until the job is done. He is one hundredth done. The job has not been named.*

*Current count: **5***

---

> *"A long time ago I made a deal with the devil. I've got a job to do for him, and until that job is complete, I am not allowed peace. Until my job is done, I cannot be allowed to die."*
> — Reficul, to George, Session 7

---

## Death #1
**Session:** 3 — See You in Hell
**Cause:** Self-inflicted. Mid-combat with Queen Ariel, he stabbed himself and announced his intentions clearly.
**Last words:** *"See you in hell."*
**Where he woke up:** Hell. Specifically the Fields of Asphodel, where he found a boat, an inaccurate map, and apparently spent several sessions having what he later described as "a bit of a punishment, slightly" and what he told George was a "vacation."
**Notes:** Mira performed a medicine check of 21 on his body and could not clinically confirm cause of death — but the stab wound was the cause. His changeling true form was revealed in death: approximately three feet tall, fiendish horns, long white hair, blood shifting green to blue with temperature. Nobody had seen this before.

---

## Death #2
**Session:** 6 — The Eel That Sings
**Cause:** Rolled a 1 on the acrobatics check jumping off a crashing boat. The hull landed on him.
**Last words:** Unknown — he didn't have time.
**Where he woke up:** The black sand beach of Persephone's shrine, erupting from the sand as an entirely different body: approximately seven feet tall, extremely naked, and very much aware of this fact. He picked up his own dead changeling child form, threw it into the River Lethe, fashioned his white robes into a loincloth, and turned to Grungrak.
**Notes:** His first words in the new body were *"I'm on vacation, baby."* Grungrak was furious. The horse was fine — it is metal and doesn't have this problem. The turkey was not fine. The turkey died with the boat and did not come back. Reficul did.

---

## Death #3
**Session:** 6 — The Eel That Sings
**Cause:** Swan dived into Persephone's soul pool to retrieve Eric. The soul pool is not water. It is a swirling mass of souls. This was an extremely bad plan that he executed immediately and without hesitation.
**Last words:** Unknown — he was diving.
**Where he woke up:** The floor of Persephone's shrine, shaking his dice.
**Notes:** He died, came back, and dived again. See Death #4.

---

## Death #4
**Session:** 6 — The Eel That Sings
**Cause:** Swan dived into Persephone's soul pool a second time. Still not water. Still a swirling mass of souls. Still a bad plan. He was now on a different set of dice.
**Last words:** Unknown.
**Where he woke up:** The floor of Persephone's shrine, this time with Eric flopping onto the stone beside him.
**Notes:** The different dice worked. Eric was retrieved. Persephone snapped her fingers and Eric could speak. His first words: *"Hello. My name is George."* Then: *"Where is Ariel?"* Reficul's persistence across two consecutive deaths retrieved a ghost husband from a pool of souls so he could reunite briefly with his wife before they both forgot each other in the River Lethe. Whether this counts as a win is a matter of perspective.

---

## Death #5
**Session:** 7 — The Gate of Ivory
**Cause:** A boat crashed on him. Again. He appeared at the Gate of Ivory beach after making it out of hell, had approximately one second to deliver what was clearly going to be important tactical information, and a flying boat landed on him.
**Last words:** *"Crush the—"*
**Where he woke up:** The same beach, beard significantly shorter. d4 roll for luck: 2. The bow missed his face by a hair and took the beard with it. He went on "vacation" again.
**Notes:** His voice had already been stolen by Urscylla and was orbiting her as a glowing yellow orb by the time this happened. Grungrak caught the orb mid-combat. It said: *"George, George — big run. Heave."* This instruction, delivered by a disembodied stolen voice, was instrumental in defeating Urscylla. The beard did not grow back immediately. It is unclear when it grew back. He did not comment on this.

## The Rules (As Best We Understand Them)

- No death saving throws — when he hits 0 HP, he's dead
- He goes to hell
- He comes back
- His true changeling form is revealed in death: small (~3 ft), fiendish horns, long white hair, temperature-shifting blood
- He is 1/100th done with the job
- Nobody knows what the job is
- His clothes have twice been waiting for him when he returned — once as white robes on the black sand beach, once already packed in a chest inside Persephone's shrine. Someone put them there. Nobody knows who.
- He would rather not talk about it. *"I would rather forget myself dying."*
