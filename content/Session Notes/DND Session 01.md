## TL;DR

Five strangers meet in a tavern, spend a morning causing chaos at a carnival, get kidnapped by fog-cloaked figures, wake up shipwrecked, encounter a comically misogynistic and isolated kingdom, get shaken down by beautiful pirate women, and steal a god's statue using it as a battering ram — all in one session.

## Full Summary

### The Tavern — Where Everyone Meets

The session opened in a tavern. Three groups happened to converge there:

- **Mira and Inquizio** were simply passing through the area — no specific destination, just traveling.
- **George (Grungrak), Reficul, and Halcyra** were on an active quest to recover George's memories. Reficul had found George in a cave (the circumstances of *why* Reficul was in that cave remain unexplained). Halcyra had witnessed Reficul's morally questionable approach to guiding George and inserted herself — a freshly-18-year-old paladin who decided she could fix this.

George was doing what George does: singing at the bar, spectacularly drunk. Very much the same energy he still carries session to session.

The inciting incident: Inquizio, invisible at the time, tried to steal the rock George was carrying. The rock was not invisible. A floating rock is, it turns out, very noticeable. Reficul — who can already see Inquizio due to his true sight, which lets him see through invisibility — spotted him immediately. Inquizio is invisible to everyone except Reficul. Mira, across the tavern, noticed the floating rock and recognized it, or perhaps recognized George because of the commotion around it.

Mira walked over and said: **"Grungrak, is that you?"**

George looked at her and said: **"No. My name is George."**

On the spot. The name George was born in a tavern, deflecting a woman from his past, while drunk. Little did anyone know what that name would carry.

Inquizio, meanwhile, had retreated upstairs and committed his first legendary act: **he stole every pillow in the inn** — specifically the ones from the shared room with Mira. All of them.

### The Carnival — Chaos, Cotton Candy, and Chosen Ones

The topic of a carnival came up. George didn't remember ever going to one (which isn't surprising, given everything). Reficul thought this sounded like exactly the right kind of distraction. They all slept and went the next morning.

Charlie ran the carnival sequence. The thoroughfare was full of games and stalls. The big top tent dominated the far end.

**Inquizio immediately disappeared and stole a truly irresponsible amount of cotton candy**, then wandered the carnival as a bundle of floating cotton candy, visible to everyone. He was, per Charlie's assessment, "a dumb little guy" at this point — this was before he got smarter. Reficul could see him. Inquizio noticed Reficul noticing him. Inquizio ran.

The rest of the party found the **hammer strength game** — the bell-ringing strongman test. There was already a crowd: a semicircle of dwarves, arms folded, watching. Classic. Every dwarf and party member who tried it failed. Each failed dwarf joined the half-circle. It kept growing.

Then a small child walked up with their parents. The party's perception checks caught the carny quietly reaching behind the machine and clicking a hidden button. The child grabbed a hammer far too large for them, staggered under its weight, and let it fall. The hammer landed. The bell rang. The entire crowd erupted.

The dwarves **picked up the child and started chanting "Chosen One"**, running through the carnival, carrying this child aloft through the crowd. Reficul, as Reficul does, immediately began **directing the chaos** — leading the charge, getting dwarves up the trapeze ladders, orchestrating an entirely unscheduled circus performance. **Nat 20 performance roll.** Human cannonball dwarves. Spectacular whim-dizzy in the big top tent.

Meanwhile he used **Minor Illusion** to swap the child out of the flock before returning them safely to their bewildered parents, who were by then just following their kid in a stroller.

Inquizio, still visible via floating cotton candy, got caught by Reficul's pointing. He ran and ducked into the big top — Reficul followed with the entire flock of dwarves. Chaos continued. No one was scheduled to do any of this.

### The Kidnapping

A **red fog** began rolling in from the audience sections. **Hooded, cloaked figures** emerged and walked into the center of the big top. The PCs felt themselves growing heavy, consciousness ebbing — that movie-style in-and-out-of-awareness feeling. Flashes: a boat, a storm, hands on a railing, darkness. Then a beach.

They woke up **shipwrecked**. Some of their gear had been scattered across the sand — they had to find it. The beach curved around a corner out of sight, and a set of stone stairs led up and out of view in the other direction.

The entire party chose the stairs.

### King Pmurt's Kingdom — Stairs, Statues, and Stunted Worldviews

At the top of the stairs: a **gatehouse**. Guards. Cooper took over as DM here.

The guards immediately made it clear they didn't speak to women. Mira attempted diplomacy. It did not go well. Reficul stepped forward.

In conversation, the party learned the following about this place:
- **Only humans exist.** Other races, creatures, peoples — none of them are real here.
- **Only this kingdom exists.** The entire world, as far as this kingdom is concerned, is this kingdom.
- The king is **King Pmurt**, and he is in charge.

Reficul, delighted, asked to meet him. The guards shrugged and let them through.

The party entered in various states of disguise:
- Halcyra found a trench coat fast. Just Halcyra in the trench coat.
- Inquizio passed as a pet — "a dude, their pet lizard, the tiny human, and a woman."
- Inquizio was invisible again (naturally).

**King Pmurt** received them in his throne room. He complained about annoying fish. Annoying birds. Annoying people being annoying. He was too large to move comfortably and was installed in a floating chair. He dispatched them to his vizier and floated away.

*(Note: Cooper confirmed later that Pmurt could move somewhat — the floating chair was preference, not necessity.)*

### Durf Numgis — A Deeply Uncomfortable Hallway

The party was walked down a hallway to meet **Vizier Durf Numgis**. He began sharing his worldview: everything — all of it — is related to sex. Everything.

This was particularly uncomfortable for **Mira** and for **Halcyra** (freshly 18). What started as an uncomfortable conversation escalated into a full war of words between Reficul and Numgis.

Numgis responded by casting a **Nightmare Spell** on the party.

**Results:**
- **Inquizio and Reficul** resisted.
- **George** went catatonic — mumbling about lightning and pain. His amnesia and trauma ran too deep for a nightmare to reach him; what reached him was already real.
- **Reficul's vision:** He found himself before the **Duke of Hells**, who demanded to know what he was doing and gave him a full dressing-down. Spiders crawled over him. In the vision, to end it, he was forced to call Numgis *mommy*.

Numgis then led them to a **balcony** where his **floating ship** was docked. He offered to take them somewhere aboard it.

The party said no. Emphatically. They jumped off the balcony railing.

Reficul and Halcyra used **slow fall** and **wings** respectively to land safely. The party touched down on the other beach — the one around the corner they had ignored when they first woke up. Little did they know, if they'd gone that way first, they would have ended up here anyway.

### The Pirate Ladies — A New Deal

On this second beach: **a crew of very attractive pirate women**.

They wanted something from inside King Pmurt's kingdom — and the people who had just fallen out of the sky were exactly the group to get it for them. Their pitch was simple: retrieve what they want, or they will shoot the party with **very large cannons**.

The party agreed and headed back up.

*(Memorable moment: Inquizio was talking during the pirate negotiations. Someone asked "What are you supposed to be?" He answered: "Yes, quiet." He was not quiet. He had simply misunderstood the question as being about himself.)*

### The Heist — Statues, Prisoners, and a Butcher

In the dead of night, the party devised a plan. They split up:

- **Mira** voluntarily turned herself in as a prisoner — getting herself inside the kingdom walls since Numgis already disliked her and wouldn't expect her to surrender.
- **Inquizio** was sent after Mira to break her out.
- **George and Halcyra** snuck over the wall into the town square. Four **statues** stood in the square. They grabbed the one with a **fishtail**, hoisted it, and used it as a **battering ram** to burst open the city gates and drag it down to the beach.
- **Reficul** used **Heat Metal** to melt some of the castle's gold-plated decorations. It didn't do much — a lot of metal, not much heat — but during the chaos that followed, a **butcher** ran up beside him and said quietly: *"There are some of us who agree with you,"* then disappeared back into the crowd.
- **Inquizio** found Mira and helped her escape through a hole in the wall.

The full party regrouped on the beach. The pirate women were **very pleased** to see the statue.

*"Oh good — now we have another job for you."*

That's where Session 1 ended.

## Character Moments

**Halcyra**
- Freshly 18 years old, inserted herself into Reficul and George's situation after seeing his "morally questionable approach" to guiding an amnesiac. Wore a trench coat to pass through Pmurt's kingdom without her wings being visible.

**Grungrak / George**
- **Gets his name.** Mira asks "Grungrak, is that you?" George says "No. My name is George." A throwaway deflection that will define the campaign.
- **George in the Nightmare.** He goes catatonic instead of dreaming — muttering about lightning and pain. Even a Nightmare spell can't reach through what he's already carrying.

**Reficul**
- **Directs the dwarf circus.** Nat 20 performance, completely unplanned, in service of chaos. Human cannonball dwarves in the big top.
- **Has to call Numgis mommy.** This is the Duke of Hells' punishment for being in this situation. Humiliating and extremely him.

**Inquizo**
- **Steals all the pillows.** His very first act as a party member is to go upstairs and steal the inn's bedding — specifically from the shared room with Mira. No explanation given.

**Mira**
- Recognized Grungrak in the tavern despite his disguise as George. Handled diplomacy with Pmurt's kingdom (even when the guards refused to speak to her). Voluntarily surrendered as a prisoner to get inside the kingdom for the heist.

**Other**
- **The butcher.** One person in that whole kingdom knows something is wrong. They found exactly the right stranger to tell.

## Lore & Worldbuilding Discoveries

- **King Pmurt's kingdom** believes only humans exist and only their kingdom exists — total cultural and cosmological isolation.
- **The town square has four statues** — at least one has a fishtail (associated with sea deity iconography). The pirates wanted at least one of them.
- **A butcher in the kingdom sympathizes with the party's disruption** — there are dissidents in this isolated human kingdom.
- **Reficul has a standing relationship with the Duke of Hells** — manifested as a vision during the Nightmare spell. The Duke knows him. This is not a new connection.
- **George's trauma precedes the campaign** — even a Nightmare can't fabricate something worse than what he carries. Lightning. Pain. He mutters it while catatonic.
- **Hooded figures with red fog** transported the party from the carnival to the beach — the mechanism and the faction behind this have not been identified.
