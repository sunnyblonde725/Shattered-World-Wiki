# Campaign Wiki — Ideas & Options

*Charlie had the idea of making a wiki for the campaign. This is a note of what Claude suggested so you can talk it through with him.*

---

## Why Obsidian is Good for This

Obsidian is basically built for wiki-style knowledge bases. The `[[double bracket links]]` work exactly like Wikipedia's internal links, and Obsidian has a **Graph View** that draws a visual map of everything connected to everything else — click any node and it shows you what links to it and from it. It gets really cool as the campaign grows.

## What a Wiki Structure Could Look Like

```
A Shattered World/
├── Wiki/
│   ├── Home.md              ← landing page with navigation
│   ├── NPCs/
│   │   ├── Nungus.md
│   │   ├── King Mert.md
│   │   ├── Arcus.md
│   │   └── ...one page per NPC
│   ├── Locations/
│   │   ├── Voxus.md
│   │   ├── X-salent.md
│   │   └── ...one page per place
│   ├── Factions/
│   │   ├── The Liberators.md
│   │   └── ...
│   └── Lore/
│       ├── The Shattering.md
│       ├── The Prophecy.md
│       └── ...
```

The Character Profiles and Session Notes are already wiki-ready (each one is its own page). The big work would be splitting NPCs and Locations into individual pages instead of the big list files they currently live in.

---

## What Makes It Feel Like a Real Wiki

Individual pages that link to each other. Instead of a big NPC list, you'd have a page for Nungus that says:

> Nungus visited **[[Voxus]]** two days before the party arrived, meeting with **[[Ravalli]]** about a sleep system connected to **[[The God of Dreams]]**.

Clicking any of those takes you to that page. The Graph View then shows a web of connections.

---

## The Practical Tradeoff

Splitting everything into individual pages is more work upfront. The suggestion was a **hybrid approach**:

- **Major NPCs** (Nungus, Mert, Arcus, Gunge, Ravalli, etc.) → individual wiki pages
- **Minor/one-scene NPCs** → stay as list entries
- **Locations** → individual pages (they accumulate lore fast)
- **Lore topics** → individual pages (The Shattering, the Prophecy, factions)

## Sharing Options

Obsidian itself isn't easy to share unless you have Obsidian Publish (a paid add-on). Three alternatives worth knowing about:

### Notion
- Free, no account needed to view a shared link
- Clean, easy to read, works great for wikis
- Downside: separate from Obsidian — you'd be maintaining two places

### World Anvil
- Built specifically for DND/worldbuilding wikis
- Has dedicated pages for NPCs, locations, factions, maps, timelines
- Free tier with public sharing
- Downside: its own whole tool to learn; moving content from Obsidian is manual

### Quartz (Obsidian → free website) ⭐ Best long-term option
- Turns your Obsidian notes into a real website automatically
- Keeps all the `[[links]]` working, includes graph view, looks great
- Hosted free on GitHub
- **The catch:** requires one-time setup — this is a job for Dad
- Once it's running, updating the wiki = just updating Obsidian notes like normal

**Bottom line:** If Dad is willing to help set up Quartz once, that's the cleanest long-term option. If that's too much, Notion is the easiest starting point.
