## Description

A cluster of rotting wooden buildings slowly being swallowed by fungus. The crop field beside it is as empty as the buildings — nothing has grown here in a long time. Inside one of the buildings: a nursery with an empty crib. Outside the buildings: murals painted on the exterior walls — a winged figure descending, and nails digging into the ground and forcing it upward. Inside the walls: more murals, these ones darker. Giant glowing horns. A black-winged angelic being.

It's not just abandoned. It's the kind of place that tells a story with its silence.

---

## Significance

Three major items came out of this town:

- **The jade fragment** — Inscribed in Infernal as the "9th accent," with the word "Birth"
- **The topaz gem** — Carved with a man's face, Celestial inscription
- **The black orb** — Caelus's prison; a sky deity locked inside a small dark sphere

The murals tell backstory: this was once a community tied to an agricultural agreement — a deal with divine powers governing crop cycles and, apparently, children. When the seed cycle broke, the children who should have grown up here were taken. The empty crib says everything the murals don't.
