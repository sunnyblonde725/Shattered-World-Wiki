## Appearance & Personality

A sentient pine cone. Face made from bark, with pine bristles arranged into a hat-like formation on top. Pronouns: they/Penopolis — that is, "Penopolis" is used as their own pronoun (e.g., "Penopolis thinks that..."). They run the tavern at X-salent with a warmth that is unusual for a tavern run by a pine cone.

They serve Grungrak two pints maximum (guild rules by species). They wrapped Reficul's magical stew in cloth to take away — an Arcana check on the stew returned: magic detected, "probably nothing." They asked the party about their group dynamic. They know about the world ending, the lightning arcs, and the land changes post-Shattering, and they spoke about it with calm curiosity rather than alarm.

Their observation about the party: *"A lot of running about each other and connectivity"* seems to be the theme.

---

## Relationship to the Party

A warm and dependable presence in a campaign that doesn't have many of those. They served Grungrak pickle juice the morning after he passed out drunk on stage and slept there all night. They ask questions that land. They are not part of any plot — they are simply good at what they do and worth returning to.
