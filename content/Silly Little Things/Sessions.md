# Sessions

*17 sessions. A shattered world. Everything went wrong in the best possible way.*

---

## Session 1 — The Carnival Trick
**Tagline:** Five strangers meet, get kidnapped by fog before they even learn each other's names, and accidentally start a revolution in a kingdom that doesn't believe other people exist.
**Best moment:** A small child barely lifts a hammer, the rigged bell rings anyway, and a flock of dwarves lifts them above the crowd chanting "CHOSEN ONE" — then Reficul nat 20 Performance-rolls an entirely unscheduled human cannonball dwarf circus in the big top.
**Where it left them:** On a beach, handing a stolen god-statue to very pleased pirate women, who immediately announce there's a second job.

## Session 2 — The City Below
**Tagline:** A submarine full of buttons, an underwater kingdom that hates men and birds, and a merman guard who just needs someone to tell him the contract he signed with his legs is illegal.
**Best moment:** Inquizo presses every button on the control panel, flooding the cabin, sending Grungrak through the floor hatch, forcing Reficul to transform into a bull shark to save him — and then getting locked outside. All in about four minutes.
**Where it left them:** Standing in an underwater throne room after delivering a diplomatic report to the red-haired mermaid queen, who thanks them by declaring combat.

## Session 3 — See You in Hell
**Tagline:** The party kills a queen, frees a city's voice, and quietly receives the information that one of them has been dead the whole time.
**Best moment:** Reficul stabs himself mid-combat, says "see you in hell," and collapses — his true form revealed as a small three-foot demon child with fiendish horns and color-shifting blood. Mira performs a medicine check of 21. Cause of death: the stab wound.
**Where it left them:** In a field with glowing white strings where Mira moves at one-fifteenth normal speed and a voice tells her she's not supposed to be here.

## Session 4 — The Blood Angel
**Tagline:** The party descends into hell through a temple full of sheep, wields a sentient sickle named Vengeance, and meets an angel that is only half of something and already has their number.
**Best moment:** The blood angel announces to Mira, "I have a number. It's 8. And I'm not alone" — then spikes itself in the chest, neck snapping back, and falls into the blood.
**Where it left them:** Ankle-deep in hell's floor, angel defeated, Mira having forgotten something she can never get back, and Phase Two combat promised for next session.

## Session 5 — The Fields of Asphodel
**Tagline:** Two companions die, the party escapes a drug cult through a doorway Mira slices in half with a borrowed weapon, and the first friendly face they see in hell is standing in a wheat field surrounded by bisected bodies.
**Best moment:** Mira grabs the sickle, asks "Vengeance, do you want to tear a hole through reality?" — and cuts the portal in half lengthwise. The cult members running through it end up in matching top and bottom halves. Stewart the sheep walks under it unharmed because he is simply too short.
**Where it left them:** In the Fields of Asphodel, staring at a farmer who introduces himself as Reficul and is completely unbothered by the pile of corpses at his feet.

## Session 6 — The Eel That Sings
**Tagline:** The party reunites with Reficul in hell, fights a horse with an axe, negotiates with a twelve-foot goddess, and nearly loses everyone to the river that makes you forget who you are.
**Best moment:** Reficul dies when the boat capsizes, erupts from the black sand as a seven-foot naked man, throws his own dead changeling child body into the River Lethe, fashions white robes into a loincloth, and announces to Grungrak: "I'm on vacation, baby."
**Where it left them:** On Charon's boat heading toward the Gate of Ivory, with Urscylla's six eel-heads surfacing on both sides simultaneously, and the sickle no longer in their possession.

## Session 7 — The Gate of Ivory
**Tagline:** The party fights a sea witch, Grungrak rolls a nat 20 to push Sisyphus's boulder over the cliff for the first time in history, and they walk out of hell into a guild town where Mira used to be a member.
**Best moment:** Grungrak tackles Charon into the River Lethe, sinks headfirst, meets a duplicate of himself, grabs his own hand with a nat 20 — and the result is labeled, simply, "self-acceptance."
**Where it left them:** In the guild town of X-salent, registered as "The Incredible Mistakes," with Reficul telling George about a deal with the devil over pints and Halcyra finally telling someone about the prophecy she's been carrying since she was fifteen.

## Session 8 — The Writing on the Wall
**Tagline:** A twelve-line poem about a creature has been appearing inside people's homes for twelve days, and the party splits between methodical investigation and Reficul accidentally killing four homeless people with an earthquake spell.
**Best moment:** Grungrak and Mira's reconciliation — she apologizes first, he tells her about meeting Grungrak in the River Lethe ("He was so kind. I see why he was your friend. It was weird"), and they end in a bear hug with Mira crying.
**Where it left them:** The poem is copied into a songbook, the creature is still in the woods, and next session is officially Prophecy Day.

## Session 9 — The House That Screamed
**Tagline:** Mira spends the day doing chemistry in a library while Reficul walks alone into a haunted Victorian manor and gets pinned to the floor by a mouse.
**Best moment:** The group prays to Apollo in the temple district, and the YOMI letters Halcyra has been carrying begin glowing with heat and light — and no one can agree whether that means the god answered or the candles were just warm.
**Where it left them:** Prophecy Day did not happen. It is 8 PM. The party is heading to Reficul's apartment. There is a creature in the woods.

## Session 10 — The Thing That Eats
**Tagline:** A morning card game over apology omelets gives way to a forest trail of melted candles and broken branches, and what waits at the end is a goop construct built to be a stomach.
**Best moment:** Reficul, mid-session, has been cursed into kobold form. The creature grabs Inquizo in its mouth and flees. Reficul — a tiny kobold — launches a mote of radiant light at the base of its skull. Both heads explode. Body becomes goop. It should not have worked. It absolutely worked.
**Where it left them:** The creature is dead, the missing merchant is confirmed dead inside it, and Halcyra and Inquizo both have dark shadow marks spreading from bite wounds that healing could only partially close.

## Session 11 — The Palace Assault
**Tagline:** The party returns to the kingdom that kidnapped them in Session 1, fights a king who deals in nightmares, and learns the name of the thing that is actually coming for them.
**Best moment:** George crit-fails his stealth landing in front of twenty guards. Grungrak immediately conjures an illusion of a second George, rolls Deception 24, and every single guard believes two Georges were supposed to be there.
**Where it left them:** King Pmurt escaped via contingency teleport — partially castrated, alive, and with a personal grudge — while Durf Numgis is already north, already working with whatever the Nightmares are, and the party finally knows his name.

## Session 12 — The Shadow Selves
**Tagline:** An owl named Gunj puts the entire party on a couch and shows each of them the version of themselves they chose not to become — and everyone cries.
**Best moment:** Mira's shadow grows into a blue dragon. The DM expected her to deny it. Instead, she walks into it — and finds Grungrak inside. She severs the cord. He becomes physical for one moment, embraces her, says "Thank you for letting me be me," and fades. The whole table cries.
**Where it left them:** Five people weeping on a couch, each holding something their shadow self left behind, with Gunj offering tissue boxes and the news that Numgis is working with the nightmare that sealed the God of Dreams.

## Session 13 — The Betrayers
**Tagline:** Reficul chooses to stay behind, George is caught practicing a secret song, and the airship they commandeered has a person hidden in the cargo hold who has been breathing for the engines.
**Best moment:** Halcyra wanders onto the airship early and finds George alone, quietly reading from a folded piece of paper — Mira's name repeating in a song he's been secretly learning for eight sessions. He shoves it in his pocket, goes completely red, and runs. He then spends an hour pacing in Pmurt's empty throne room trying to Calm Emotions on himself. He fails his first attempt.
**Where it left them:** Airborne on Pmurt's commandeered ship, flying north toward a mechanized city called Sypium, with a new passenger in the cargo hold, a second glass case they haven't opened yet, and Reficul standing in a city below, choosing to stay.

## Session 14 — The Red Sky
**Tagline:** The party finds a wizard dog living on the airship, solves a four-medallion navigation puzzle using sunlight, breath, and a pulley that hits Inquizo in the face, then arrives at their destination to find it actively on fire.
**Best moment:** Inquizo reaches the bottom of the ship, finds the grandfather clock spinning wildly out of control, grabs the hands to stop it — and the session ends. Nobody knows what that does.
**Where it left them:** On the burning grounds of Bermuda Foundation, Galveston's arm blown off but not confirmed dead (he walked off the ship when the red sky cleared), the Doctor presumably on his way back, and Inquizo holding a clock that was not supposed to be held.

## Session 15 — Stuart Returns
**Tagline:** The party opens a fog-filled locked study, frees a former judge trapped in a time loop, learns that the Doctor's expedition killed Millaphera's family, and then rides a mine train into a fire-breathing sheep dragon named Stuart.
**Best moment:** George wins three consecutive whiskey-bottle drinking contests (CON 19, 16, 16), wins the wrestling match via Cutting Words, earns 400 gold, and is described as "only slightly beginning to feel it." He then immediately throws up on a roller coaster.
**Where it left them:** Walking toward a mysterious winged woman's safehouse in the Doctor's district, with a 17-year-old caretaker in tow, a plastic canary toy, a magical set of pan pipes that summon people named Aspen, and the confirmed knowledge that grandfather clocks keep appearing everywhere and time keeps going wrong.

## Session 16 — The World Untrue
**Tagline:** The entire party falls asleep on the road, wakes up cuffed in a limousine wagon, throws a glowing clock off a bridge, turns the sky red, fights a stained glass dragon, and crashes into a capital city bathhouse where Gbot'n is eating a wet croissant.
**Best moment:** Inquizo throws the blue clock off the bridge (acrobatics 21). The sky goes red. The sun disappears. A stained glass dragon emerges from the tunnel wall. Two prophecy stanzas come true in real time. The party survives. The sky comes back. Nobody acknowledges how insane that was.
**Where it left them:** On a platform at the edge of Alastair — the capital of Errolon — staring at the Prophetess they've been looking for, a boy with burnt skin and growing claws who says "you can't take her from me," and a silver-armored knight raising a war hammer.

## Session 17 — Encounter with the Watchers
*The Watchers' Web*
**Tagline:** The party rescues the Prophetess, gondolas to a 300-foot clock tower where a sentient Machine offers everyone visions of who they could be, and Halcyra's Divine Smite destroys it — only to reveal a god named Oravis who tells her she died as a child and her father gave his life to bring her back.
**Best moment:** Mira touches the Machine and triggers the night's biggest vision — a giant scythe from a goddess called "the Lady," who turns to stone protecting her people, while a "secret third voice" says "It's a pity how things have to end... Good luck." Meanwhile George reads a music book, completely oblivious, while a god describes in detail how his past self ended two entire worlds with one hammer swing.
**Where it left them:** Back on their own empty airship, shaken, with Inquizo unconscious on the floor — put to sleep by an eight-foot Spider Goddess who just told Halcyra she has a "contract" with debts she doesn't remember signing, and handed Mira four new lines of prophecy with zero explanation.
