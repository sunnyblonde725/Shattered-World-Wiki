# DND Session 6 — The Eel That Sings

*Linked to: [[Shattered World]] | [[World State]] | [[Halcyra]] | [[Grungrak]] | [[Inquizo]] | [[Mira]] | [[Reficul]]*

---

## TL;DR

Picking up exactly where Session 5 ended — Reficul (Ian's character, who died in Session 3 and has been in hell) is alive and well in the Fields of Asphodel. The party reunites, escapes a turkey, gets on his boat, and immediately discovers the "anchor" is a metal horse with an axe who starts swinging at them. The boat crashes into the Shrine of Persephone. Grungrak, influenced by the sickle, cuts down a forest of dryads. Then a 12-foot goddess with a three-headed puppy arrives and is very unhappy about it. The party negotiates with **Persephone (Kori)**: she'll get them out of the underworld if they hand over the sickle and retrieve her niece Ariel's dead husband **Eric** from her soul pool. They find Eric, exit via elevator through the upper city, acquire a new boat guided by the Psychopomp (Thanatos) — and the session ends on a cliffhanger as **Urscylla** rises from the water on both sides of the boat. The sickle "Castrator of the Sky" is now **gone from the party**, given to Persephone. Grungrak ate pomegranate seeds in Persephone's forest. Reficul died many times this session and came back each time.

---

## Pre-Session Recap

Session 5 ended with the party arriving in the Fields of Asphodel, a wheat field in Hell under a stone sky with mechanical spotlights. A farmer named Reficul stood in the field, completely unfazed by the bisected cult members on the ground. He introduced himself. Mira tackled him the moment she recognized him. The session ended there, mid-reunion.

## Full Summary

### The Reunion in the Fields of Asphodel *(~11:00)*

Session 6 picks up immediately — Reficul is on the ground looking up at Mira, saying *"Well, I didn't realize you missed me that much."* Mira is on top of him. Grungrak has no idea who this man is.

The party has to explain: Grungrak, meet your old companion who has been in hell since Session 3. This goes poorly in the way all explanations to Grungrak go — Inquizo denies all knowledge, Mira tries her best, and Reficul handles the situation by being baffling. Grungrak eventually accepts this is just what happens now.

Reficul fills the party in. He stabbed himself in Session 3 and died — that's how he ended up in hell. He has been calling it a "vacation," which is the story for George's benefit so Grungrak/George doesn't have to deal with the reality of what happened. He found a map (highly inaccurate — makes a giant ocean look like a river). He's been exploring. He knows the exits: four gates. The one they came through (Gate of Dawn) is now broken, cleaved in half courtesy of Mira and the sickle. The Gate of Dusk requires passing through Tartarus, which Reficul declines firmly. Gate of Ivory and Gate of Horn are the viable options. The River Lethe ("forget water") runs through this area — do not fall in.

Spirits of the bisected cult members drift through the wheat field as green silhouettes, passing through the party. One turns and just stares directly at Mira. Apparently it takes one to five business days for underworld staff to process new arrivals who died down here rather than above.

Reficul has a boat. The plan is to get to it.

### The Turkey Situation *(~40:50)*

Between the party and the boat: bear traps and three cassowary-sized turkeys.

Grungrak nearly steps in a bear trap. Inquizo and the others guide him out using increasingly unhelpful instructions that devolve into the Cha Cha Slide. He makes it.

One of the turkeys locks onto Inquizo specifically. Inquizo casts **Invisibility** on himself (up to one hour). The turkey stops, looks around — then finds Grungrak instead. **Perception check: 15** — Inquizo determines the turkey is coming specifically for him. He does not immediately share this.

Mira, who grew up in farmland and has real context for how dangerous large birds are, starts running. She rolls **Deception (19)** to mask her fear. She fools no one. The party correctly notes there are two states a turkey exists in: murder and lust. This one is in murder mode.

Reficul produces the wheat seeds and holds them out. The turkey turns and eats directly from his hand. It calms completely. It is just a bird that wanted seeds.

The turkey has a handle on its back — a harness, like a carrying handle on a bag. Reficul grabs it and carries the turkey toward the boat. It is now the group's emotional support turkey.

### Getting on the Boat *(~45:06)*

The boat is floating roughly 15 feet from shore. There is a gangplank. Everyone gets on. Mira grabs onto Grungrak's shirt while navigating, which briefly causes a crisis because there appears to be an invisible entity clinging to him. Everyone gets aboard. Gangplank raised.

To test if the turkey can survive open water, someone puts it in. The turkey swims approximately five feet, eyes go blank, and it rolls. The party saves it with a net. The turkey is recovered, gets back on the boat, and is now their very confused mascot.

Reficul confirms: the anchor needs to come up. He didn't know how to raise it when he found the boat, so he threw things around until something fell. They need to find the winch. It's below deck.

### The Boat — Anchor Is a Horse *(~1:11:26)*

Grungrak goes below and finds what he assumes is the steering wheel. He grabs it and pulls with everything he has — **Strength: 24** — and successfully removes it from the floor. He removes the floor section attached to it. He also removes himself from the boat, arcing backward through the air toward the River Lethe. **Halcyra catches him (Athletics: 15)**, pulling him back before he hits the forget water. The platform and most of the wheel land in the river.

There is now a large hole in the deck.

Below that hole: the anchor system. What everyone assumed was a metal anchor is a **metal horse**, chained through a bandana collar around its neck. The horse also has an axe affixed to the bandana. The collar reads: `...` (dot, dot, dot — meaning: source. The horse is a grandson of Poseidon's horse — descended from a divine horse in Poseidon's bloodline).

The horse notices Grungrak. It grabs the axe in its teeth and pulls it free.

**Roll for initiative.**

**The Horse Fight:**
- Grungrak runs. The horse is faster. It's a horse.
- Inquizo (still invisible) attempts to sneak past the horse to lock the winch. **Stealth: 15.** Gets through. **Strength to lock the winch: 6** — too light. Tries again with everything he has. **Strength: 20** — pulls the lever so hard it pins him underneath it. He is now on the floor under a lever, invisible, next to a horse with an axe.
- Grungrak casts **Earth Tremor**. The table briefly debates whether boat decking counts as "ground." It does. Both the horse and Reficul are in the area. Both fail their Dexterity saves. Both fall prone and take 2d6 damage. *(~1:26:19)*
- Mira casts **Burning Hands** (15-foot cone toward the horse). **Horse Dex save: 13** (succeeds — half damage). Horse takes **5 fire damage**. Total damage on the horse: approximately 11. *(~1:39:19)*
- Halcyra shoots the horse with radiant damage. **Rolls a 1.** The radiant bolt bounces off the horse and hits Reficul squarely in the face. **7 radiant damage.** Reficul is described as looking very crispy.
- Reficul casts **Heat Metal** on the chain/collar. The collar is a polyester bandana. It heats and melts. The horse, now very warm, takes a slow breath, **sits down on the hole in the deck** (plugging it entirely with its body weight), closes its eyes, and goes to sleep. The flooding stops. The horse has napped the problem into submission. *(~1:43:53)*
- Everyone makes **Constitution saves** from the boat shaking (all pass, one nat 20). The horse remains asleep on the hole. Everything is fine.

### The Boat Crashes *(~1:54:53)*

Without a steering wheel and with only the Lethe's current pushing them, navigation is theoretical. Grungrak tries to use the rudder post where the wheel used to be. **Strength: 1.** The rudder locks on something. The boat rotates.

The boat drifts downstream and runs aground — directly into the **Shrine of Persephone**, which was marked on the map.

Everyone needs to get off before it fully lists:
- Most party members jump safely (Inquizo: 21, Halcyra: 18)
- Reficul: **Rolls a 1.** Does not get off in time. The boat flips over. The deck lands on him. He dies.

A small changeling child in white robes appears where Reficul was — his true form, revealed in death. Then the child also just... lies there, dead.

Then a **very tall, very naked man** erupts from the black sand next to the body. Approximately seven feet tall. Extremely well-endowed (the table notes this at length and at volume). He picks up the small changeling child and throws it into the river. He wraps the white robes around himself as a loincloth. His first words to Grungrak: *"I'm on vacation, baby."*

Grungrak is furious. He pulls out the sickle, walks away from the group and into the white forest at the edge of the beach, and starts cutting things.

The beach is **black sand**. The trees are **white** with **black leaves** and covered in **pomegranates**. Every tree the sickle touches begins to bleed red.

The turkey went down with the boat. The turkey is **properly dead**. Stewart the sheep made it off safely. The horse is on the beach — it fell from the sinking boat and is fine, because it is metal.

### The Pomegranate Forest *(~2:07:55)*

Grungrak moves through the white forest, angry, with the sickle whispering at him.

Each tree he tries to cut transforms — shifting into a **dryad or nymph** in the last second before contact. They scream and run for the building at the edge of the forest. The whole forest clears. The dryads are now in the building, and several are crying.

Pomegranates litter the ground everywhere. Grungrak sits down among them and **eats one like an apple**, squinting at the taste (the outside of a pomegranate rind is awful). He eats more. Nobody stops him.

The percentage die is rolled for the sickle's influence on Grungrak: **27%** — the sickle has control over him (below 50 is the sickle). The table notes he probably should not keep holding this weapon.


Mira nearly eats one, then remembers what she knows about mythology and puts it down: *"She's not that hungry."*

### Persephone Arrives (Kori) *(~2:29:24)*

A figure walks out of the building. She is **12 feet tall**. She has a three-headed dog with her — a puppy, proportional to her size, which makes it roughly Scooby-Doo-sized to a normal person. Her name is **Kori** (Persephone).

She has **Truesight.** She sees Inquizo immediately despite his Invisibility. She ensnares Grungrak with vines and drags him toward the building without preamble.

Inside, dryads hold protest signs that say "KILL HIM" and are crying. Some throw rocks at Grungrak. They are protesting his existence. He agrees he deserves this.

Mira explains: they are not dead, they ended up here by accident, they did not mean to clear the forest (the sickle was controlling him), and they would like to leave.

Grungrak tries: *"You never told me your name."*

Persephone, fighting back a smile: *"Keep calling me beautiful."*

Grungrak, who called her "really pretty vine lady" one minute ago, accepts this without hesitation: *"I will call you beautiful."*

### The Deal with Persephone *(~2:45:31)*

Persephone will get them out of the underworld under two conditions:

**1. Hand over the blade.**

The sickle — "Castrator of the Sky" / Vengeance — is given to Persephone. The percentage die had already established the sickle had control, not Grungrak. He gives it to Reficul to hand over so he doesn't have to touch it again. Persephone takes it without ceremony.

**The sickle is no longer in the party's possession.**

**2. Get Eric out of her pool.** *(~2:48:43)*

Persephone has a problem. Her **niece** (Ariel — the queen the party killed in Session 3) has been swimming through her soul pool looking for her husband. The husband — **Eric** — drowned trying to breathe underwater to make out with his wife. He has been in the pool. Ariel keeps looking for him. *(Note: Eric is a completely separate NPC from the party member George, who is the clay copy of Grungrak. They are not the same person and have no connection.)*

Persephone needs them separated. She needs Eric removed from her domain so Ariel stops swimming through her things. She cannot handle it herself for reasons she does not explain.

The deal: she gets the party out through the Gate of Ivory if they bring Eric up with them — and take him wherever he needs to go to find Ariel.

The party agrees. Reficul **swan dives into the soul pool** to retrieve Eric. *(~2:56:37)* He dies immediately (soul pool is dangerous). He comes back. He dies again. He comes back again, shaking his dice. Eventually — using a different set of dice — a **semi-corporeal attractive figure** surfaces and flops on the stone floor.

Persephone snaps her fingers. Eric's throat shifts. He can speak.

*"Hello. My name is George."* *(~2:59:19)*

Then, immediately: *"Where is Ariel?"*

The party navigates this information exchange badly. Someone tells him Ariel is dead. Eric then learns the party killed her. He is upset but has nowhere to direct it — he is a ghost, and frankly, she kind of stabbed herself first (the final blow is debated). Persephone tells him she doesn't know exactly where Ariel is, but she is "somewhere else." The plan becomes: go find her.

**Eric's confirmed skill:** He can run a sailboat *"all night long."* He demonstrates this by immediately knowing the correct nautical terminology for everything.


### The Upper City and the Elevator *(~3:08:36)*

Persephone snaps her fingers. The party is at the base of a massive elevator system ascending through the underworld's layers.

The horse gets in the elevator. The horse presses every button using the flat face of its axe. This takes some time. Grungrak asks Persephone (via some kind of divine connection) to turn off the elevator music so he can play his own. Persephone obliges — music appears. Grungrak plays.

**Grungrak composes "The Ballad of George"** on the way up — an adaptation of "The Ballad of Grungrak," retitled for Eric. Eric listens to a dwarf sing a song about him in an elevator in the underworld. He contributes the second verse. It is, by all accounts, a good tune.

At the top: a city. **Skyscrapers** in every direction. Trees wrapped around buildings. **Roads of asphalt** alongside **roads of flowing water** with boats navigating them (Venice-style, but vertical). This is the upper layer of the underworld — the civic level, above the rivers and the fields.

Persephone gives them a new, more accurate map. The Gate of Ivory is nearby, accessible via the water roads.

### The Psychopomp (Thanatos) *(~3:17:20)*

At the top of the water road, near the gate: a **very tall cloaked figure with antlers**, drowsy, barely acknowledging their existence. He makes the exact sound from the Hades video game. The party explains Persephone sent them. He gets in the boat. The boat sinks slightly under his weight. He begins to guide them toward the Gate of Ivory.

Eric is on board. The horse is on board. Stuart the sheep is on board. Reficul is on board (he found a chest containing his own clothes inside Persephone's shrine — someone put them there).

### Urscylla — Session Cliffhanger *(~3:20:14)*

The boat moves along the water road toward the gate.

On the left side of the boat: **three forms surface from the water**. On the right side: **three more**. Eel-like heads. Then one tips upward and reveals what is under the waterline — not a body. A **massive tentacle**. All six are the same. Eel heads above the water, tentacles below.

Urscylla. Six heads. Six tentacles. On both sides of the boat simultaneously.

**Roll for initiative.**

Session ends. The party had to leave for dinner.

## Lore & Worldbuilding Discoveries

- **The underworld has layers.** The Fields of Asphodel are one layer. Above them: an upper civic level with skyscrapers, water roads (boat traffic), and asphalt streets. An elevator connects the levels. The Gate of Ivory is in the upper city.

- **The four gates of the underworld:** Gate of Ivory, Gate of Horn, Gate of Dusk (requires Tartarus — strongly avoided), and the Gate of Dawn (now broken, courtesy of Mira and the sickle in Session 5).

- **The River Lethe ("forget water") flows through the underworld.** Fall in and you forget everything. Grungrak nearly landed in it when the steering wheel came out.

- **Pomegranate seeds in Persephone's domain bind you to the underworld.** Persephone herself ate six seeds and is bound here for six months a year. **Grungrak ate multiple pomegranate seeds** in her forest. The DM knows. No one told the characters what this means yet.

- **The soul pool** in Persephone's shrine is a swirling mass of souls (not water). Semi-corporeal dead can be recovered from it, but the process is dangerous and kills Reficul twice in the attempt.

- **The Psychopomp (Thanatos)** is accessible in the upper city. He is very tall, cloaked, antlered, and perpetually drowsy. He can guide the dead (or near-dead, or not-dead) to the appropriate gate.

- **Ariel's husband Eric** drowned trying to breathe underwater to be with Ariel. He is semi-corporeal. He can run a sailboat flawlessly and is now traveling with the party to find her. *(Note: Eric is a completely separate character from the party member George — Grungrak's clay copy. They are different people with no connection.)*

- **Ariel is Persephone's niece** in some genealogical sense. Persephone referred to the situation as "my niece brought a problem." The exact family connection is unclear.

- **The metal horse (grandson of Poseidon's horse)** is now traveling with the party. Its collar reads "..." meaning "source" — it is descended from a divine horse in Poseidon's bloodline. It does not need air to breathe. It has an axe. It presses buttons with the flat of its axe blade. It is currently on the new boat.

- **The sickle "Castrator of the Sky" / Vengeance is gone.** Given to Persephone as payment. This ends the sickle's influence on Grungrak but also removes a major artifact from the party.

- **Urscylla guards the river near the Gate of Ivory.** Six eel-heads above the waterline. Six tentacles below. Surfaces on both sides of the boat simultaneously. The session ended at initiative.
