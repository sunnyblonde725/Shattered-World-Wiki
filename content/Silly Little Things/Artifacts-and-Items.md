# Artifacts & Items

*Notable objects across the campaign — things with history, weight, or unanswered questions.*

---

## The Sickle — "Castrator of the Sky" / Vengeance

**Who has it:** Persephone (given away in Session 6)
**First appeared:** Session 4
**What it is:** A sentient artifact that is literally a piece of the goddess Cybele/Avani herself — her severed rage and grief made physical. It introduced itself: *"I am Vengeance. Against the sky. Against love loss."*
**What we know:** It was originally wielded by Arcus, who descended into Hell with it and didn't come back. The party found it in the underground temple. It responded to children of Cybele (Arcus and Lorya raised their hands). Grungrak carried it for several sessions. It was given to Persephone as payment for passage out of the underworld.
**What we don't know:** What Persephone intends to do with a sentient artifact made of divine vengeance. Whether its influence on Grungrak left any trace. Whether it has its own agenda that outlasts whoever holds it.
**Status:** With Persephone — no longer in party's possession

## The Black Orb — Unknown Entity Imprisoned

**Who has it:** Inquizo (in his backpack)
**First appeared:** Session 4
**What it is:** A black orb containing an imprisoned entity of unknown identity. The entity speaks in riddles and contradictions and described its prison as "a terrible box without a blankie." It has been inside "forever" and was given immortality — meaning it cannot die, and therefore cannot leave by dying. **The entity is NOT Caelus — Ian confirmed this.** The true identity is still unknown.
**What we know:** The entity is imprisoned, immortal, and riddle-speaking. A Celestial inscription on the orb's context reads: "Agents of Kaelas, entrap the minds of the divorced." The blood angel called its father "K.L.I.S." / "Kaelas" — possibly connected to the orb's creator or the entity inside. Inquizo has been carrying it wrapped in spare clothes like a dangerous egg.
**What we don't know:** Who the entity in the orb actually is. Whether Kaelas and K.L.I.S. are the same being. What releasing it would do. How it connects to Caelus (the sky deity, blood angel's father) if at all.
**Status:** In Inquizo's backpack

## The Nails — Blood Angel Artifacts

These three items came off (or out of) the blood angel the party fought in Hell. They are grouped because they all came from the same entity and may share significance.

### Arcus's Nail Image

**Who has it:** Inquizo
**First appeared:** Session 4–5
**What it is:** An image associated with the nail the blood angel drove into Arcus's chest — it depicted a person cutting their own stomach open.
**What we know:** The nail itself caused Arcus to become enraptured (the angel whispered "Bina" to him). The imagery is violent and body-focused.
**What we don't know:** What "Bina" means or who it refers to. Whether the image is prophetic, symbolic, or a record. Whether the nail is still inside Arcus.
**Status:** Held by Inquizo

### Lorya's Serpent-Circle Jewel

**Who has it:** Inquizo
**First appeared:** Session 5 (Lorya's nail pull scene)
**What it is:** A jewel that was connected to Lorya — taken during the blood angel encounter. Its design involves a serpent forming a circle.
**What we know:** Inquizo took it. In Arcus's nail image, a serpent appears staring directly at Inquizo — the same serpent has appeared in multiple visions. Lorya and Arcus are children of Cybele/Avani.
**What we don't know:** Whether the jewel is connected to Lorya's divine parentage, the serpent that keeps appearing, or something else entirely. Whether it was *meant* for Inquizo.
**Status:** Held by Inquizo

### The Spike from the Blood Cocoon

**Who has it:** Inquizo
**First appeared:** Session 5
**What it is:** A spike pulled from the blood cocoon during the nail extraction sequence — a dagger image is frozen inside the crystal of it.
**What we know:** It came from the blood angel's environment. A dagger frozen in crystal is not subtle imagery.
**What we don't know:** Whether the frozen image is literally a weapon waiting to be used, or metaphorical, or a record of something that already happened.
**Status:** Held by Inquizo

## The Jade/Emerald Necklace — Inquizo's Lost Item

**Who has it:** Urscylla (given away in Session 7 while charmed)
**First appeared:** Inquizo's inventory before Session 7
**What it is:** A necklace depicting a snake eating itself — or possibly a bird, or a bird eating itself. The exact image was noted as uncertain.
**What we know:** Inquizo gave it to Urscylla while charmed by her aria. Urscylla smiled when she received it. Eels don't smile — the DM noted this specifically. The design may connect to the serpent that keeps appearing across multiple visions.
**What we don't know:** Where Inquizo got it. Whether it was significant to him personally or just loot. Why Urscylla smiled. Whether its loss has consequences given the serpent's pattern of watching Inquizo.
**Status:** With Urscylla — lost to the party (Urscylla was pinned under a boulder, not confirmed dead)

## The Bow of Dusk

**Who has it:** Halcyra
**First appeared:** Session 12 (received from the old man at her paladin oath)
**What it is:** A +2 longbow that deals radiant damage only — no piercing. Always rolls at least 12 to hit. A weapon built specifically for someone on the path from darkness to light.
**What we know:** An old man gave it to Halcyra when she took her paladin oath. He said: *"You just gotta get through the night first."* A dawn version exists — the bow she will become when she has grown through whatever is ahead of her. It is currently the dusk version.
**What we don't know:** Who the old man was and how he knew to give it to her. Whether the bow changes form on its own or whether "becoming the dawn version" is metaphorical. Whether the bow is connected to Apollo.
**Status:** Halcyra's possession — currently equipped

## The Lightning Bolt Necklace — Mira's Father's Gift

**Who has it:** Mira (wearing it)
**First appeared:** Session 12 (passed through the shadow dream by her father's spirit)
**What it is:** A lightning bolt necklace — her father's final gift, passed through the shadow dream. Adds 2d6 lightning damage to any attack (physical or magical) up to proficiency bonus times per day.
**What we know:** Her father sold his soul when Mira was five to save her life — *My soul for hers.* The dragon patron who has been speaking through her and manifesting the sickle has held that contract. The necklace was his last gift to her. She put it on immediately upon waking.
**What we don't know:** Where her father is now — dead? In hell? Held by the dragon? Whether the necklace carries any of his presence, or whether it is simply what it is: a weapon made of grief and lightning.
**Status:** Mira's possession — wearing it

## The Prophecy Scroll

**Who has it:** Halcyra
**First appeared:** Referenced as early as Session 7 (pulled out in the tavern); Halcyra has had it since age 15
**What it is:** A scroll containing a multi-stanza prophecy that centers, without naming her, on Halcyra — the child of Apollo, the one meant to fuse the fractured worlds.
**What we know:** Multiple stanzas speak directly to her situation. The cost of the world's repair will require her to give something irreplaceable. She has been searching for the "chosen hero" described in it — not knowing that hero is herself. She shared it with Mira privately in Session 8. The full-party reading ("Prophecy Day") has been derailed twice and still hasn't happened.
**What we don't know:** Who gave it to her at 15. Whether she has fully read it. Whether she's connected it to herself yet.
**Status:** Halcyra's possession — physically in her pocket

## The Two-Sided Necklace — George's Dream Item

**Who has it:** Grungrak/George
**First appeared:** Session 12 (received at the end of shadow therapy)
**What it is:** A spinning necklace with two faces — one side shows an olive branch (Necklace of Tranquility), the other a forte music symbol (Power of Courage). He holds the deal and chooses which face to use.
**What we know:** It was given to him at the end of the shadow dream sequence, when the real Grungrak emerged from the dragon and embraced Mira. George woke up smiling through tears.
**What we don't know:** The mechanical specifics of how each side works. Whether the choice between tranquility and courage reflects something about George's ongoing identity question.
**Status:** George's possession

## The Chains of Eternity

**Who has it:** Inquizo (shackle bracelets on both wrists)
**First appeared:** Session 12 (shadow therapy item)
**What it is:** Shackle bracelets. Up to proficiency bonus times, he can launch the chains to restrain a target — but he is also restrained at the same time. They share the same chains, Marley-style.
**What we know:** His shadow — the Warden Brother — dissipated into these chains. They are a physical manifestation of the thing that holds him down, and also a weapon. He cannot be free of them without also losing the ability to restrain something else.
**What we don't know:** Whether using them advances or suppresses his shadow work. Whether they have any connection to the sister or the brother his shadow showed him.
**Status:** Inquizo's possession — worn on both wrists

## The Shadow Tattoo — Reficul's Left Arm

**Who has it:** Reficul
**First appeared:** Session 12 (shadow therapy; the First and Last Witness merged with him)
**What it is:** A pulsating dark mark on his left arm. Grants the ability to summon another creature's shadow physically — living targets within 30 ft (he becomes the shadow and deals psychic damage); for deceased people, he can project their appearance through Whispers-style mechanics.
**What we know:** His shadow chose to integrate rather than fight him — a rare outcome. The shadow had been looking for a deity who could change form. Reficul may now have access, through the tattoo, to information his shadow was gathering.
**What we don't know:** What exactly the shadow learned about the campaign's divine landscape. Whether the tattoo's abilities will grow. What "a deity who changes form" refers to.
**Status:** Reficul's — permanently part of him

## Pmurt's Crown

**Who has it:** Inquizo ("My special crown")
**First appeared:** Session 13 (found in airship cargo)
**What it is:** A tall red velvet crown — the original crown Pmurt gave the Doctor in exchange for Starglass and the airship. Tricks the ship's AI into recognizing whoever wears it as King Pmurt.
**What we know:** Inquizo bumped the push-to-start button while wearing it. The ship AI said "Hello, King Pmurt" and accepted his voiced command to go to Sypium. The crown is now directing an airship in the hands of a kobold who won't read labels.
**What we don't know:** Whether there are other ship systems that respond to the crown. Whether Pmurt will try to recover it.
**Status:** Inquizo's possession — currently wearing it

## The Skull Mask — "dr.l"

**Who has it:** Inquizo (found in Session 13; he won't read the label)
**First appeared:** Session 13 (found in the airship cargo among hats)
**What it is:** A metal skull-face mask labeled "dr.l" on the inside. It belongs to the Doctor — now confirmed as **Dr. Alastair Morgenstern Larkin** (Session 14; found in his travel log aboard the ship). "dr.l" is his initial: L for Larkin.
**What we know:** Inquizo found it and kept it. He has declined to read the label — meaning he does not yet know the name that everyone else now has. The Doctor is described as "a different man underneath his skin" — a shapeshifter, or possessed, or something stranger. His mask being on the ship suggests he left in a hurry, or left it deliberately, or it was taken from him. Galveston called him "the head luminary."
**What we don't know:** What the Doctor's face looks like without it. Whether the mask has any function beyond identity. Whether Inquizo reading the label would change anything — and at this point, whether it matters that he still hasn't.
**Status:** Inquizo's possession

## The Sending Stones — Party Communication

**Who has them:** Mira and Gunj (matched pair, given Session 13); Halcyra received one separately
**First appeared:** Session 13 (given by Valu/Aeolus the wind god)
**What it is:** Paired stones that allow long-distance communication across the fractured world.
**What we know:** Valu gave them as he briefly manifested. Mira used hers to contact Gunj and ask him to travel to the island city to support Reficul.
**What we don't know:** Range limitations in a shattered world. Whether they work across world-boundaries.
**Status:** In use — distributed between Mira (with party on airship) and Gunj (heading to island city)

## The Thorin Statue

**Who has it:** On the airship (the party loaded it, George carried it up two flights of stairs)
**First appeared:** Referenced among Pmurt's kingdom's statues; moved to airship Session 13
**What it is:** One of four statues from King Pmurt's original kingdom, each containing or representing a god. Thorin's people are likely near Sypium. The statue shed a single tear at the island city — it is not in the right place.
**What we know:** The mechanic is confirmed: bring a statue to its people and the god inside can breathe (Valu manifested when his statue reached the kenku). Mira and Halcyra believe getting Thorin to his people will vitalize him. It is made of different materials than the Valu statue — different makers.
**What we don't know:** Who Thorin's people are. Whether they are connected to the cave civilization near Sypium. What Thorin's domain is.
**Status:** On the airship — heading north

## Mira's Pact Weapon — The Sickle

**Who has it:** Mira
**First appeared:** Session 11 (manifested during the King Pmurt fight without her consciously calling it)
**What it is:** A warlock pact weapon that manifested as a sickle — the shape chosen by Mira's patron, a blue dragon with green veins who holds her father's soul contract.
**What we know:** It appeared on its own. Mira used it to partially castrate King Pmurt. The patron has been active and present the entire campaign; the sickle is the clearest physical evidence yet. Mira did not choose the sickle's shape — her patron did. The last notable sickle in this campaign was the Castrator of the Sky, now with Persephone.
**What we don't know:** Whether the sickle's shape (matching Vengeance) is a coincidence, a message, or a claim. Whether Mira can dismiss it or whether the patron can manifest it without her consent again.
**Status:** Mira's pact weapon — can be summoned or dismissed

## The Second Glass Case — Opened

**Who has it:** The creature was released; the case remains on the airship
**First appeared:** Session 13 (found); Session 14 (opened)
**What it is:** A glass case from the airship's cargo hold, under a tapestry depicting a sun, rays, wings, and sheep. It contained a burned humanoid entity — many eyes, wires growing from it, capable of communicating by spelling words with its wires.
**What we know:** Halcyra negotiated with the creature: she offered it sunlight (walked it to a window and let it bask) in exchange for the sun medallion needed for the navigation puzzle. The creature spelled out "Obviously" with its wires when asked if that was all it needed. It gave the sun pendant. It remains aboard the ship.
**What we don't know:** What the creature is. Whether it is related to Starglass. Why it was in a case with Apollo-adjacent imagery. What it wants beyond sunlight.
**Status:** Creature released and present on the airship; sun medallion given to the party

## Pages 35–40 from "Runes from Ancient Ages"

**Who has it:** Halcyra (checked out from the X-salent city library, Session 9; Mira read them first)
**First appeared:** Session 9
**What it is:** Six pages from a library book confirming that the marked ruins in the region are purely magical in origin, linked to a deity — whom the author deliberately declined to name, noting two possible candidates but refusing to choose.
**What we know:** The ruins are not architectural or geological. They are divine. The mystery substance (the goop), the writing, and the constructs are all connected to this unnamed deity — who is almost certainly Malafera, now that Session 13 has revealed her.
**What we don't know:** Who the two candidates the author considered were. Whether naming the deity in the text itself matters.
**Status:** Halcyra's possession

## The Navigation Medallions — Sun, Moon, Star

**Who has them:** Placed into the navigation console on Pmurt's airship (three of four)
**First appeared:** Session 14
**What they are:** Four medallions corresponding to celestial symbols — sun, moon, star, and gear — that fit into the ship's navigation console. The navigation puzzle was discovered in Session 14 aboard Pmurt's airship.
**What we know:** The sun medallion came from the second glass case creature (Session 14). The moon medallion was obtained from the breath-lock mechanism (Mira breathed through it and rolled 18 vs DC 17). The star medallion was embedded in the sail rigging — Inquizo pulled the pin, the pulley swung, it hit George in the face, and the medallion fell loose. Three of four placed.
**What we don't know:** Where the gear medallion is. Whether placing all four activates something beyond navigation. Whether the Doctor knows the party has been using them.
**Status:** Sun, moon, star — placed in console. Gear medallion — missing.
